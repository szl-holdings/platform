## Tier 1: Core Category Statement

PRISM Counsel is a **litigation command system** for plaintiff-side insurance defense firms operating in New York.

It is not a chatbot. It is not a case management clone. It is the command layer above everything else — turning fragmented signals into governed legal action and outcome intelligence.

---

## Tier 2: Product Thesis (one paragraph)

Plaintiff-side litigation teams manage dozens of matters across carriers, courts, adjusters, and deadlines. Critical signals are buried in email, scattered across documents, and tracked in spreadsheets. PRISM Counsel makes every signal visible, every deadline tracked, every action governed — with a proof chain for everything that moves and Microsoft 365 as the native delivery surface.

## Tier 3: Module Messaging

### Matter Desk
> Your complete matter command surface.

All active matters in a single view. PRISM health scores, settlement forecasts, deadline pressure, and insurer behavior — organized by priority, not docket order.

### NY Practice
> Insurance defense and no-fault command for New York.

NY DFS Regulation 68 compliance clocks. No-fault claim tracking. Insurer silence window detection. Arbitration readiness scoring. Built for the rules and rhythms of NY plaintiff-side litigation.

### Prep / Review / Sign-Off
> From demand assembly to partner approval — governed.

Three-stage workflow: Prep assembles and checks the packet, Review flags contradictions and low-confidence AI extractions, Sign-Off records partner approval before any document moves. Human-in-the-loop is structural.

### Section 31
> The intelligence engine under every surface.

14-domain matter twin. Worldline signal assembly. Multi-dimensional pressure graph. Forecast diff tracking. SHA-256 proof chain for every AI output. The deep intelligence layer that makes everything else possible.

## Tier 4: Trust & Governance Messaging

> Governance built for legal accountability, not as an afterthought.

Six controls:
1. **Human approval on every consequential action** — demand letters, settlement positions, compliance filings. Structural, not optional.
2. **Source-grounded AI** — every recommendation tied to a specific document, record, or data feed.
3. **Privilege-aware architecture** — attorney-client protections encoded at the data layer.
4. **Immutable proof chain** — SHA-256 hashed, timestamped, actor-attributed, exportable.
5. **Role-based access with audit** — partner, associate, paralegal, and admin roles with full audit logging.
6. **Hallucination detection** — contradiction and confidence scoring before AI outputs reach any review queue.

## Tonal Guidelines

- **Restrained** — No exclamation points. No "revolutionary" or "game-changing" language.
- **Specific** — Name the regulation (Regulation 68). Name the jurisdiction (New York). Name the workflow (Review Before Send).
- **Serious** — This is legal matter management. Lives, liability, and livelihoods depend on getting it right.
- **Direct** — Short sentences. Active voice. No AI buzzword filler.
- **Confident** — Assert claims without hedging. "PRISM Counsel is not a chatbot" not "PRISM Counsel may differ from chatbot-style tools."
