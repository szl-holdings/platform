# PRISM Counsel — Positioning Strategy

## Core Thesis

PRISM Counsel is a **litigation command system** — not a chatbot, not a case management clone, and not a generic AI tool for lawyers.

It sits above the tools law firms already use (case management, email, documents, Microsoft 365) and turns fragmented matter signals into governed legal action and outcome intelligence.

## Primary Positioning

**For plaintiff-side insurance defense litigation firms that manage high-volume NY matters, PRISM Counsel is the matter intelligence and governance layer that makes every signal visible, every deadline tracked, every action proofable — without replacing the systems you already use.**

## The Market Problem We Solve

Plaintiff-side insurance litigation is a coordination and intelligence problem. Most firms solve it with:
- Email for communication
- Spreadsheets for deadline tracking
- Shared drives for document management
- Manual review for demand readiness

The consequences:
- Deadlines missed because they were buried in email threads
- Demand packets going out incomplete because no checklist enforcement existed
- Insurer patterns going unrecognized because no behavioral intelligence was being assembled
- Partner time wasted on manual review that AI-assisted systems could handle in seconds
- No audit trail when clients or regulators ask what happened and why

PRISM Counsel is the command layer that makes all of this visible, governed, and defensible.

## The Insurance Defense Wedge

We start with NY insurance litigation — specifically:
- Plaintiff-side auto, no-fault, bodily injury, premises liability
- New York Insurance Regulation 68 (11 NYCRR Part 65)
- PIP/no-fault claim clock tracking
- Arbitration packet readiness
- NYSCEF and court scheduling intelligence

This is not an accident. NY insurance litigation has:
1. **Explicit regulatory compliance requirements** (Regulation 68 creates objective deadline violations that are measurable and consequential)
2. **High matter volume** (firms managing 50-300+ active matters need infrastructure to manage signal volume)
3. **Insurer behavioral patterns** that repeat and are learnable
4. **Strong demand for defensible AI use** given malpractice exposure and discovery obligations

The wedge creates a reference customer class before expanding to adjacent practice areas.

### Level 4: Objection Handling

**"We already have case management software."**
PRISM Counsel is not case management. It is a command layer above case management — connecting signals from your existing system and giving you intelligence and governance that case management cannot provide.

**"We're skeptical of AI making legal decisions."**
PRISM Counsel does not make legal decisions. Human approval is required on every consequential action — demand letters, settlement recommendations, compliance filings. The AI surfaces signals; attorneys and partners decide. The proof chain records every decision.

**"We don't trust AI with privileged information."**
Privilege-aware architecture is a first-class constraint. Privileged materials are tagged at ingestion. AI models are never trained on privileged content. Export pipelines screen and redact automatically. The proof chain records what the AI accessed.

**"Generic AI tools haven't worked for us."**
PRISM Counsel is not generic. It is built for your practice area: NY insurance litigation, Regulation 68, no-fault claim clocks, insurer behavior intelligence, NYSCEF integration. The intelligence is matter-specific, not general-purpose.
