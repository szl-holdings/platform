## What it does

PRISM Counsel was the full legal command surface for the SZL Holdings platform. It provided end-to-end matter management with court filing integration, multi-jurisdictional compliance support, recovery tracking, and Guardian-approved approval chains — all governed by the same Proof Chain infrastructure that runs every SZL Holdings product.

127 components. Multi-jurisdictional. Cross-domain intelligence fusion from defense (Aegis) and maritime (Vessels) verticals.

## Feature Highlights

- **Matter Center** — Active legal matters with exposure scoring and resolution tracking
- **Exposure Graph** — Cross-portfolio legal risk visualization by entity, counterparty, and jurisdiction
- **Contract Monitor** — Contract obligations, deadlines, and renewal triggers with automated alert escalation
- **Policy Gating** — Counsel-required actions enforced through Guardian approval queue with Covenant Policy
- **Audit Export** — Immutable legal event logs for regulatory use, generated by Proof Chain
