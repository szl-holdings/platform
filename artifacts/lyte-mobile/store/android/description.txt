Lyte — AIOps Command Center

AI-powered infrastructure operations for SREs, DevOps, and platform engineers. Lyte delivers intelligent observability, anomaly detection, and incident management for teams running modern cloud-native infrastructure.

KEY FEATURES:

🤖 AI Anomaly Detection — ML engine surfaces meaningful anomalies with root cause hypotheses before they become outages.

📊 Unified Observability — Metrics, logs, and traces in a single operational view. Drill from health summary to service traces in seconds.

🚨 Intelligent Incident Management — Correlates affected services, suggests remediation steps, and coordinates response — from your phone.

🔕 Smart Alerting — Noise-reduced alerts that learn your patterns. Critical pages get through; false positives get suppressed.

🗺️ Topology Maps — Visual service dependency maps show blast radius instantly before users are impacted.

📟 On-Call Ready — Native PagerDuty and OpsGenie integration. Acknowledge, escalate, and resolve without leaving the app.

Built for teams running Kubernetes, cloud-native, and hybrid infrastructure at scale. Requires organizational account.
