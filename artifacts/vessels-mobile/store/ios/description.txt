Vessels — Fleet Command Mobile

Maritime fleet intelligence and command for shipping operators, port authorities, and maritime logistics professionals. Vessels delivers real-time AIS tracking, vessel operations management, and port intelligence from anywhere in the world.

REAL-TIME AIS TRACKING
Live positions of your entire fleet on an interactive maritime chart. Vessel speed, heading, ETA calculations, and route deviation alerts — all updating in real time.

PORT INTELLIGENCE
Live berth availability, port congestion indexes, weather conditions, and customs status for ports across your operational network.

VESSEL OPERATIONS
Access vessel specifications, crew manifests, cargo information, and voyage documentation from any device. Critical operational data when you need it, wherever you are.

ROUTE MONITORING
Active voyage tracking with waypoint alerts, weather overlays, and NAVTEX integration. Know when vessels deviate from planned routes before problems escalate.

INCIDENT ALERTING
Push notifications for vessel arrivals, departures, delays, and safety incidents. Stay informed on critical fleet events 24/7.

CHARTER & CARGO MANAGEMENT
Track charter agreements, cargo manifests, and loading/discharge operations in a unified timeline for each vessel.

Built for maritime operators, fleet managers, port agents, and logistics professionals who need reliable fleet intelligence at sea and ashore.
