Vessels — Fleet Command Mobile

Maritime fleet intelligence for shipping operators, port authorities, and logistics professionals. Real-time AIS tracking, vessel operations, and port intelligence from anywhere.

KEY FEATURES:

🚢 Live AIS Tracking — Real-time fleet positions on interactive maritime charts. Speed, heading, ETA, and deviation alerts.

⚓ Port Intelligence — Berth availability, port congestion, weather, and customs status across your operational network.

📋 Vessel Operations — Specifications, crew manifests, cargo information, and voyage documents accessible anywhere.

🗺️ Route Monitoring — Active voyage tracking with waypoint alerts and weather overlays. Catch deviations early.

🔔 Fleet Alerts — Push notifications for arrivals, departures, delays, and safety incidents around the clock.

📦 Charter & Cargo — Charter agreements, cargo manifests, and loading/discharge timelines per vessel.

Built for maritime operators, fleet managers, port agents, and logistics professionals. Requires organizational account.
