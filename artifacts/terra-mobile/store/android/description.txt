Terra — Real Estate Intelligence

Professional real estate intelligence for acquisition teams and investors who move fast on distressed properties and off-market opportunities. Terra puts deal-winning data in the field.

KEY FEATURES:

🏚️ Distress Intelligence — Live feed of distressed properties, pre-foreclosures, and off-market opportunities filtered to your criteria.

📸 Field Intelligence — Photograph, geo-tag, and annotate properties in the field. Notes sync instantly to your deal team.

📋 Acquisition Pipeline — Full deal lifecycle from first look to closing, tracked and organized across devices.

📊 Market Analytics — Neighborhood pricing trends, distress rates, absorption data, and comp transactions for faster underwriting.

🔔 Watchlist Alerts — Price and status triggers on specific properties. Get notified the moment a watched property moves.

👥 Team Coordination — Shared deal rooms with notes, tasks, and property data for aligned acquisition teams.

Built for professional real estate investors, acquisition professionals, and asset managers in competitive markets. Requires organizational account.
