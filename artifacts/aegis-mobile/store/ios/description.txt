Aegis — Unified SOC Command Center

Aegis brings enterprise-grade Security Operations Center (SOC) command directly to your iPhone. Built for cybersecurity professionals and security teams who need real-time threat intelligence and incident response at their fingertips.

REAL-TIME THREAT MONITORING
Stay ahead of threats with live feeds of security incidents, alerts, and anomalies across your entire infrastructure. Color-coded severity levels let you triage at a glance.

INCIDENT RESPONSE
Create, assign, and track security incidents from detection to resolution. Built-in runbooks and response playbooks keep your team coordinated during active breaches.

INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD
Correlated threat intelligence from multiple sources surfaces the signals that matter. ATT&CK framework mapping puts adversary TTPs in context.

SECURE BIOMETRIC ACCESS
Face ID and Touch ID protect access to sensitive operational data. All communications are encrypted in transit and at rest.

TEAM COORDINATION
Push alerts route critical events to the right analysts instantly. Escalation workflows ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

COMPLIANCE & AUDIT
Audit trails and evidence collection built into every incident workflow. Export reports in formats accepted by major compliance frameworks.

Aegis is designed for security professionals at enterprises, MSSPs, and government agencies who demand the highest standards of operational security.
