## 10-Minute Track: The Investor Hook

**Goal:** Category + two signature innovations. Enough to get a second meeting.

**Script:**

**[0:00–2:00] — The category (Command overview)**
> "SZL is not an AI dashboard company. We're a governed operational intelligence company. Here's what that means."
> 
> Open: `/command/strategy`  
> Point to the nine-step loop: Signal → Context → Recommendation → Simulation → Policy → Execution → Proof → Outcome → Learning  
> "Every consequential decision across every domain flows through this loop. Human-in-the-loop is enforced by code, not by convention."

**[2:00–6:00] — Decision Twin (Lyte)**
> "Here's our first signature innovation. Meet Decision Twin."
>
> Open: `/lyte/decision-twin`  
> Select: Vantex Acquisition approval (the stalled $4.2M decision)  
> Click: Simulate → Delay  
> Show: Downstream impact on revenue SLA, staffing, vendor penalties  
> Show: Confidence bands, assumptions panel  
> "This isn't a what-if calculator. This is governance-grade simulation with a full audit trail. Every decision your team makes, every assumption they baked in — permanently attributed and retrievable."

**[6:00–10:00] — Policy Compiler (Alloy)**
> "Here's our second signature innovation. Meet Policy Compiler."
>
> Open: `/command/operations/alloy/policy-compiler`  
> Type: "Any spend above $50,000 requires CFO approval unless emergency override is present"  
> Click: Compile  
> Show: Structured policy object generated — conditions, effect, required approver  
> Show: Validation warnings, version number, rollback button  
> "Your operators write the rule in plain English. Alloy compiles it into executable policy with full governance rails. That's the category."

## Objection Handling

**"This looks like a lot of dashboards."**
> "The dashboards are the evidence that the governance is working. The product is the Policy Compiler, the Decision Twin, and the Proof Chain — the dashboards just make it legible. Palantir users would recognize this pattern."

**"Why not just use Palantir?"**
> "Palantir is an ontology company. We're a governed signal-to-action company. They model the world; we govern decisions about it. We're also 1/100th the implementation cost."

**"Is this AI or software?"**
> "It's governed AI. Every AI recommendation has evidence, confidence, and an approval model. We preserve human judgment for consequential actions. This is the difference between AI that's risky and AI that's enterprise-deployable."

**"Can you do [specific vertical]?"**
> "We can. Terra, Aegis, Vessels, and Carlota Jo are domain packs on the same operating system. Adding a domain pack takes weeks, not years. The platform primitives — policy, proof, simulation, audit — are already there."
