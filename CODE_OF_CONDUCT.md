# Code of Conduct

> [Platform Repo](https://github.com/szl-holdings/szl-holdings-platform) | [Security](./SECURITY.md) | [Contributing](./CONTRIBUTING.md) | [Contact](https://szlholdings.com)

## Our Pledge

SZL Holdings is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone involved with our platform — whether as a team member, evaluator, design partner, enterprise customer, or security researcher.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, professional, and inclusive environment.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Demonstrating professionalism, empathy, and respect in all interactions
- Providing and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Prioritizing the security and privacy of users and data
- Respecting confidentiality of proprietary information and trade secrets

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Publishing private or proprietary information without authorization
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by SZL Holdings, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Communication channels (email, Slack, etc.)
- Public representations of the organization
- Interactions with evaluators, partners, and customers

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to **[conduct@szlholdings.com](mailto:conduct@szlholdings.com)**.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially. SZL Holdings reserves the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and other interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.
