## About Page (Extended)

### What SZL Holdings builds

SZL Holdings is a strategic holding company operating six purpose-built platforms under one compounding architecture:

**Lyte** — Business observability. Surfaces execution risk, ownership drift, and workflow friction before they compound.

**Vessels** — Maritime intelligence. Fleet command, AIS analytics, compliance monitoring for global maritime operators.

**Aegis** — Unified defense & intelligence. Threat correlation, SOC workflows, incident governance, and analyst tradecraft tools.

**Terra** — Real estate intelligence. Market intelligence, deal pipeline, and portfolio analytics for institutional real estate.

**PRISM Counsel** — Legal matter command. Deadline tracking, pressure scoring, privilege-safe document handling.

**Carlota Jo** — Private advisory. Operational management for UHNW household environments.

All six platforms run on **Alloy** — the shared execution fabric that handles approval routing, audit trails, and governed automation.
