## Trust Section

### Section Header
GOVERNANCE IS NOT OPTIONAL

### Trust Points
- **Human-in-the-loop**: Every recommendation requires explicit human approval before execution.
- **Evidence chain**: Signal → Policy → Execution → Proof. Full audit trail on every decision.
- **Sandboxed outputs**: All generated artifacts are restricted to designated directories with atomic writes.
- **Path redaction**: Absolute paths are automatically redacted from all exports and manifests.
- **File-mode restrictions**: Generated artifacts are locked to owner-read-only (0600) by default.
