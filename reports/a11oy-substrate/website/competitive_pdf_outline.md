## 3. Competitive Advantages (2 pages)

### 3.1 The Twin Model
- Every vertical creates a Digital Twin (not just a dashboard)
- Twins ingest signals, not reports
- Twins produce recommendations, not visualizations

### 3.2 Evidence Chain
- Signal → Policy → Execution → Proof
- Audit-grade provenance on every decision
- No competitor offers this end-to-end

### 3.3 Human-in-the-Loop
- Every recommendation requires explicit approval
- Rollback paths documented in every recommendation
- Confidence scores visible to the decision-maker

### 3.4 Vertical Reuse
- Shared substrate across 7 domains
- New verticals deploy on the same architecture
- Reduces marginal cost of entering new domains

## 4. Vertical-by-Vertical Differentiation (3 pages)

### 4.1 Pulse vs. Executive Dashboards
- Dashboards show metrics; Pulse shows *decisions*
- Signal pressure scoring vs. static KPIs

### 4.2 Fincept vs. FP&A Tools
- Traditional FP&A is backward-looking
- Fincept produces "Capital Weather" — forward-looking, signal-driven

### 4.3 KORA vs. Project Management
- PM tools track tasks; KORA tracks *decision debt*
- Approval chain optimization is unique to KORA

### 4.4 Terra vs. PropTech
- PropTech focuses on listings; Terra focuses on *diligence risk*
- Environmental + FEMA integration is rare

### 4.5 Sextant vs. Maritime Software
- AIS tracking is commodity; sanctions screening + route risk is not
- Claims risk placeholder unique to Sextant model

### 4.6 PRISM Counsel vs. Legal Tech
- Legal tech focuses on document management
- PRISM focuses on *matter risk* and deadline pressure

### 4.7 Growth Engine vs. Marketing Automation
- Marketing automation executes; Growth Engine *proves*
- Proof-to-pipeline loop converts customer success into demand
