## Messaging Discipline

**Lead with governance, not AI.** Every competitor leads with AI. The differentiation is the governance layer — covenant policy, approval gates, proof chain — that makes AI safe to use in high-consequence operations.

**Lead with the platform, not the features.** The 8 domain verticals are proof of the platform's breadth. The platform thesis (A11oy) is the investment case.

**Do not claim "production" for anything that isn't.** Alpha status is honest and credible at growth capital. "Production" claims that are wrong destroy credibility in diligence.

---

## What Investors Should Take Away from a 30-Minute Demo

1. The accountability gap is real — enterprise AI adds noise, not governance
2. A11oy solves it structurally — not policy, but architecture
3. The platform already works — 13 surfaces, live data in most, full auth
4. The team can execute — not just designed; built, shipped, and running
5. The moat is the proof chain and covenant policy — extremely hard to retrofit
