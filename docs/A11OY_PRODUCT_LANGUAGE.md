# A11OY_PRODUCT_LANGUAGE.md — Product Language and Tone

This document defines the approved product language for A11oy and the SZL Holdings platform. All product copy, documentation, pitch materials, and agent-generated content must conform to these rules.

---

## Approved One-Liner

> **A11oy is the Live Enterprise Execution Fabric — the governed agentic layer that transforms business signals into governed actions with cryptographic proof.**

Use this line in investor presentations, one-pagers, and top-level README summaries.

---

## Expanded Description

A11oy senses business signals across seven enterprise verticals, structures them into causal context, correlates them across domain boundaries, explains the reasoning chain, recommends governed actions with confidence scores, routes for the appropriate human approval, executes with full policy enforcement, and preserves cryptographic proof of every decision — in real time.

It is not a dashboard. It is not a copilot. It is not a workflow tool. It is the execution fabric that sits between signal detection and action outcome.

## Tone Rules

1. **Precise over vague.** State what the product does structurally. Avoid superlatives without evidence.
2. **Qualified over overreached.** When in doubt about a claim's accuracy, add the approved qualifier or remove the claim.
3. **Operator-centric.** Write from the perspective of what the operator can do, decide, and trust. Not from the perspective of what the AI does on its own.
4. **Proof-forward.** Reference the Proof Chain, Proof Ledger, and Proof Packet when describing trust and auditability.
5. **Vertical-aware.** When discussing domain capabilities, name the specific vertical. Avoid generic "enterprise" language where specificity is possible.
6. **No vendor allusions.** Do not describe A11oy using language that evokes specific competitor products, even approvingly.
7. **Active prototype framing.** In investor materials and public documentation, frame Phase 1 as an active prototype and demo platform. Do not imply production scale that has not been achieved.
