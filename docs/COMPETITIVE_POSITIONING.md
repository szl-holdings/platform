## Category: Governed Decision Operating System

SZL occupies a unique position at the intersection of decision intelligence, AI governance, and multi-domain enterprise operations.

## Landscape

### Tier 1: Platform Companies

| Company | Strengths | SZL Differentiation |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Palantir** | Ontology-first data model, massive government contracts, AIP for GenAI | SZL is decision-lifecycle-first, not ontology-first. Open governance primitives vs. closed ecosystem. Proof chain + decision replay have no Palantir equivalent. |
| **Anduril** | Real-time mesh networking, autonomous systems, defense focus | SZL targets enterprise governance across commercial domains. Shared approval gates and policy engine are governance-native, not defense-native. |
| **Dataiku** | ML lifecycle governance, visual flow, strong analyst tooling | Dataiku governs models. SZL governs decisions. Dataiku has no cross-domain signal cascade, no proof chain, no decision replay. |

### Tier 2: AI Governance

| Company | Strengths | SZL Differentiation |

## SZL's Unique Position

Based on publicly available documentation and product analysis (April 2026), SZL's combination of these six properties is not replicated by any identified competitor:

1. **Decision lifecycle as the primitive** — not data models, not ML pipelines
2. **Cross-domain signal cascading** — maritime ↔ real estate ↔ legal ↔ security ↔ operations
3. **Immutable proof chain** — hash-linked evidence for every consequential action
4. **Full decision replay** — reconstruct any decision from trace
5. **Policy-governed AI** — Covenant Policy Engine enforces risk-tier rules before execution
6. **Probabilistic simulation** — Monte Carlo engine runs before recommendations

## Strategic Wedge

SZL enters through **multi-domain holding companies and conglomerates** — organizations that:
- Operate across 3+ business verticals
- Face cross-domain regulatory obligations
- Need governed AI across diverse operations
- Cannot adopt Palantir's closed ecosystem
- Require evidence-backed decision audit trails

## Moat Compounding

Each new domain added to the platform:
- Inherits all existing governance primitives
- Creates new cross-domain signal pathways
- Increases proof chain coverage
- Expands evaluation scenario library
- Deepens the competitive gap
