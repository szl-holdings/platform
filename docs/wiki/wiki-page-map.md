## Content Brief Per Page

### Home
Lead with: What this repo is, who SZL Holdings is, the ecosystem overview with product summaries. Ecosystem diagram reference. Quick links to key wiki sections. One-paragraph platform thesis.

### Platform Overview
Lead with: What the platform does at a product level. Lyte + Alloy as the core. Domain packs as the extensions. Product status table. Architecture summary. Link to Architecture page for depth.

### Architecture
Lead with: The signal-to-action pipeline diagram. Layer breakdown (signal ingestion, normalization, context engine, routing, execution, audit). Stack enumeration. Infrastructure topology. Link to in-repo architecture docs.

### Deployment Model
Lead with: Where the platform runs (Replit development, Azure production). Deployment options table. Infrastructure components. Operational requirements. Contact path for enterprise deployment inquiries.

### Security Posture
Lead with: Security is structural, not a compliance add-on. Auth model (OIDC/PKCE). Authorization (RBAC). Data in transit/at rest. AI governance enforcement. Audit trail design. Disclosure process.

### Trust Center
Lead with: What trust means in an AI-assisted operations platform. Human-in-the-loop enforcement. Evidence-backed decisions. Audit trail. Compliance templates (SOC 2, HIPAA, financial services). Contact for trust assessments.

### Screenshots and Demos
Lead with: What you'll see. Lyte command dashboard. Alloy workflow view. Aegis SOC command. Vessels fleet map. Terra property intelligence. Screenshot captions with context. Demo access request link.

### Buyer Use Cases
Lead with: What problem does each buyer role have. Map to platform capability. ROI framing. Reference to `docs/buyer/use-cases.md` for full detail. Contact path.

### Investor Overview
Lead with: Market thesis. Why now. Product differentiation. Platform portfolio. Readiness stage. Capital use. Link to full investor docs.

### FAQ
Lead with: Common questions from technical reviewers, buyers, investors. No softball questions — address real friction points directly.

### Roadmap
Lead with: Current stage. Near-term milestones (Phase 2). Medium-term (Phase 3). Long-term vision. No fake dates — use quarters or "next" framing.

### Glossary
Lead with: Platform-specific terminology. PRISM, Alloy, signal, Helmsman, Sentinel, Compass, dark activity, etc. Brief, precise definitions.
