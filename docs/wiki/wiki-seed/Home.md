SZL Holdings builds **governed decision infrastructure software**. This wiki is the extended reference layer for the platform repository. For the executive overview, see the [README](https://github.com/stephenlutar2-hash/szl-holdings-platform#readme).

---

## Platform Thesis

Business observability must connect to action, not just visualization. AI outputs without traceability create noise, not trust. Every decision should have a signal, a routing path, an approval gate, and an audit trail.

**Lyte** surfaces the signal. **Alloy** governs the action. Domain packs extend the same architecture into security, maritime, and real estate.

## Products

### Lyte — Business Observability
The command surface for operators who need to see risk, bottlenecks, ownership gaps, and next actions in one place. PRISM framework: People, Revenue, Infrastructure, Security, Market. Signal timeline, correlation engine, priority action queue, execution accountability.

**Status:** Functional alpha. Active development.

### Alloy — Execution Fabric
Signal normalization, workflow orchestration, approval controls, human-in-the-loop gates, and immutable audit trail. The governance layer beneath every AI-assisted operation.

**Status:** Functional alpha. Integrated with Lyte.

### Aegis — Security & Defense Intelligence
Unified defense platform consolidating SOC, managed operations, and intelligence engine. SOAR playbook engine, STIX/TAXII protocol support, MITRE ATT&CK v14 mapping.

**Status:** Functional alpha. StateRAMP readiness track in Phase 2 roadmap.

### Vessels — Maritime Intelligence
Fleet command, AIS tracking, route anomaly detection, sanctions screening, voyage economics, and dark activity monitoring.

**Status:** Functional alpha.

### Terra — Real Estate Intelligence
Distress signal detection, ownership analysis, underwriting workflows, broker scorecards, and deal pipeline management.

**Status:** Functional alpha.

### Carlota Jo — Premium Advisory
White-glove advisory operations for UHNW residential clients. Private intake, service lanes, client portal, structured engagement workflows.

**Status:** Live — accepting clients.
