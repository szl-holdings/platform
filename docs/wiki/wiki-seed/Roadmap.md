# Roadmap

The SZL Holdings platform roadmap is organized around three development phases. Dates are not published — phases advance on quality and capability gates, not calendar pressure.

---

## Current Stage — Functional Alpha

All major platform products are at functional alpha. Core architecture, authentication, data layer, and UI surfaces are complete. Active development focus is on production readiness, revenue activation, and compliance infrastructure.

---

## Phase 2 — Production Readiness & Revenue Activation

**Focus:** Move from functional alpha to production-deployed, revenue-active status.

**Focus:** SOC 2 certification, enterprise onboarding infrastructure, partner integrations, and platform expansion.

| Milestone | Notes |

---

## Long-Term Vision

The platform is designed to become the standard governance layer for AI-assisted enterprise operations — the Alloy execution fabric as infrastructure, with Lyte and domain packs as the command surfaces for each vertical.

Long-term expansions under evaluation:
- Additional domain packs (legal operations, financial services operations, government/defense)
- Edge deployment for latency-sensitive operations
- Federated multi-org coordination

---

## What Is Not on the Roadmap

- Consumer-facing products
- Social features
- No-code / low-code general automation (distinct from Alloy's governed execution)
