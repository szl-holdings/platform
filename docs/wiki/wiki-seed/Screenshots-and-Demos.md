### Lyte — Business Observability

![Lyte Overview](../../docs/media/screenshots/lyte-overview.jpg)

Lyte command surface — PRISM framework dashboard with signal timeline, priority action queue, and ownership map. Dark-first command-center aesthetic.

**What to look for:**
- Signal severity classification and routing
- PRISM dimension breakdown (People, Revenue, Infrastructure, Security, Market)
- Priority action queue with ownership attribution

**What to look for:**
- Human-in-the-loop approval interface
- Audit trail with actor attribution
- Workflow routing visualization
