# Investor Overview

SZL Holdings is building the governed decision infrastructure layer for enterprises where AI-assisted decisions require human accountability. The platform is at functional alpha stage across all major products.

---

## Thesis

Business observability tools show what happened. They do not tell operators what to do next, who is accountable, or whether the recommended action is approved to execute. AI tools add recommendation volume without adding governance. The result is more data, more noise, and more untracked decisions.

SZL Holdings addresses the signal-to-action gap: connecting what's observable to what's executable, under governance, with full attribution.

---

## Market Position

The platform operates at the intersection of three categories:

| Category | Established Players | SZL Differentiation |
|----------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Business intelligence | Tableau, Power BI, Looker | Action-oriented, not just visualization |
| Workflow automation | ServiceNow, Monday.com | AI-native, governance-first |
| AI operations | Various LLM wrappers | Governed execution, evidence-backed, audit-first |

The core differentiator is the Alloy execution fabric: workflow orchestration and approval enforcement that makes AI-assisted operations durable and accountable at enterprise scale.

## Why Now

Three converging forces create the window:

1. **Enterprise AI adoption is accelerating** — buyers are realizing that AI outputs without governance create liability, not leverage
2. **Observability tooling is maturing** — the market understands the category; differentiation now is in action and accountability, not just visibility
3. **Regulatory pressure on AI** — EU AI Act, SEC AI disclosure requirements, and sector-specific rules are creating demand for auditable AI operations tooling
