## Overview

The ROI case for the SZL Holdings platform is built on four quantifiable categories: decision cycle compression, triage time reduction, audit and compliance overhead reduction, and missed follow-up elimination. These are not claims about what customers have achieved — they are modeled ranges based on industry benchmarks and the documented capabilities of the platform.

---

## The Four ROI Vectors

### 1. Decision Cycle Compression

**The problem:** In organisations managing multiple operational domains (security, logistics, real estate, legal), decision cycles are long because signal collection, context-building, and recommendation are all manual processes. Executives and operations leads spend a substantial portion of their time assembling information before they can act.

**What the platform does:** Lyte's PRISM framework aggregates signals across domains into a single, contextualised view. The Alloy execution fabric routes recommendations to the right actor with full context attached. Decision-ready intelligence replaces raw data.

**Modeled impact range:** Operational decision cycles compress by **30–60%** in early deployments when replacing fragmented dashboard-and-spreadsheet workflows. This range is based on industry benchmarks for business intelligence and operational intelligence platform deployments (Gartner, IDC, Forrester 2023–2025 research on decision latency). SZL does not claim to have measured this figure in production deployments.

**Translating to value:** A portfolio operations team spending 20 hours/week on manual signal synthesis at $120/hour fully-loaded cost = $12,480/month in synthesis cost alone. A 40% reduction = ~$5,000/month saved per team, or ~$60,000/year — before any improvement in decision quality.

---

### 2. Triage Time Reduction

**The problem:** Security, maritime, legal, and real estate operations all share the same triage burden: when a signal arrives, someone must determine whether it is actionable, assign it, and route it to the right person with the right context. This process is frequently manual, error-prone, and slow.

**What the platform does:** Domain packs (Sentra, Vessels, Terra, Counsel) surface signals pre-classified by severity, type, and recommended action. AI advisory agents (Sentinel, Helmsman, Compass) perform first-pass triage and generate ranked recommendations. Human operators review and confirm, not investigate from scratch.

**Modeled impact range:** First-pass triage time reduced by **40–70%** compared to unaided analyst workflows. Source: Forrester Wave for Security Intelligence (2024) reports median 45% triage acceleration from AI-assisted SOC platforms; maritime and real estate benchmarks suggest similar ranges for structured signal classification.

**Translating to value:** A security operations team handling 200 alerts/day at 15 minutes per alert = 50 hours/day in triage. A 50% reduction = 25 hours/day recovered. At $90/hour blended analyst cost = $2,250/day, or ~$585,000/year. Even at 10% of that impact: $58,500/year per SOC deployment.

---

### 3. Audit and Compliance Overhead Reduction

**The problem:** Regulated industries (financial services, legal, maritime, healthcare-adjacent) spend significant engineering and operations time assembling audit evidence. Evidence collection is typically retroactive, manual, and unreliable.

**What the platform does:** The Proof Chain is an immutable, append-only audit trail attached to every significant action across the platform. Every AI recommendation, human decision, workflow execution, and configuration change generates an attributed, timestamped, queryable event. Audit evidence is created as a byproduct of normal operation — not assembled retroactively.

**Modeled impact range:** Audit evidence assembly time reduced by **50–80%**. Source: Deloitte (2024) benchmarks on automated audit trail systems vs. manual evidence collection; Protiviti survey data on compliance function efficiency. The platform's Proof Chain architecture directly targets this category.

**Translating to value:** A compliance team spending 120 hours/quarter on audit evidence assembly at $150/hour = $72,000/year. A 60% reduction = $43,200/year in compliance labor recovered. At scale across multiple domains, this number compounds.

---

### 4. Missed Follow-Up Elimination

**The problem:** In workflow-intensive operations (legal matter management, maritime voyage management, deal pipelines, security incident management), follow-ups fall through the gaps between systems. CRMs, ticketing tools, email, and spreadsheets are not coordinated. Things get missed.

**What the platform does:** Alloy's workflow orchestration layer tracks every pending action, routes follow-up assignments to the responsible actor, and escalates overdue items. The Outcome Graph maintains the full lifecycle of every action — from recommendation to resolution. Nothing is declared complete until the outcome is recorded.

**Modeled impact range:** Follow-up miss rate reduced by **60–85%** compared to uncoordinated multi-tool workflows. This figure is modeled based on workflow automation platform benchmarks (Salesforce, ServiceNow, and ProcessMaker ROI studies 2022–2024). The platform's human-in-the-loop confirmation model creates a structural accountability mechanism that uncoordinated tools cannot replicate.

**Translating to value (legal):** A legal matter management team with 5% follow-up miss rate on 400 matters/year = 20 missed follow-ups. At average $5,000 in administrative cost per missed follow-up (rescheduling, penalties, lost opportunity) = $100,000/year in avoidable cost. An 80% reduction = $80,000/year saved.

Modeled efficiency gains of 30–80% across decision, triage, audit, and follow-up workflows translate to $200,000–$800,000/year in recovered labor and avoided cost at mid-market scale.
