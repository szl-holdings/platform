**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Stephen Lutar

---

## Purpose

GitHub Stars at SZL Holdings are not casual bookmarks. They are a deliberate research and benchmarking layer — a living index of the external ecosystem that informs product decisions, architectural choices, and competitive positioning across the SZL platform portfolio.

A star means: *this repo is worth tracking, referencing, or learning from.* Nothing more. Nothing less.

---

## Guiding Principles

**1. Stars are structured input, not acknowledgment.**
Starring a repo is not appreciation or social signal. It is an act of curation. Every repo starred should have a reason.

**2. Quality over velocity.**
500 well-organized stars beat 2,000 random ones. When in doubt, don't star.

**3. Stars serve the products.**
Everything curated should connect back to Lyte, Alloy, Aegis, Vessels, Terra, Carlota Jo, or the SZL infrastructure layer. Tangential repos do not earn a star.

**4. Lists are mandatory for intent.**
If a starred repo is not assigned to a list, it has no strategic value. Stars without lists are noise.

**5. Regular pruning is part of the system.**
Repos become stale. Frameworks die. Projects get abandoned. The collection must be actively maintained.

---

## What a Star Represents

A starred repo must meet at least one of these criteria:

- **Reference implementation** — shows how a real system solves a hard problem
- **Architectural benchmark** — demonstrates structural patterns worth adopting
- **Competitive signal** — reveals what the market is building and how
- **Production-grade tooling** — actively used in real systems, not demos
- **Design/UX inspiration** — raises the quality bar for product or documentation

---

## What a Star Does Not Represent

- Appreciation or support for a project
- Intent to contribute or fork
- Agreement with design choices
- A repo worth reading in full

---

## Star Count Target

| Range | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| < 50 | Getting started — too sparse for signal |
| 50–150 | Early signal — useful but not yet comprehensive |
| 150–350 | Operational — meaningful research layer active |
| 350–600 | Mature — strong benchmarking coverage |
| > 600 | Review for pruning — volume may exceed quality |

**Target operating range: 200–400 curated stars.**

## Inputs to the Star Queue

Stars should come from:

1. **Active research sessions** — when solving a specific problem on a product
2. **Competitor analysis** — reviewing category leaders, funding announcements
3. **Technical decision moments** — selecting a library, evaluating a pattern
4. **Weekly scan** — brief review of trending repos in relevant domains
5. **Reference in documentation** — when a repo is cited in an article worth tracking

Stars should not come from:

- Trending page browsing without a specific reason
- Following other users' star collections
- Automatic bookmarking of tools tried once
- Social reciprocity
