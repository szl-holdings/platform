# What Ouroboros Is NOT

This page exists because "black holes + Tesla + AI runtime" sounds like a crank thesis. It is not a crank thesis. The math is public domain, the citations are real, the tests pass. To earn the right to be read, we lead with what we are explicitly not claiming.

## We do not claim AGI or emergence

Ouroboros is a runtime envelope. It measures whether an AI system is leaking, wasting, or lying. It says nothing about whether the system is intelligent, conscious, or general. A perfectly clean Ouroboros loop can still wrap a profoundly limited model. A failing Ouroboros loop can still wrap a useful one. Cleanliness, horizon, and resonance are necessary conditions for trustworthy deployment. They are not sufficient conditions for intelligence.

## We do not claim consciousness

The Kuramoto coherence primitive says coupled agents can synchronize. It does not say synchronized agents are conscious. The literature on integrated information theory and global workspace models is interesting and adjacent and not what we are doing. We measure observable order parameters, not subjective experience.

## We do not claim over-unity, free energy, or wireless power miracles

We use Tesla-coil math because Tesla-coil math is the cleanest available formalism for resonance, impedance, and Q-factor. We do not endorse, derive from, or imply over-unity. The Resonance Handoff Theorem is bounded above by the Landauer thermal ceiling. Any handoff above that ceiling is rejected by the runtime — in practice, this means the runtime will refuse couplings that claim free energy, the same way it refuses safety alarms on mean aggregations.

## We do not claim the simulation hypothesis

The black-hole thermodynamics primitives (Page curve, holographic bound, no-cloning, Hawking rate, witness anchor) come from real physics literature: Page 1993, 't Hooft 1993, Susskind 1995, Wootters & Zurek 1982. We use the math for what it provably is: a bound on information release. We do not extend it metaphysically.

## We do not claim a theory of mind, soul, past lives, or destiny

Sometimes the work feels older than the moment. That feeling is well-documented in cross-domain synthesis going back to Poincaré. The runtime makes no metaphysical claims about it. It is a runtime.

## We do not claim novelty where novelty does not exist

- Page curves: Page, 1993.
- Holographic bound: 't Hooft 1993, Susskind 1995.
- No-cloning: Wootters & Zurek, 1982.
- Landauer limit: Landauer, 1961.
- Kuramoto model: Kuramoto, 1984.
- Impedance matching: Pozar, *Microwave Engineering*.
- AKOrN (oscillatory neurons): Miyato/Löwe/Geiger/Welling, ICLR 2025.

The novelty in Ouroboros is the **synthesis** — bringing these primitives together as ten measurable governance signals for AI runtimes. The individual primitives are public domain or properly cited.

## We do not claim safety by ourselves

A clean Ouroboros loop is a necessary precondition for safe deployment in regulated verticals. It is not sufficient. Safety also requires policy, red-teaming, human review, threat modeling, privacy hygiene, and operational discipline. Ouroboros is one layer of a stack.

## We do not claim production readiness for every deployment

- v0.1 of the unified payload is suitable for development, evaluation, and regulated-vertical pilots.
- v1.0 will require: formal verification of the two theorems, signed-release pipeline, third-party security audit, production OTel collector, and at least three logo customers.

## What we do claim

The runtime measures ten observable primitives. The tests pass (256/256). The citations are real. The synthesis is novel-but-grounded. The thesis is falsifiable on every primitive. That is what we claim. Nothing more, nothing less.
