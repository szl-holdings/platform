# sentra — Flagship Makeover: Inspiration & Leaders Studied

**Tab leaders looked up, then adapted into our own idiom.** Principles adopted, not assets.

## Leaders studied for sentra (assurance / continuous-verification layer)

### Anthropic — constitutional assurance & transparency
sentra is our assurance / continuous-verification flagship, so Anthropic's published safety and
transparency practices are the primary leader. We studied the idea that assurance is a *living,
continuously-applied* property rather than a one-time certificate.

- Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback:
  https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback
- Anthropic transparency hub: https://www.anthropic.com/transparency
- Claude's constitution (continuously-applied governing rules):
  https://www.anthropic.com/constitution

**What we made our own:** a *breathing assurance pulse* — assurance presented as a live,
continuously-beating signal in the hero, rather than a static "compliant" badge.

### Anduril — Lattice accountability surface
For the operational density and accountability framing we studied Lattice.

- Lattice architecture & accountability:
  https://www.faf.ae/home/2026/4/21/andurils-lattice-platform-architecture-accountability-and-the-future-of-autonomous-warfare-in-american-defense-strategy
- Lattice walkthrough: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpFFScTovII

**What we made our own:** the dense, operational metric strip tied to our LOCKED doctrine numbers
and 100%-green-route posture.

## Synthesis for sentra
Anthropic continuous-assurance + Anduril accountability density, rendered as a breathing
assurance pulse over the wire-mesh canvas (Live Wires lineage, no Three.js / no CDN), in Kanchay
tokens.
