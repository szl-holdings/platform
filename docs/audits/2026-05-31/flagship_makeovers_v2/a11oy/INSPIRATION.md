# a11oy — Flagship Makeover: Inspiration & Leaders Studied

**Tab leaders looked up, then adapted into our own idiom.** Every external pattern below is cited
with a primary URL. We adopted *principles*, not assets — fonts, color, and motion are all
Kanchay-native (open-source: Inter / IBM Plex / JetBrains Mono).

## Leaders studied for a11oy (governance / constitutional layer)

### Anthropic — constitutional transparency
a11oy is our constitutional-governance flagship, so Anthropic is the natural leader to study.
We took the public-constitution-as-product idea: the governing document is visible, versioned,
and diffable rather than hidden.

- Claude's Constitution (public governing document): https://www.anthropic.com/constitution
- Claude's new constitution (versioning the governing rules in the open):
  https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-new-constitution
- Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback (the underlying method):
  https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback
- Anthropic transparency hub: https://www.anthropic.com/transparency

**What we made our own:** a *live constitution diff* in the hero — the governing rule-set is
surfaced above the fold as a versioned, inspectable artifact, in our gold/teal/navy idiom rather
than Anthropic's warm-paper look.

### Anduril — Lattice open-architecture C2
For the systems-aesthetic (dense, operational, accountable) we studied Anduril's Lattice.

- Lattice platform architecture, accountability & autonomy:
  https://www.faf.ae/home/2026/4/21/andurils-lattice-platform-architecture-accountability-and-the-future-of-autonomous-warfare-in-american-defense-strategy
- Lattice walkthrough (open architecture, mesh data layer, human-final-authority):
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpFFScTovII
- Scale context — $20B U.S. Army contract:
  https://orangeslices.ai/anduril-awarded-a20b-us-army-contract/

**What we made our own:** the dense metric strip and "human retains final authority" framing —
rendered with our LOCKED doctrine numbers (13-axis reasoning, 749 decls, 100% green routes)
rather than sensor-to-shooter telemetry.

## Synthesis for a11oy
Anduril's operational density + Anthropic's visible-constitution transparency, expressed in
Kanchay tokens with an animated wire-mesh canvas hero (Live Wires lineage, no Three.js / no CDN).
