# amaru — Flagship Makeover: Inspiration & Leaders Studied

**Tab leaders looked up, then adapted into our own idiom.** Principles adopted, not assets.
Fonts/color/motion are Kanchay-native (Inter / IBM Plex / JetBrains Mono, all open-source).

## Leaders studied for amaru (threat / signal-fusion layer)

### Anduril — Lattice mesh & sensor fusion
amaru is our threat-intelligence / signal-fusion flagship, so Anduril's Lattice is the primary
leader. We studied the decentralized data layer (Lattice Mesh), sensor-to-shooter latency framing,
and the doctrine that a human retains final authority over autonomous action.

- Lattice architecture, accountability & autonomy:
  https://www.faf.ae/home/2026/4/21/andurils-lattice-platform-architecture-accountability-and-the-future-of-autonomous-warfare-in-american-defense-strategy
- Lattice walkthrough (open architecture, mesh data layer, provenance tagging):
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpFFScTovII
- Scale context — $20B U.S. Army contract:
  https://orangeslices.ai/anduril-awarded-a20b-us-army-contract/

**What we made our own:** an *animated threat timeline* — fused signals arriving over time on a
single accountable surface, expressed with our doctrine numbers and Kanchay color rather than
defense telemetry.

### Anthropic — transparency & provenance
For the "every fused signal is explainable and traceable" framing we studied Anthropic's
transparency posture.

- Anthropic transparency hub: https://www.anthropic.com/transparency
- Constitutional AI method: https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback

**What we made our own:** data-provenance tagging on every timeline event (where a signal came
from, when, and under which doctrine version), surfaced in the dense hero metric strip.

## Synthesis for amaru
Lattice mesh fusion + Anthropic-style provenance/transparency, rendered as an animated,
time-ordered threat surface in Kanchay tokens with the wire-mesh canvas (Live Wires lineage,
no Three.js / no CDN).
