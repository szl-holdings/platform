## 1. Headline framing

SZL sits at the intersection of four converging markets: **military/defense AI**, **drone & counter-UAS autonomy**, **sovereign AI infrastructure**, and **govtech/defense IT**. We do not claim the whole of any one — we claim the *verifiable-governance* slice across them.

| Layer | SZL definition | Size | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | All AI applied to defense/military + sovereign-AI infrastructure + defense IT modernization, globally | **~$200B blended by 2030** (see build-up below); broader sovereign-cloud envelope reaches **$482B by 2032** | sources §2 |
| **SAM** | U.S. + Five-Eyes military AI software/services + critical-infrastructure + `.gov` governance & assurance spend that a verifiable-receipt layer can attach to | **~$15–25B** by 2030 | §3 |
| **SOM** | Realistic 3–5-year capture as an early-stage governance-substrate vendor | **$150M–$1B cumulative** | §4 |

> **Honesty flag on the directive's numbers.** The Series-A brief proposed "TAM $500B, SAM $50B." We could not source a $500B *defense-AI* TAM from CSIS/Brookings/IDC; the defensible, sourced defense-AI figures are an order of magnitude smaller (military-AI market ~$19B by 2030 per Grand View; defense IT ~$149B by 2030 per The Business Research Co.). $500B is only reachable if you fold in the entire sovereign-*cloud* envelope ($482B by 2032). We present the sourced build-up below and let the partner choose the framing; we will not assert $500B as a defense-AI TAM because no primary source supports it.

## 3. SAM — serviceable (U.S. + Five Eyes + critical infra + .gov)

- North America held **32.8%** of the military-AI market in 2024; software was **42.5%** of the market ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/artificial-intelligence-military-market-report)). Applying these to the 2030 figures yields a U.S. military-AI-software base of roughly **$2.7B**, growing.
- Defense IT in North America reaches **$60B by 2030** (USA alone **$54B**) ([National Law Review](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/defense-it-spending-market-2026-2030-unveiling-growth-developments-latest)); the AI/data-analytics/cybersecurity slice is the SZL-serviceable portion.
- Adding Five-Eyes military AI, U.S. critical-infrastructure assurance, and `.gov` AI-governance mandates, SZL's serviceable market is **~$15–25B by 2030**. The U.S. Army's single ceiling-$10B/10-yr enterprise AI contract precedent ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/news/2026/05/31/ai-is-the-brain-of-war-global-defense-ai-market-to-surge-to)) shows individual program scale.
