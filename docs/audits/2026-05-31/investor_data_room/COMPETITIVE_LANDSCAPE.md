## 4. Where SZL is genuinely differentiated (and where we are NOT)

**Differentiated:**
1. **Formal verification** — a Lean-stated governance gate (749 declarations, honest 163 sorries) is unique in this set. No competitor publishes machine-checkable proofs of their gate.
2. **Tamper-evident Body of Evidence** — a Merkle-DAG receipt chain that re-derives offline (replay hash `bacf5443…`), Lean-proved (TH11). The "verify our logs, don't trust them" posture.
3. **Oversight-only positioning** — Killinchu never fires; it watches, refuses, and proves. This is the exact Cannonico Warhacker problem.
4. **Model-agnostic routing** — a11oy.code routes any open/frontier LLM and receipts "which model decided what."

**NOT differentiated / honest weaknesses:**
- We have **no revenue, no contracts, no ATO**. Every competitor above is far ahead commercially.
- Our **web-edge security is weak** (wildcard CORS, no auth on public Spaces) — disclosed in `TECHNOLOGY_DUE_DILIGENCE_PACKET.md`.
- The headline **Λ-uniqueness is a Conjecture, not a Theorem**; SLSA is **L1, not L3**; 5/6 UDS bundles unsigned.

## 5. Strategic posture

SZL is a **picks-and-shovels assurance layer**, not a platform challenger. The most credible outcomes are (a) becoming the governance/BoE standard layer adopted across UDS Core and allied stacks, or (b) acquisition by a platform (Anduril/Shield AI/Palantir class) needing provable governance to clear ATO faster.
