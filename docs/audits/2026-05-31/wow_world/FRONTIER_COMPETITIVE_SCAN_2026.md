## Part C — White-space synthesis

The entire frontier — labs and defense — competes on **two axes: capability/speed and autonomy/scale**. A third axis is structurally **unoccupied**:

> **The provable-governance axis: a per-decision, cryptographically-verifiable, court-admissible record that an AI action was license-clean, gate-cleared, and chip-attested — verifiable by a third party without trusting the vendor.**

Mapping our 10 features onto this open axis:

| Open white-space niche | Frontier status | Our feature(s) |
|------------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Per-inference court-admissible governance receipt | **Nobody ships it** (Anthropic = training-time only) | #3 Sovereign AI Passport, #10 Provable Inference Cloud |
| Court-admissible kill-chain chain-of-custody | Primes race on *speed*, not admissibility | #8 The Glass Hand-Off |
| Reflexive self-governing constitution (config gate-checks its own changes) | No GitOps/agent product does this | **#4 Doctrine-as-Code OS, #9 Doctrine v∞** |
| Public, real-time, *governed* multi-model deliberation | LLM Council 3D exists but ungoverned | #2 PURIQ Live Council |
| Time-scrub of a governed system from a tamper-evident DAG | Event-sourcing is backend-only | #1 Khipu Time Machine |
| ZK-federated threat intel with a *receipt* as the federation unit | Academic ZK-FL exists; no receipt-native product | #6 Receipt-Federated Threat Intel |
| Contributory, signed, reward-incentivized adversary catalog | CNAS dataset is static/unsigned | #7 Killinchu Open Adversary Catalog |
| Governance *in the weights*, anchored to a hash-locked reference gate | Constitutional AI lacks a hash anchor + drift proof | #5 The Provable Mind |

**Conclusion.** The 10 wow-features do not chase the frontier on capability or speed — a race SZL cannot win against $61B Anduril or Anthropic's compute. They claim the **uncontested third axis**: *provable, receipted, reflexive governance*. The two genuinely-novel features (#4, #9) sit at its purest point — a constitution that safely edits itself and gate-checks its own changes — which **no lab and no prime has shipped or announced** as of June 1 2026. The faster the frontier makes AI act, the more valuable a verifiable record that it acted *within bounds* becomes. That is the wedge.
