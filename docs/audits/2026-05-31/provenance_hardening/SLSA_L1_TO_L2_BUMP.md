## 1. Honest Verdict

**SLSA Build Level: L2.** Self-assessed, with real evidence below. **NOT L3.**

Per the [SLSA v1.0 build track](https://slsa.dev/spec/v1.0/levels):
- **L1** = provenance exists.
- **L2** = provenance is **signed** and generated by a **hosted build platform** (tamper-resistant against the build itself); distribution requires authenticated provenance.
- **L3** = hardened, isolated build platform with non-forgeable provenance and strong isolation between builds.

We claim **L2** because we now produce **signed provenance** (DSSE + Cosign, verifiable against a published key) generated on a hosted platform (GitHub Actions). We do **not** claim L3: we do not have a uniformly hardened, isolated CI across all artifacts, and we have not audited build isolation to the L3 bar.
