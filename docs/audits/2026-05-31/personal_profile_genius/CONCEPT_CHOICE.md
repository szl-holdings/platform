# CONCEPT_CHOICE — The Provenanced Notebook

**Chosen concept:** a **hybrid of Option D (The Living Notebook) + Option B (The Thinking Machine)**, fused around one idea no precedent has shipped:

> **A personal site that is itself an artifact in the chain it describes.**
> Every artifact on the page is one of Stephen's own and *actually runs*; every idea wears a **proof-state ripeness** badge graded by a machine, not by ego; and the page **mints a Khipu receipt for your visit** — so reading the notebook is itself a provenanced act, recorded in the same DAG the work is about.

Signed: **Yachay.**

---

## Why this and not the others

- **Not A (3D Workshop):** a 3D bench is *decorative-leaning* and heavy; the directive demands real widgets and Lighthouse >85. A workshop would spend its budget on polygons, not on the artifacts doing real work.
- **Not C (Cathedral/Lab split):** a left/right toggle is a layout gimmick, and "Cathedral" edges toward the mysticism the HARD RULES forbid.
- **Not E (Map of Knowledge):** beautiful, but a pannable world-map is closer to the org card's "empire survey" — it maps *domains*, not the *thinker*. We must focus on the mind, not the territory.
- **Not F (Multi-View tabs):** tabs are the most-done pattern in software portfolios; the opposite of "things no one has thought of."
- **D + B chosen:** the long-scroll Notebook (D) gives the *interior-of-one-mind* feel and lets each widget teach the sentence beside it (Ciechanowski applied to oneself). The Thinking-Machine DAG (B) supplies the connective tissue — artifacts are nodes, and the reader can **stack** them (Matuschak) to compose a derivation trail. Neither alone is novel; the fusion + provenance is.

## The four originality pillars (mapped to research)

1. **Self-operable artifacts** — the PURIQ Master Formula is a *live calculator*; the Lean theorem-of-the-week *compiles a snippet in a web worker*; the Khipu explorer *fetches/derives real receipts*. (Ciechanowski's "the widget is the argument," but the widgets are the founder's own work.)
2. **Machine-graded ripeness** — each idea-node shows `proved` / `sorry` pulled from the Doctrine v11 locked numbers (749 / 14 / 163). Appleton's garden ripeness, but graded by Lean, not by the author.
3. **Live "Today"** — latest commit + this-morning's thought, machine-pulled. Wolfram's exposed machinery, made live; Willison's recency-as-credibility.
4. **The page is provenanced** — every load mints an anonymous Khipu receipt (`hash(salt + page + minute)`, no PII) and tells you *"you are receipt #N in the chain."* No personal site on earth does this. It is the literal embodiment of the SZL doctrine — even reading about the work is a receipt-emitting act — and it is privacy-preserving by construction (computed client-side, never stored, never sent).
