# Investor-Safe Positioning — SZL Holdings

**Date:** April 2026  
**Purpose:** Guide what to say and not say to investors, design partners, and media across SZL Holdings and Carlota Jo

---

## The Core Positioning (Use This)

**SZL Holdings** is building a focused business observability company. The commercial wedge is Lyte + Alloy. Carlota Jo is the services lane generating near-term revenue. Vessels, Terra, and Aegis are expansion paths staged for after the wedge is commercially proven.

**Lyte** is the product — a command surface that surfaces execution risk, ownership drift, and workflow friction before they compound.

**Alloy** is the engine — an execution fabric for signal routing, workflow orchestration, and auditability.

Together, Lyte + Alloy is the story to investors, lenders, and design partners. Everything else is option value.

## Phrases to Avoid

| Phrase | Why | Replace with |
|--------|-----|-------------|
| "6 platforms live" | Not accurate for external-facing commercial readiness | "One focused commercial wedge, multiple prototyped lanes" |
| "$180M+ capital deployed" | Unverifiable, misleading | Omit or replace with stage-accurate framing |
| "3 continents market reach" | Implies operating revenue across continents | "Founder and operations across Washington D.C., London, Singapore" |
| "Enterprise scale" | Implies enterprise customers | "Enterprise-grade architecture" or "designed for enterprise teams" |
| "Institutional-grade intelligence" (Carlota Jo) | Implies Lyte/Alloy are production AI systems | "Structured, evidence-based advisory practice" |
| "94% clients engage on subsequent mandates" | Specific and unverifiable | Remove entirely |
| "14+ countries served" | No basis for this at early stage | "International advisory — London, New York, by arrangement" |

## The Honest Investor Story

### What's true and worth saying
1. **The wedge is focused.** Most early companies try to build too many things at once. SZL Holdings has deliberately chosen one commercial focus: Lyte + Alloy for business observability.
2. **The architecture is durable.** Alloy is the shared operating spine. Once it works for Lyte, expanding into Vessels, Terra, or Aegis doesn't require starting over.
3. **The founder operates directly.** Stephen Lutar is in every design-partner conversation, every pilot negotiation, every investor call. No management layer.
4. **Carlota Jo creates near-term revenue.** While Lyte matures, the advisory practice creates cash, relationships, and credibility with HNW and family office clients.
5. **Trust by design.** The audit trail and governance layer is built in, not bolted on. That matters to enterprise buyers and to regulated environments.

### What's honest about stage
- Lyte + Alloy is in design-partner mode. No commercial pilots have been formally announced.
- Vessels, Terra, and Aegis are prototyped but not in active go-to-market.
- Carlota Jo is live but is a boutique practice, not a scaled service business.

## SZL Holdings vs. Stephen Lutar — How to Separate

This separation matters to investors and partners who need to understand governance.

| | SZL Holdings | Stephen Lutar |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | The holding company / entity | The founder and operator |
| **What it does** | Owns and funds the platform companies | Builds and runs them directly |
| **How to contact** | Via contact form at szlholdings.com | Directly — same contact page |
| **What it's not** | Not a product, not a brand to sell into | Not managing a portfolio of passive investments |

**The right framing:** "SZL Holdings is Stephen Lutar operating a focused portfolio of software and services platforms. It is founder-led in the deepest sense — no management layer, no passive ownership."

## Carlota Jo Positioning

Carlota Jo is the advisory and services practice. For investor purposes, it serves two functions:
1. **Near-term revenue** — premium advisory generates cash before the software scales
2. **High-trust relationship development** — HNW and family office clients are the kind of operators who may become Lyte design partners or early customers

**For external use:** "Carlota Jo is a premium executive advisory practice focused on operational excellence, strategy, and residential management for UHNW principals. It is one revenue-generating entity within the SZL Holdings portfolio."

**What to avoid:** Saying Carlota Jo is "powered by Lyte + Alloy" in a way that implies a live AI system is doing the advisory work. Rosa does the advisory work. Lyte and Alloy may eventually provide the operational infrastructure — but that's a future integration, not a current one.
