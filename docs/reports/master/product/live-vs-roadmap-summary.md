## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Live — production** | Fully operational, serving real users or clients with no caveats |
| **Live — design partners** | Operational with a small cohort of design partners; not yet broadly available |
| **Live — accepting clients** | Accepting qualified inquiries; not automated or scaled |
| **Prototype** | Architecture and design exist; not deployed for external use |
| **Expansion — post-wedge** | Planned; goes to market after Lyte + Alloy is commercially proven |
| **Internal** | Operational but for internal/founder use only |
