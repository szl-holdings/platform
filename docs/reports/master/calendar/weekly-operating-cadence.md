# SZL Holdings — Weekly Operating Cadence

## Monday: Plan & Publish
- Review previous week's analytics (dos_page_views, dos_analytics_events)
- Publish weekly canonical article on owned site
- Schedule X thread for the week
- Update Linktree if new campaign links
- Review lead pipeline (dos_leads)

## Tuesday: Distribute & Engage
- Cross-post article to Medium
- Post 2 X singles derived from article
- Respond to all X/LinkedIn engagement from Monday
- Review newsletter subscriber growth

## Wednesday: Create & Build
- Write Substack Note
- Create PDF/carousel asset if scheduled
- Draft next week's canonical article
- Review campaign performance (dos_campaigns)

## Thursday: Depth & Authority
- Publish LinkedIn post (text or PDF document)
- Post X single (domain-specific insight)
- Publish Substack Note
- Review and update content calendar (dos_content_calendar_items)

## Friday: Wrap & Report
- Publish newsletter (Substack flagship)
- Post LinkedIn carousel or engagement piece
- Post 1-2 X singles
- Weekly analytics snapshot
- Update content calendar for next week

## Weekend: Light Touch
- Optional: 1 founder reflection post (X or LinkedIn)
- No active publishing — let content compound
- Review saved ideas for next week
