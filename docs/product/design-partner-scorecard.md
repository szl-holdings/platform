## Purpose

A single, weekly scorecard that captures the health of every active design partner relationship. The scorecard drives:

- Weekly account-by-account review
- Escalation triggers
- Founder check-in cadence
- Conversion forecasting

A partner without a current scorecard is a partner we are not actually managing.

## Weekly Indicators (1–5 scale; 3 is on track)

### 1. Operator Usage (weight: 25%)

| Indicator | 1 (red) | 3 (on track) | 5 (excellent) |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Sessions/week (champion) | 0 | ≥ 5 | ≥ 10 |
| Decisions recorded in Proof Chain | 0 in last 7 days | ≥ 10 / week | ≥ 25 / week |
| Mobile usage | None | ≥ 1 / week | ≥ 3 / week |
| Spread of users beyond champion | Champion only | 2–3 users | 4+ users |

### 2. Metric Trajectory (weight: 25%)

| Indicator | 1 (red) | 3 (on track) | 5 (excellent) |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Progress toward target | < 25% by end of land phase | ≥ 50% by midpoint | On or ahead of target |
| Direction | Declining or flat | Improving | Improving with acceleration |
| Confidence in measurement | Disputed | Clean | Verified by both parties |

### 3. Sponsor Engagement (weight: 15%)

| Indicator | 1 (red) | 3 (on track) | 5 (excellent) |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Attended kickoff | No | Yes | Yes, with prep |
| Attended midpoint | No | Yes | Yes, with written takeaways |
| Responsiveness to founder | > 7 days | 2–3 days | Same day |
| Mention of platform internally | No | Occasionally | Recurring |

### 4. Champion Engagement (weight: 15%)

| Indicator | 1 (red) | 3 (on track) | 5 (excellent) |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Attends weekly cadence | Misses 2+ | Misses 0–1 | Brings agenda |
| Contributes to roadmap input | None | Some | Detailed and prioritized |
| NPS-style ask: would they recommend? | 0–6 | 7–8 | 9–10 |
| Public reference willing? | No | Yes, anonymized | Yes, named |

### 5. Open Issues (weight: 10%)

| Indicator | 1 (red) | 3 (on track) | 5 (excellent) |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| SEV1 / SEV2 open | Yes | None | None for 30+ days |
| SEV3 open | > 5 | 1–3 | 0 |
| Roadmap items > 30 days unaddressed | > 3 | 1–2 | 0 |

### 6. Conversion Signal (weight: 10%)

| Indicator | 1 (red) | 3 (on track) | 5 (excellent) |
|-----------|---------|--------------|---------------|
| Has the sponsor named a budget owner? | No | Yes | Budget confirmed |
| Has expansion been discussed? | No | Mentioned | Specific scope |
| Decision date set? | No | Yes, end of pilot | Yes, with internal review scheduled |

## Aggregate Score and Tier

| Score | Tier | Action |
|-------|------|--------|
| ≥ 4.2 | Champion partner | Highlight in cohort report; ask for case study early |
| 3.5–4.1 | On track | Standard cadence |
| 2.8–3.4 | Watch | Founder joins next cadence call; corrective plan within 14 days |
| 2.0–2.7 | At risk | Founder + sponsor conversation; restructure or terminate |
| < 2.0 | Critical | Decision within 30 days: restructure, terminate, or rescope |

---

## Weekly Review Cadence

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Monday | Each CSM updates their scorecards | CSM |
| Tuesday | Founder reviews all scorecards (15 min) | Founder |
| Tuesday | Any partner < 3.0 escalated to founder for outreach | Founder |
| Friday | Cohort summary email to founder + leads | CSM lead |

---

## Cohort-Level Aggregation

In addition to per-partner scorecards, we track cohort-level metrics:

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Average cohort score | ≥ 3.5 |
| Partners in "watch" or worse | ≤ 1 |
| Conversion rate by end of cohort | ≥ 67% |
| Reference willingness by end of cohort | ≥ 80% |
| Case studies in flight by end of cohort | ≥ 50% |
| Roadmap items contributed | ≥ 5 per partner |
| Time-to-decision after pilot end | ≤ 14 days |

## Why a Single Scorecard

Multiple scorecards drift apart. A single document, weekly, with named scoring criteria, forces:

- Honesty (the rubric is fixed)
- Comparability (across partners and across cohorts)
- Forecastability (we can predict conversions)
- Founder bandwidth (15 min on Tuesday, not death-by-meeting)
