# Command — Unified Command: Demo Script

**Duration:** 8–10 minutes  
**Persona:** Marcus Holt (CFO / Executive)  
**URL:** `/command/`  
**Pre-requisite:** Demo seed loaded; signed into platform

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Strategy page shows 6 domain health cards with scores (Aegis 91, Vessels 87, SZL 84, PRISM 88, Lyte 82, Terra 79)
- [ ] At least one convergence event visible on the Convergence page
- [ ] Geospatial map shows GeoPin markers
- [ ] Executive briefing shows at least one pending action

---

## Step 1 — Strategy Overview (3 min)

**URL:** `/command/strategy`

> "This is Command — the unified operating system for the entire portfolio. Every domain pack surfaces here. The composite health score of 84 tells you the portfolio is operational."

Point to the six domain health cards.

> "Terra is monitoring at 79 because three positions are approaching a maturity wall. That's 90-day advance detection — the system caught it before the calendar did."

Navigate to **Executive Briefing** in the sidebar.

> "Every morning, the AI briefing engine generates this — ecosystem health, domain-by-domain status, and the exact actions waiting for executive sign-off. The content isn't static — it comes from live signals in the database."

Point to **Required Executive Actions** — "Approve fleet rerouting for Bay of Bengal corridor."

> "This isn't a notification. This is a governed action: if Marcus approves here, the approval propagates to Vessels in real time. If he dismisses, the record shows who, when, and why."

---

## Step 2 — Operations View (2 min)

**URL:** `/command/operations`

> "The operations view shifts from strategy to execution. You see workflow completion rates, agent performance, and the live action queue."

Point to active workflow items.

> "Every workflow is a DAG — a directed acyclic graph of tasks. The system knows which tasks are blocking which outcomes. This is how you go from 'we have a problem' to 'here is the step that unblocks it.'"

---

## Step 3 — Geospatial Intelligence (2 min)

**URL:** `/command/geospatial`

> "This is the geospatial intelligence layer. Every GeoPin is a live event from across the portfolio — a vessel position, a property distress signal, a field agent ping."

Point to the map markers.

> "These are live. The GeoPin store updates in real time. You can see the full portfolio's physical footprint in a single view."

---

## Step 4 — Convergence (1 min)

**URL:** `/command/convergence`

> "Convergence is where signals from different domains collide and create compound risk. When a weather event in the Bay of Bengal triggers both a Vessels route deviation AND a Terra property flood risk, Convergence shows you the compound impact before it hits earnings."

---

## Step 5 — Counterfactual Simulation (1 min)

**URL:** `/command/counterfactual`

> "The what-if engine runs Monte Carlo simulations on portfolio decisions. What happens to margin if we reroute three vessels around the Cape? The engine models it in-process — no spreadsheets."

---

## Avoidance Guide

- Do NOT demo **Export History** as fully functional — download delivery is not wired to object storage
- Do NOT demo **Competitive Atlas** as live intelligence — data is seeded
- CORTEX cross-domain badge counts are seeded — do not present as real-time signal counts

---

## Questions to Anticipate

**"Is this one app or many?"**  
> "It's one unified command surface backed by a shared API and data layer. Each domain pack has its own workspace but all data flows through the same decision ledger."

**"What prevents an executive from taking an action they shouldn't?"**  
> "Every action goes through the Guardian policy engine. Policies define who can approve what, under what conditions, and what tier of oversight is required. Tier 1 actions are autonomous; Tier 5 require human-in-the-loop approval."
