# Prism Counsel — Executive Overview

**Audience:** Managing Partners, COOs, Legal Technology Decision-Makers  
**Date:** April 2026

---

## The Problem

Plaintiff-side insurance litigation firms handle dozens to hundreds of active matters simultaneously. Attorneys and paralegals spend significant time doing work that should be automated: assembling matter status from disparate sources, chasing medical records, identifying what is missing before mediation, manually reviewing carrier communications for silence windows and posture changes, and tracking deadline compliance across a sprawling portfolio.

Meanwhile, the signals that determine whether a matter resolves well — insurer response patterns, coverage dispute indicators, venue velocity, evidence completeness, lien exposure — are scattered across email inboxes, SharePoint folders, case management systems, and individual attorney knowledge. No single view exists.

The result: matters that should settle quickly get stuck. Evidence gaps go unnoticed until mediation. Deadlines are managed by memory. AI tools are used ad hoc, producing outputs that nobody reviewed and nobody can trace.

---

## What Prism Counsel Does

Prism Counsel is a legal matter intelligence platform that gives plaintiff-side litigation teams the observability and governed execution infrastructure they need to run a high-volume practice with confidence.

**It does three things:**

### 1. Makes Every Matter Visible

Prism Counsel builds a structured, living picture of every matter — the Matter Twin. Parties, claims, deadlines, evidence completeness, financial exposure, insurer behavior, and AI-generated insights are assembled into a single view that updates automatically as new information arrives.

Attorneys see what is missing, what has changed, and what needs attention — without assembling it themselves.

### 2. Surfaces the Right Intelligence

The Pressure Graph scores 12 dimensions of legal pressure across every matter: deadline proximity, insurer response behavior, coverage disputes, evidence completeness, settlement friction, venue dynamics, and more.

Six data products synthesize those dimensions into actionable signals: the Insurer Pressure Index, Venue Velocity Index, Settlement Friction Map, and others. Forecasts show where each matter is heading — and why.

Worldline enriches internal matter data with external context: court venue patterns, weather at the incident date, regulatory insurance signals, lien recovery context.

### 3. Governs Every AI Output

Prism Counsel uses AI extensively — for document extraction, classification, matter analysis, settlement strategy, and communication review. But every AI output is anchored to a verifiable Proof Chain entry, carries a confidence score, and cannot be exported until an attorney has reviewed and approved it.

The Copilot Workbench gives attorneys and paralegals a structured interface for interacting with AI across five modes: matter analysis, communications, document review, strategy, and operations. Every response is source-grounded. Every response is logged. Advisory only — AI recommends, attorneys decide.

## Why This Matters Now

The legal profession is in transition. The firms that will win the next decade are those that can handle more matters without proportionally more headcount, produce better outcomes through better intelligence, and demonstrate governance and auditability of their AI usage to clients and oversight bodies.

Prism Counsel is built for that transition. It is not an AI assistant bolt-on — it is an intelligence and execution infrastructure layer that makes every attorney in the firm more effective.

## Who Uses It

**Attorneys** use Prism Counsel to understand where their matters stand, prepare for mediation and trial, review AI-generated analysis, and approve content for external use.

**Paralegals** use it to track deadlines, upload documents, route communications, submit work for attorney review, and monitor the review queue.

**Firm operators and legal ops** use it to monitor connector health, system status, and aggregate matter portfolio health.

**Managing partners** use it to understand portfolio-level risk, throughput, and exposure.
