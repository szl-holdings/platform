## KORA — Detailed Solution Brief

### The Problem

Operations and executive teams at most organizations are managing from lagging, fragmented signals. Approval queues live in email. SLA health lives in a spreadsheet. Team performance is in one tool, financial metrics in another, escalations in a third. Nobody has the full picture.

When problems compound — a delayed approval cascades into a missed SLA cascades into a customer escalation — they're discovered after the fact, not prevented.

### The Solution

KORA delivers **Decision Intelligence** — the capability to see across an organization's full operational surface in real time and understand signal, causality, and recommended action in a single, traceable workflow.

**PRISM Framework:**
- **Pulse** — Organization's operating heartbeat: revenue trend, team health, delivery cadence, customer satisfaction
- **Risk** — What is at risk of drifting: approval queues, churn signals, SLA exposure, ownership gaps
- **Intelligence** — AI-modeled reasoning across the Pulse and Risk signals, with confidence scores and contributing evidence
- **Signals** — Event-driven anomalies, drift patterns, behavioral changes across every connected data source
- **Motion** — The action layer: escalations routing, approval workflows, intervention tracking

**Command Inbox:** Every actionable signal in one ranked, attributed, routable queue.

**Action Queue:** The tasks that need to happen, who owns them, what's blocked, and what's overdue.

**Approvals Center:** Every approval that's waiting, who it's waiting on, and how long it's been sitting.

**A11oy Integration:** Every signal can become a workflow. Every action is approved, logged, and attributed.

### Use Cases

| Use Case | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Approval latency reduction | Identify and resolve stalled approvals before they cascade |
| Executive operational briefing | Single dashboard for C-suite operational health view |
| PMO signal management | Project signal aggregation with escalation routing |
| Financial workflow visibility | Approval and authorization trail for financial operations |
| Operational readiness scoring | Organizational health scoring for board reporting |

## PARAGON — Detailed Solution Brief

### The Problem

Security operations teams are drowning in alerts and tools. The average enterprise deploys 40+ security tools. MTTD (Mean Time to Detect) for advanced threats is still measured in months. SOC analysts report alert fatigue as their primary challenge.

### The Solution

PARAGON delivers unified defense and intelligence command — three workspaces sharing one intelligence context.

**Defense Workspace:** SOC command, MITRE ATT&CK v14 detection coverage, SOAR playbook engine, threat intelligence integration (STIX/TAXII).

**Command Workspace:** MSP operations command — multi-client SLA management, ticketing, client health dashboards. For managed security providers.

**Intelligence Workspace:** AI research command — model registry, experiment tracking, ensemble evaluation, LLM governance. For organizations building or evaluating AI systems.

## SEXTANT — Detailed Solution Brief

### The Problem

Fleet operators have vessel position data in one system, voyage economics in a spreadsheet, compliance risk in a manual review process, and dark vessel flags in email. They are legally liable for sanctions violations they could detect but don't. The commercial cost of a poorly-timed voyage decision exceeds $1M per vessel.

### The Solution

SEXTANT delivers maritime intelligence in one command surface:
- Real-time AIS telemetry and fleet tracking
- Voyage economics modeling (cost per mile, ETA accuracy, fuel efficiency)
- Dark vessel detection (AIS signal gap analysis)
- Sanctions screening (OFAC, UN, EU, UK)
- Route intelligence and weather risk analysis
- Exception Center with consequence modeling
- Helmsman AI agent for fleet intelligence and recommendations
