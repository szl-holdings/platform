## The Problem We Solve

Your organization generates more operational data than it can act on. The teams responsible for outcomes spend their time in three places: chasing signals across disconnected tools, synthesizing information that should be synthesized automatically, and discovering problems too late to prevent them from compounding.

This is not a technology deficit. You have the tools. The problem is that no single system connects what those tools know with what the people running your business need to understand — and act on — in real time.

SZL Holdings builds that system.

## What We Build

**KORA — Decision Intelligence**  
The cross-domain decision command surface for executives, operations leads, and PMOs. KORA makes every operational surface visible, contextual, and actionable through the PRISM framework (Pulse, Risk, Intelligence, Signals, Motion). Command inbox. Action queue. Approval latency detection. Readiness scoring. All in one surface, with full audit trail.

**A11oy — Execution Fabric**  
The system that closes the loop. When KORA surfaces a signal, A11oy routes the action. Workflow engine, human-in-the-loop approval gates, agent coordination, and immutable audit trail. Every consequential action is confirmed by a person and recorded permanently.

**PARAGON — Defense & Intelligence**  
For organizations that need unified visibility across security operations, managed services, and AI research. Three workspaces, one context: Defense (SOC), Command (managed services), Intelligence (AI governance).

**SEXTANT — Maritime Intelligence**  
Fleet command with real intelligence. AIS telemetry, voyage economics, dark vessel detection, sanctions screening, route analysis. For maritime operators where the cost of poor visibility is measured in sanctions exposure and voyage economics.

**DOMAINE — Real Estate Intelligence**  
Property intelligence for NYC brokers and investors. Live distress data pipeline, ownership structure tracking, deal pipeline management. Finding opportunities before the competition does.

## Why This Is Different

**One infrastructure investment, five domain platforms.** Every SZL platform shares the same authentication model, the same audit trail, the same AI engine, the same design system. You add a vertical; you don't add an infrastructure problem.

**AI that explains itself.** Every AI recommendation includes the reasoning and contributing signals. Your people make the decisions — the platform makes them faster and better-informed.

**Human-in-the-loop by design, not by policy.** Consequential actions require explicit human approval at the workflow engine level. This cannot be bypassed in code. It is how we build, not how we write policies.

**Audit trail that is an operational tool.** Every significant action, AI recommendation, approval, and outcome is logged permanently and attributably. Not for compliance theater — for operational accountability that is genuinely useful.
