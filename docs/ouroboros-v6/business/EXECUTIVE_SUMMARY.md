## What it is

A runtime trust envelope for AI systems. Ten observable primitives that measure whether an AI system is leaking, wasting, or lying — continuously, in production.

## Why now

Enterprises deploying AI agents have observability (Datadog, Splunk, Honeycomb) but not governance evidence. Logs answer "what happened." Witness roots answer "what can be proven." The gap between the two is the regulated-vertical procurement bottleneck.

## What's defensible

- **Published thesis** with DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.19944926](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19944926).
- **144 passing tests** across 5 TypeScript workspaces.
- **Two theorems** (Cleanliness, Resonance Handoff) with full citation chain to Page, 't Hooft, Susskind, Wootters & Zurek, Landauer, Kuramoto, Pozar.
- **Air-gapped deployment mode** for HIPAA, defense, public-sector buyers who cannot phone home.
- **Property-based verifier** + benchmark suite proving the primitives hold across the input space.
- **Threat model + privacy posture** against GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, SOC2, ISO 27001.
