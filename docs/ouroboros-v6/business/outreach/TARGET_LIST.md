## How to use this list

1. Don't email all at once. Pick 3 per category for week 1.
2. Personalize every email — reference a recent post, talk, or paper.
3. Lead with the DOI and the test count, not the thesis.
4. Never pitch acquisition. Pitch design partnership or technical collaboration.
5. Track replies in a simple spreadsheet. 5% reply rate is normal cold; 15% is a good template.
