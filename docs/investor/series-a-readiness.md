## What This Document Is

A single, candid assessment of SZL Holdings' readiness to raise a Series A. It is the document the founder uses to decide *when* to enter the round — and the document a prospective lead investor receives before diligence.

It is honest about what is ready and what is not. The same document goes to the friendliest investor and to the most skeptical.

---

## Headline Posture

**Status (as of April 2026):** **Pre-Series A**, on a deliberate path to be Series A-ready by **Q4 2026 / Q1 2027**.

We are not raising a Series A on April 2026 metrics. We are raising on:

1. ≥ 6 named design partners with at least 4 in active production
2. Cohort 1 conversion rate ≥ 67%
3. ARR ≥ $1.5M with line of sight to $5M
4. Cross-domain proof in production (≥ 2 packs running for at least 2 customers)
5. Trust Center complete; SOC 2 readiness assessment underway
6. Senior team formed (founder + 4–6 senior contributors named)
7. Three reference customers willing to take investor calls

The plan is to enter the round when at least 6 of these are met.

The round starts when:

- Cohort 1 design partners are 5/6 converted to commercial commitments
- Cumulative ARR ≥ $1.5M
- One named customer reference willing to take investor calls
- Senior engineering hire in seat
- Trust Center reviewed by at least one external security advisor
- Product roadmap reviewed and signed off by board

Target trigger date: Q4 2026.

## Key Investor Questions and Honest Answers

### "What is the category?"

Governed decision infrastructure. We document this in [CATEGORY_POSITIONING.md](../sales/category-positioning.md). It is new; the budget does not yet exist on most buyers' P&Ls. We are creating the budget through the design partner motion.

### "Who is the buyer?"

Operator-led decision. Mid-market and Enterprise organizations in regulated industries. See [BUYER_PERSONAS.md](../sales/buyer-personas.md). Not Fortune 100 IT in 2026.

### "How big can this be?"

Total addressable market analysis is in [INVESTOR_NARRATIVE.md](investor-narrative.md). The honest version: the *current* category is small. The category we are creating could be on the order of $10–30B at maturity if every regulated mid-market operator runs governed decisions. We do not promise that today; we promise the wedge.

### "What is the moat?"

Six primitives + five domain packs running on a shared platform with full audit attribution. The moat is architectural composition + cross-domain breadth + audit-grade governance. See [MOAT_MAP.md](moat-map.md). It is not "AI" — that is a substrate.

### "Why now?"

Three converging trends:
1. AI-driven decision volume is exploding; governance has not kept up
2. Regulatory expectation on AI governance is rising (EU AI Act, US executive orders)
3. Mid-market operators have given up on copilots and dashboards as their decision system

### "Why this team?"

Founder background documented in [INVESTOR_NARRATIVE.md](investor-narrative.md). Operator-tier engineering depth. Adviser roster covers the domains we sell into. Senior hires are in flight.

### "What if a hyperscaler ships this?"

Microsoft / Google / AWS will ship pieces. They will not ship the operator-tier, mid-market, multi-domain composition. Their incentives push them to enterprise + horizontal generic. Our wedge is operator-tier vertical depth on a shared governance layer. See [OBJECTION_HANDLING.md](../sales/objection-handling.md).

### "What if Palantir comes down-market?"

Palantir's pricing floor is high; their motion is consultant-heavy. We sell to organizations that cannot afford a Palantir engagement. If they re-architect their motion to mid-market, we will need to sharpen our wedge — but their organizational gravity is upward.

### "What's broken?"

Honestly:
- We are pre-revenue (a few hundred K in design partner ARR by mid-2026)
- We do not have SOC 2
- Native MFA at the platform level is not yet implemented (KG026)
- Our team is small relative to the ambition
- We have not yet executed cross-region failover against live load

These are documented in [KNOWN-GAPS.md](../operations/known-gaps.md). They are not surprises. They are the work.

### "When do you need to raise?"

We have runway through Q3 2027 at current burn. We *want* to raise in Q1 2027 to fund the 2027 expansion plan. We will raise earlier if a strong term sheet appears; we will not raise prematurely if the metrics are not there.

## Series A Pitch Themes

### Theme 1: Category Creation

We are building a category that did not exist. The thesis is: every operator decision in regulated industries needs governance. The platform is the infrastructure for that governance. Five domain packs prove it works across domains.

### Theme 2: Architectural Composition

Six primitives. Five domain packs. One platform. The moat is not in any one primitive — it is in the composition and the cross-domain breadth.

### Theme 3: Operator-Tier Wedge

We sell to operators, not to procurement. Mid-market, not Fortune 100. Our cycle time and price point are at the wedge that Palantir cannot serve and that hyperscalers cannot reach down to.

### Theme 4: Honesty as Competitive Advantage

We publish a Known Gaps document. We publish the Trust Center. We publish pricing. We publish the diligence packet. In a category full of vendor evasion, we are the credible builder.

### Theme 5: Operator-Funded Growth

The design partner program funds itself. Cohort 1 generates revenue while producing references and product input. We do not need to fund a category-creation campaign before commercial signal exists.
