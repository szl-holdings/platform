## Trial / Proof-of-Value

All plans include a **14-day free trial** with full Enterprise-tier feature access. No credit card required.

For Enterprise and Command evaluations, a structured **Proof-of-Value (PoV)** period of 30–60 days is available — full Enterprise access, customer production data, direct founder support, and agreed success criteria.
