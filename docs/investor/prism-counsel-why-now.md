## The Timing Argument

Legal AI is not new. But the prior wave — automated discovery tools, contract review assistants, legal research assistants — was largely document-centric and operated at the task level. It made individual tasks faster. It did not change how a practice is run.

The current moment is different for three reasons:

---

### 1. The AI Infrastructure Moment

The models capable of genuine legal reasoning — multi-step analysis, source integration, nuanced judgment — have become accessible at a price point that makes them viable inside matter-level economics.

GPT-4o class models can analyze a matter's full communication history, extract posture signals, compare them against similar patterns, and produce a grounded assessment in seconds. This was not possible or economical 18 months ago.

But the models alone are not the product. The firms that can assemble the right infrastructure around the models — context assembly, permission-aware retrieval, governed output, audit trail, human oversight gates — will build the durable product. Raw model access is not differentiation. The platform layer is differentiation.

Prism Counsel is that platform layer — built specifically for the legal matter context.

---

### 2. The Governance Pressure Moment

Law firms are under pressure to demonstrate that their AI usage is governed, auditable, and responsible. Bar associations, clients, and courts are increasingly asking: When AI was used in this matter, how was it supervised? Can you show the review chain?

This creates a category that did not exist 18 months ago: **governed legal AI infrastructure**. Not just AI that helps attorneys work faster — but AI with a demonstrable audit trail, human review gates, and source traceability built into the workflow.

Prism Counsel is positioned for this category. The Proof Chain, review workflow, attorney sign-off gates, and immutable audit trail are not features bolted on for compliance optics — they are the architecture.

Firms that deploy unaudited AI tools today face increasing exposure. Firms that deploy governed AI infrastructure today are building a defensible position.

---

### 3. The M365 Distribution Moment

Microsoft 365 is deployed in over 80% of U.S. law firms with 100+ attorneys. Microsoft's rollout of Copilot and the declarative/custom agent framework has created a distribution channel for legal AI products that did not exist before.

Prism Counsel can deploy into a law firm's existing M365 environment — using their SharePoint, Outlook, and Teams infrastructure. Attorneys do not need to adopt a new tool; Prism Counsel meets them where they already work.

This is a distribution advantage that narrows the adoption friction to near-zero for M365-native firms. It also creates a natural path from a lightweight declarative agent (quick lookups in Teams) to a full custom engine agent (complete Prism Counsel capability in Teams) — a low-friction, high-value expansion motion.

## The Market

**Primary market:** Plaintiff-side NY insurance litigation practices.

- NY is the largest personal injury litigation market in the United States
- No-fault insurance laws create a high-volume, structured matter type that is ideal for intelligence automation
- The combination of structured claim data, defined deadlines, and carrier communication patterns makes pressure scoring highly tractable
- NY plaintiff firms range from boutique (10-20 attorneys) to large (200+ attorneys), with the mid-market (20-100 attorneys) as the primary target segment

**Adjacent markets (Phase 2+):**
- Plaintiff-side litigation in other high-volume states (FL, TX, CA, NJ)
- Other insurance litigation types: workers' compensation, premises liability, product liability
- Defense-side insurance litigation (different posture, same matter intelligence value)
- Legal operations at insurance carriers managing inbound claims

**Market size:**
NY alone has over 2,000 plaintiff-side insurance litigation practices. The mid-market segment (20-100 attorneys, 50-500 active matters) is the primary wedge. At $X per matter per month, the addressable revenue in NY alone is substantial.
