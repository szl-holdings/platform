## Revenue Architecture

The SZL revenue model is built on three layers:

| Layer | Cadence | Drivers |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Subscription (recurring) | Annual | Platform editions + domain pack entitlements + seats |
| Services (non-recurring) | One-time | Onboarding, implementation, custom integration, training |
| Add-ons (recurring) | Annual | Premium support, retention extension, custom AI model, residency |

Subscription is ~85% of revenue at maturity. Services are ~10%. Add-ons are ~5%.

| Type | Recognition |
|------|-------------|
| Subscription | Ratably over the subscription term |

| Custom integration | At completion |

## Pricing Sensitivity

The revenue model assumes:

- Average platform fee: $130K (Starter) → $200K (Pro) → $450K (Enterprise)
- Average pack fee: $48K
- Average packs per customer: 1.6 (Year 1) → 2.4 (Year 3)
- Discount average: 18% (skewed by design partners)

Sensitivity:

- 10% price reduction → revenue plan reduces ~10%, payback period extends ~1 month
- 20% pack attachment improvement → revenue plan increases ~12%
- Conversion rate 12% → 8% in 2027 → revenue reduces ~30%

The plan is most sensitive to conversion rate (driven by GTM motion quality) and pack attachment (driven by product breadth and CSM motion).
