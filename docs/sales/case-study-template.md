## Why This Template Exists

Every case study we publish must:

1. Be defensible (numbers from real measurement, not estimates)
2. Be approved by the customer (legally + reputationally)
3. Tell the SZL story in the customer's words
4. Be useful in a sales conversation (the AE quotes from it)
5. Be repeatable (so the next case study takes days, not weeks)

This template is the structure. Every case study uses it.

---

## Production Process

| Step | Owner | Duration |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1. CSM proposes case study target after pilot Week 8 | CSM | 1 week |
| 2. Customer sponsor agrees in principle | CSM + Sponsor | 1 week |
| 3. Marketing schedules customer interview (champion + sponsor) | Marketing | 2 weeks |
| 4. First draft from interview transcript + measurement data | Marketing | 1 week |
| 5. Customer review and edits | Customer | 1–2 weeks |
| 6. Final approval and sign-off | Sponsor | 1 week |
| 7. Publication (named or anonymized) | Marketing | 1 week |

Total typical time: 8–10 weeks from proposal to publication.

## Numbers Discipline

Every number in a case study must:

1. Have a measurement method documented
2. Have a baseline
3. Have a comparison period (e.g., "30 days pre-launch vs. 30 days post-launch")
4. Be verified by the customer
5. Be the customer's number, not ours

If a customer cannot validate a number, the number does not appear.

If a measurement method is contested, we describe both the high and the low estimate, and explain the variance.

---

## Anonymization Rules

If the customer wants anonymization, we:

- Replace name with industry + size descriptor ("a 200-person maritime operator")
- Remove geography to a region ("North America" rather than country)
- Remove dates to relative ("within 90 days of go-live")
- Remove staff names to roles ("the head of operations")

We do not:

- Fabricate any details
- Move numbers to round figures unless the customer asks
- Mix data from multiple customers into one composite

---

## Approval Discipline

A case study is published only when:

1. The customer sponsor signs the final draft
2. Marketing has reviewed for compliance with this template
3. The CSM confirms the customer is in good standing
4. Founder has read the final
5. Customer has been given a 7-day window to retract

Any of those failing means the case study sits in draft until resolved.
