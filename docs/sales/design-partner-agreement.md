## DESIGN PARTNER AGREEMENT

This Design Partner Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of **[Effective Date]** (the "**Effective Date**") between:

- **SZL Holdings, [entity form, jurisdiction]** ("**SZL**"), with its principal place of business at [Address]; and
- **[Partner Legal Name], [entity form, jurisdiction]** ("**Partner**"), with its principal place of business at [Address].

SZL and Partner are each a "**Party**" and collectively the "**Parties**."

### 1. Background

SZL operates a governed-decision infrastructure platform consisting of one or more domain packs (the "**Platform**"). Partner wishes to participate in SZL's Design Partner Program (the "**Program**") on the terms set out below.

### 2. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for **twelve (12) months** (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 11. The Initial Term may be extended by mutual written agreement.

### 3. Scope

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Domain pack(s) in scope | **[Aegis / Vessels / Terra / Counsel — list specific packs]** |
| Tenant tier | **[Per [TENANT_TIERS.md](../product/tenant-tiers.md)]** |
| Permitted users | Up to **[N]** named users on Partner's side |
| Production deployment | Required (not a sandbox) |
| Kickoff Date | **[Date — within 30 days of Effective Date]** |

### 4. Fees

| Fee | Year 1 | Year 2 | Year 3+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform fee | **50% of list** | 25% off list | List + 7% escalator |
| Pack fee(s) | **50% of list** | 25% off list | List + 7% escalator |
| Onboarding | **Included (no charge)** | n/a | n/a |

Year 1 pricing is locked and cannot be increased mid-term. Fees invoiced **[annually in advance / quarterly]**, due Net 30.

### 5. Success Metric

The Parties will, **within fourteen (14) days of Kickoff Date**, agree in writing on:

- **A specific, measurable success metric** for the Program (the "**Success Metric**"). Examples: time-to-defensible-decision-trail; audit response time; analyst hours saved per week.
- **The baseline** for that metric (Partner's pre-Platform measurement).
- **The Day-90 measurement methodology.**
- **The Day-90 review meeting date.**

If a Success Metric is not agreed within fourteen (14) days, either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice with no further obligation.

### 6. Partner Commitments

Partner commits to:

a) **Real production deployment** of the in-scope domain pack(s) — not a sandbox or evaluation environment;

b) **Quarterly product feedback session** with SZL's founder and Partner's operator champion;

c) **Up to four (4) reference calls per year** with prospects mutually agreed in advance;

d) **One (1) case study per year**, in a form mutually agreed; anonymized acceptable, named preferred. Partner may withhold consent to publish the final case study, in which case SZL will not publish it;

e) **Logo permission** — limited license for SZL to use Partner's name and logo on SZL's website and marketing materials, revocable on 30 days' written notice;

f) **Honest feedback**, including reporting of bugs, regressions, and unfit features;

g) **One (1) public reference per year** — conference panel, podcast, written piece, or analyst briefing — at Partner's choice of forum.

### 7. SZL Commitments

SZL commits to:

a) **Onboarding** — provisioning, identity (OIDC/SCIM) setup, signal source connection, operator training (one cohort), Success Metric instrumentation, all included;

b) **Monthly founder working session** with Partner's operator champion;

c) **Quarterly product input session** — Partner's prioritized requests will receive a written disposition (build / decline / defer with rationale);

d) **Pricing lock** through the Initial Term;

e) **Service level objectives** per [SLO_DEFINITIONS.md](SLO_DEFINITIONS.md) (or attached SLO exhibit);

f) **Reasonable prioritization** of Partner's requested capabilities, where consistent with SZL's broader roadmap.

### 8. Data

Data handling is governed by the separately executed **Data Processing Agreement** (the "**DPA**") attached as Exhibit A or executed contemporaneously. In the absence of a DPA, the data terms in [DATA-RETENTION.md](../security/data-retention.md) apply.

Partner retains all right, title, and interest in Partner data submitted to the Platform. SZL processes Partner data solely to provide the Platform and the Program.

### 9. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to hold the other's Confidential Information in strict confidence and use it only for the purposes of this Agreement. Confidentiality obligations survive termination for **three (3) years**.

### 10. Intellectual Property

a) SZL retains all right, title, and interest in the Platform and any improvements, including improvements derived from Partner's feedback (Partner grants SZL a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to incorporate feedback into the Platform).

b) Partner retains all right, title, and interest in Partner data and any work product produced by Partner.

c) Each Party retains its own pre-existing intellectual property.

### 11. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement:

a) For convenience, **at the end of the Day-90 pilot period** or **at the end of the Initial Term**, on **thirty (30) days'** written notice;

b) For material breach not cured within thirty (30) days of written notice;

c) Immediately, on the other Party's bankruptcy, insolvency, or similar event.

On termination:

- SZL provides Partner a **ninety (90) day data export window**.
- No claw-back of pilot pricing.
- Partner's logo permission terminates 30 days after termination.
- Partner is not bound by NDA from honestly describing Partner's reasons for termination.

### 12. Warranties and Disclaimers

a) Each Party warrants it has authority to enter into this Agreement.

b) SZL warrants the Platform will substantially conform to its documentation. **OTHER THAN AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE PLATFORM IS PROVIDED "AS IS" DURING THE DESIGN PARTNER PROGRAM. SZL DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.**

### 13. Limitation of Liability

**EXCEPT FOR (i) BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR (ii) INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT WILL EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY PARTNER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY. NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.**

### 14. Indemnification

a) SZL will indemnify Partner against third-party claims that the Platform infringes intellectual property rights, subject to reasonable cooperation and standard carve-outs.

b) Partner will indemnify SZL against third-party claims arising from Partner data or Partner's use of the Platform in violation of this Agreement.

### 15. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of **[Governing State / Country]**. Disputes will be resolved in the **[Courts / Arbitration forum] of [Jurisdiction]**.

### 16. General

a) **Notices** to the addresses above; email permitted with read receipt or written acknowledgment.

b) **Assignment** — neither Party may assign without the other's written consent, except in connection with merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

c) **Entire agreement** — this Agreement, the DPA, and any exhibits constitute the entire agreement and supersede prior discussions.

d) **Amendment** — only by writing signed by both Parties.

e) **Severability** — if any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect.

f) **Counterparts and electronic signature** — permitted.
