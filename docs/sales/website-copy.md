### The Loop Section

**Headline:**
> Every consequential decision follows one canonical loop.

**Body:**
> Signal → Context → Recommendation → Simulation → Policy → Execution → Proof → Outcome → Learning

> Nine steps. Every domain. The governance does not change.

**Loop step descriptions (inline):**
- **Signal** — Every decision starts with a business event, ingested from your operational surface
- **Context** — Cross-domain intelligence enriches the signal before a human ever sees it
- **Recommendation** — AI proposes an action with full evidence, confidence score, and model attribution
- **Simulation** — Monte Carlo models the risk of each scenario before any approval is requested
- **Policy** — Covenant Policy checks every action against your governance rules
- **Execution** — Approved actions run as tracked, durable workflows
- **Proof** — An immutable Proof Chain record is sealed — traceable to signal, approver, and outcome
- **Outcome** — Actual results are recorded and compared to the prediction
- **Learning** — Outcomes calibrate the model — the platform gets smarter with every decision

### Six Primitives Section

**Headline:**
> Six primitives. Not features.

**Subheadline:**
> These are architectural constraints that run across every domain pack — not add-ons or configuration options.

**Primitive descriptions:**

- **Outcome Graph** — Full decision lifecycle tracking: recommendation → decision → outcome. The platform learns from every closed loop.

- **Proof Chain** — Immutable audit trail with provenance for every AI output. Model identity, source citations, confidence score, and actor attribution — permanently recorded.

- **Covenant Policy** — Enforces human-in-the-loop governance at the platform layer. AI cannot bypass it.

- **Decision Simulation** — Probabilistic risk modeling before action. Operators see not just what to do but what could happen — with confidence intervals and sensitivity analysis.

- **Workflow Engine** — Durable multi-step process orchestration. Complex operational decisions run as tracked, recoverable workflows.

- **Event Fabric** — Cross-domain signal backbone. Maritime signals, security alerts, legal filings, and financial events share a common layer — enabling correlated intelligence across all domains.

## Copy Don'ts

- Do not use "AI-powered" without noting the governance model
- Do not use "disrupting" or "revolutionizing"
- Do not call Lyte or CORTEX "dashboards"
- Do not claim SOC 2 certification — say "built to SOC 2 controls, certification targeted for 2026"
- Do not fabricate customer logos or testimonials
- Do not say "autonomous AI" — always "advisory-only with mandatory human approval gates"
