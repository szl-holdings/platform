## Company Overview

**SZL Holdings** builds governed decision infrastructure for enterprise operators — the structural layer between signal detection and action execution that enforces governance, attribution, and outcome tracking on every consequential decision.

The company was founded by **Stephen Lutar** and is currently in the design partner phase, building alongside early enterprise customers in security, maritime, real estate, legal, and professional services.

---

## Key Messages

1. **"Not a dashboard — a governed decision surface."** Lyte shows operators what to do next, who is responsible, and whether the action is safe to execute. Dashboards show what happened.

2. **"AI without governance is noise."** AI tools add recommendation volume without accountability. SZL Holdings enforces human approval gates on all consequential AI-assisted actions via Covenant Policy.

3. **"Every decision has a signal, a route, a proof, and an outcome."** The governed decision loop creates a full chain of custody: Signal → Context → Recommendation → Simulation → Policy → Execution → Proof → Outcome → Learning.

4. **"The accountability gap is the next enterprise problem."** As AI permeates operations, the question shifts from "what is the AI recommending?" to "who approved it, based on what evidence, and what happened?"

5. **"Six primitives, not features."** The platform is structurally different — Outcome Graph, Proof Chain, Covenant Policy, Decision Simulation, Workflow Engine, and Event Fabric are architectural primitives that run across every domain pack, not add-on features.

6. **"One platform. Every high-consequence domain."** The hierarchy is: SZL Holdings → Lyte (flagship command) → Alloy (execution fabric) → CORTEX (mobile) → Domain packs. Not a portfolio of startups — a governed platform with domain extensions.

## Products (For Coverage)

### Lyte — Flagship Governed Command Surface

The PRISM framework (People, Revenue, Infrastructure, Security, Market) in a single command surface. Signal timeline, priority action queue, Monte Carlo simulation results, and execution accountability. The primary entry point to the SZL governance infrastructure.

### Alloy — Execution Fabric

Workflow orchestration, approval gates, and immutable audit trail. The governance layer that enforces the governed decision loop — connecting every signal to an accountable action with full attribution.

### CORTEX — Unified Mobile Command

All domain workspaces in one iOS/Android app. Biometric authentication, workspace switcher with cross-domain badge counts, unified command feed, and workspace-adaptive AI copilot.

### Six Platform Primitives

**Outcome Graph:** Tracks the full decision lifecycle — recommendation → decision → outcome — enabling closed-loop AI learning.

**Proof Chain:** Immutable audit trail with provenance for every action, including AI-generated content with model identity and source citations.

**Covenant Policy:** Permission and approval gates that enforce human-in-the-loop governance. AI cannot bypass it.

**Decision Simulation:** Probabilistic simulation before action — confidence intervals, sensitivity analysis, scenario comparison.

**Workflow Engine:** Durable multi-step process orchestration with agent coordination and checkpoint recovery.

**Event Fabric:** Cross-domain signal backbone — normalizes and routes signals from maritime, security, real estate, and legal so they can trigger correlated intelligence across domains.

### Domain Packs

**Aegis:** Security and defense domain pack. SOC command, MITRE ATT&CK mapping, SOAR playbooks, XDR, threat intelligence.

**Vessels:** Maritime domain pack. Fleet command, AIS telemetry, sanctions screening, dark vessel detection, voyage P&L.

**Terra:** Real estate domain pack. NYC distress pipeline, ownership graph, deal workflow, AI underwriting.

**PRISM Counsel:** Legal domain pack (integrated into Aegis). Matter management, AI triage with approval gates, proof chain audit trail, court filing integration.

**Carlota Jo:** Advisory domain pack. Client portal, service catalog, booking system, secure document delivery.

**IMPERIUM:** Cloud sovereignty domain pack. Multi-cloud governance, policy enforcement, and cloud estate visibility.

SZL Holdings builds governed decision infrastructure for enterprise operators. SZL Holdings builds the governed decision infrastructure that enterprise operators have been missing. As AI tools flood the market with recommendations, the accountability gap grows: decisions made without attribution, AI outputs without governance, signals without routes to action.

SZL Holdings addresses this with one canonical loop — Signal → Context → Recommendation → Simulation → Policy → Execution → Proof → Outcome → Learning — powered by six platform primitives: Outcome Graph, Proof Chain, Covenant Policy, Decision Simulation, Workflow Engine, and Event Fabric. CORTEX delivers this command layer on iOS and Android.
