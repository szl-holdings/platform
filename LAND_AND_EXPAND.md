## The Strategy

Customers do not buy the SZL platform whole on day one. They buy a single domain pack, prove it works, and then expand:

- **More packs** (cross-domain)
- **More seats** (broader adoption)
- **Higher edition** (new operational requirements)

The land step is small, focused, and proves value. The expand step is systematic, signal-driven, and compounding.

This is the playbook for the full motion.

## Phase 1: Land

### What "Landing" Means

The customer has signed an MSA and is in active production use of the platform on at least one domain pack, with a defined success metric and a champion.

### Land Profile (the smallest viable deal)

| Element | Default at land |
|---------|----------------|
| Edition | Starter (or Pro if they need cross-domain) |
| Packs | 1 |
| Seats | 5–25 |
| Contract | Annual |
| Initial ACV | $42K – $200K |

We do not push for larger first deals. A small land that works is more valuable than a large land that doesn't.

### Land Success Criteria (90 days post-go-live)

| Criterion | Required |
|-----------|---------|
| At least 10 decisions through the loop | ✅ |
| Champion uses platform ≥ 5 sessions / week | ✅ |
| Mobile usage by champion at least once / week | ✅ |
| Success metric trending toward target | ✅ |
| Sponsor attended at least one review | ✅ |
| Zero open SEV1 / SEV2 | ✅ |

If these are met, the customer transitions cleanly to Adopt phase.

## Phase 2: Adopt

### What "Adopting" Means

The platform is part of the operator team's daily rhythm. Multiple users (not just the champion) are active. The success metric has been hit or exceeded. The customer has internalized the value enough to start asking "what else can it do?"

### Adopt Indicators (months 4–12)

| Indicator | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Active users / seats | ≥ 60% |
| Daily active sessions per active user | ≥ 1 |
| Decisions / week trending up | ✅ |
| Outcome Graph showing accept/override patterns | ✅ |
| At least one feature request escalated to product | ✅ |
| Sponsor attends QBRs | ✅ |
| Customer mentions us internally as a tool of choice | ✅ |

### Adopt Activities

| Activity | Cadence | Owner |
|----------|---------|-------|
| QBR with sponsor + champion | Quarterly | CSM + Founder |
| Outcome Graph review (acceptance / override / achievement) | Quarterly | CSM |
| Roadmap input session | Quarterly | Founder |
| Mobile rollout to broader team | Ad hoc | CSM |
| Operator office hours | Monthly | CSM |
| First reference call (if design partner) | Quarter 4 | Founder + Marketing |
| Case study scoping (if appropriate) | Quarter 4 | Marketing |

### Adopt Failure Modes

| Failure | Recovery |
|---------|----------|
| Champion left customer | Identify successor within 30 days; re-onboard |
| Adoption flattens at < 30% of seats | Founder + sponsor conversation; reset scope or reduce seats |
| Success metric was wrong | Acknowledge; pick a better leading indicator |
| Customer's organizational change kills momentum | Pause QBR cadence; re-engage when organization stabilizes |

## Phase 3: Expand

Once the customer is firmly in Adopt phase (typically by end of Year 1), expansion becomes the primary CSM motion.

### Expansion Triggers

See [EXPANSION_MOTION.md](EXPANSION_MOTION.md) for the trigger taxonomy. Headline:

| Vector | Typical timing |
|--------|---------------|
| Pack expansion | Quarter 4 of Year 1, or any time a new domain emerges |
| Seat expansion | When usage exceeds 80% of cap for 30 days |
| Edition upgrade | When multi-tenancy, SCIM, residency, or pager support becomes a requirement |

### Expansion Cadence

| Cadence | Activity | Owner |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Weekly | Health snapshot reviewed for expansion signals | CSM |
| Monthly | Lyte usage data reviewed for triggers | CSM |
| Quarterly | QBR with expansion conversation if any vector active | CSM + Founder |
| Annually | Renewal-and-expansion proposal in same paper | CSM |

## End-to-End Customer Lifecycle Example

A representative mid-market customer over 3 years:

| Quarter | State | ACV | Notes |
|---------|-------|-----|-------|
| Q1 (land) | Pro + Aegis pack, 25 seats | $168K | Pilot scoped on incident triage |
| Q2 | Live, day 60 | $168K | Champion active; metric trending |
| Q3 | Live, day 120 | $168K | First QBR; sponsor attends |
| Q4 | Adoption strong | $168K | First case study scoped |
| Year 2 Q1 | Add Vessels pack (sister team) | $228K | Pack expansion, +$60K |
| Year 2 Q2 | Add 25 seats | $264K | Seat expansion, +$36K |
| Year 2 Q4 | Renewal at list + 7%; multi-year discussed | $282K | Renewal motion |
| Year 3 Q2 | Edition upgrade to Enterprise + multi-tenant | $480K | New tenant onboarded; SCIM enabled |
| Year 3 Q4 | Add Terra pack (acquisition team) | $528K | Pack expansion |

ACV trajectory: $168K → $282K → $528K. NRR Year 3: 314%.

This is the model behind the [REVENUE_MODEL.md](REVENUE_MODEL.md) NRR targets.

## Why Land Small, Expand Systematically

### Why land small

- Faster decision cycle for the customer
- Less procurement friction
- Operator champion is more accountable when scope is small
- Failure mode is contained
- Faster time to first proof of value
- Builds CSM relationship without burning trust on a giant scope

### Why expand systematically

- Trigger-based, not opportunistic — easier to forecast
- Each expansion is a small commercial conversation, not a renegotiation
- Customer's procurement team prefers amendments to new MSAs
- CSM bandwidth scales — not every customer needs founder for an expansion
- Keeps net dollar retention compounding

## Pricing Discipline Across the Motion

| Phase | Pricing posture |
|-------|----------------|
| Land (design partner) | 50% off list, locked Year 1 |
| Land (commercial) | List with discount per [PRICING_PACKAGING.md](PRICING_PACKAGING.md) discount authority |
| Adopt | No discount adjustments mid-term |
| Expand (pack) | List with up to 10% AE discretion |
| Expand (seat) | List per increment |
| Expand (edition) | Modular Enterprise pricing per [PLATFORM_EDITIONS.md](PLATFORM_EDITIONS.md); founder approval |
| Renewal | List + 7% escalator default |

We do not give expansion discounts to compensate for renewal escalator pushback. The escalator is real; expansion is real; conflating them erodes both.

## Forecasting Land + Expand

### Land forecast

- Pipeline-based; standard Sales / CRM forecasting
- Based on stage progression in [GO_TO_MARKET_MOTION.md](GO_TO_MARKET_MOTION.md)

### Expansion forecast

- Per [EXPANSION_MOTION.md](EXPANSION_MOTION.md):
  - Seat expansion: data-driven, high confidence
  - Pack expansion: customer-driven, medium confidence
  - Edition upgrade: per-deal, medium-low confidence

### Renewal forecast

- 90 days pre-renewal: forecast at 90% of contract value (default assumption: renewal happens, escalator applies)
- 60 days pre-renewal: refine based on QBR sentiment
- 30 days pre-renewal: confirm or escalate to founder if at risk
