# Privacy Overview — SZL Holdings

## Data We Collect

### Account Data
- Name, email (via authentication provider)
- Organization/tenant association
- Role assignments

### Usage Data
- Page views (path, timestamp, referrer)
- Feature usage events (anonymized)
- Session metadata (user agent, viewport)

### Business Data (Customer-Provided)
- Domain-specific records (properties, vessels, incidents, etc.)
- Documents and files uploaded by users
- Workflow configurations and approval records

### Lead/Marketing Data
- Newsletter subscription (email, source, campaign)
- Contact form submissions
- Lead capture (name, email, company, source)

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- Payment card numbers (handled by Stripe)
- Social security numbers or government IDs
- Biometric data
- Location tracking beyond IP-derived country
- Cross-site tracking or advertising cookies

## Where Data Lives
- **Database**: PostgreSQL hosted on Replit infrastructure (US-based)
- **Files**: Replit Object Storage
- **Analytics**: In-platform tracking (dos_page_views, dos_analytics_events) — no third-party analytics

## Data Retention
- Account data: Retained while account is active
- Usage data: 90-day rolling window
- Business data: Retained per customer agreement
- Lead data: Retained until unsubscribe or deletion request

## Your Rights
- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Request account and data deletion
- **Portability**: Export your data in standard formats
