Most enterprise AI platforms describe their capabilities as features.

We describe ours as structural primitives — and the distinction matters more than it sounds.

A feature can be toggled off. A primitive is load-bearing. Remove it and the structure fails.

Here are the six structural primitives every decision runs through on the SZL Holdings platform:

**Outcome Graph** — the memory layer. Every decision, every action, every outcome becomes a node. Causal relationships become edges. The system compounds learning over time. Without it, you have no mechanism to know whether your AI reasoning is improving.

**Proof Chain** — the immutable audit trail. Every consequential action writes a tamper-evident record: who authorized it, what evidence supported it, what policy governed it, what happened. Not logs. A traversable provenance graph a regulator can inspect.

**Covenant Policy** — the governance layer. Defines which actions are permitted, which require human confirmation, and which are blocked — by action type, risk profile, role, jurisdiction, and organizational policy. Not documentation. Enforcement.

**Decision Simulation** — the what-if engine. Before any action executes, operators see the probability distribution of outcomes. Not "do this." But "here is what happens if you do this — compared to the alternatives."

**Workflow Engine** — the execution layer. Orchestrates the sequence of steps, human approval gates, automated tasks, and integration calls that turn a confirmed decision into a completed action. Every step that touches an external system requires a confirmed human authorization.

**Event Fabric** — the integration layer. Real-time event stream that surfaces signals, routes them to the right reasoning engine, and propagates state changes across the platform as decisions are made.

What makes this defensible: all six run simultaneously, on shared infrastructure, across every domain.

A SOC alert in Aegis. A voyage exception in Vessels. A distress lead in Terra. All six primitives. Every time. The same structural guarantee.
