## Style Reminders

- **Voice**: Professional, calm, thought-provoking. Not editorial. Not promotional. The tone of a founder who has thought carefully about a difficult problem and is sharing the analysis clearly.
- **Specificity**: Every claim is grounded in something concrete — a platform primitive, a scenario, a decision type, a structural constraint. Avoid abstract assertions without grounding.
- **Length**: Thursday essays: 600–900 words. Deep Dives: 900–1,400 words. Operator Lens: 500–700 words.
- **No emoji walls**. No hollow transition phrases ("Additionally," "Furthermore," "In conclusion").
- **Cross-links**: Every issue links to the preceding issue and previews the following one. Build the arc.
