Six primitives. Not six features.

Features can be toggled off. Primitives are load-bearing. Remove one and the structure fails.

Every governed decision on the SZL Holdings platform runs through the same six structural components:

**Outcome Graph** — decision memory. Every action, every outcome. The system compounds learning.

**Proof Chain** — immutable audit trail. Who authorized what, with what evidence, under what policy.

**Covenant Policy** — governance enforcement. Not documentation. A structural gate on every consequential action.

**Decision Simulation** — what-if engine. Probability distributions before commitment, not recommendations without uncertainty.

**Workflow Engine** — execution with human confirmation built in. The same governance loop that generates the recommendation closes the action.

**Event Fabric** — real-time signal routing across all domains simultaneously.

All six run simultaneously. A SOC alert in Aegis, a voyage exception in Vessels, a distress lead in Terra. Same primitives. Same governance guarantee. Every time.
