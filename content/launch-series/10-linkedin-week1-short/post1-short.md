The accountability gap is the next enterprise problem.

Every technology wave follows the same pattern: adoption first, consequences second.

The internet made information abundant before it made misinformation legible. The cloud made infrastructure elastic before it made governance tractable. AI is doing the same thing — faster, across more decision types, with more consequential outputs.

The gap emerges when AI-generated recommendations outpace the organizational structures designed to govern them. The result: more data, more noise, more decisions running in parallel — no attribution, no audit trail, no closure mechanism.

SZL Holdings builds the infrastructure layer between signal detection and action execution that enforces governance, attribution, and outcome tracking on every consequential decision.

Not a dashboard. A governed decision surface.
