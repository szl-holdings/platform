Six primitives, not features.

Most software companies describe what their product does. SZL Holdings is built on what the platform enforces — structurally, at every layer, regardless of which domain it's applied to.

Those enforcements are six primitives. Not features. The architectural spine.

• Outcome Graph — tracks every decision lifecycle (recommendation → decision → outcome) so AI agents calibrate against what actually happened.

• Proof Chain — immutable, append-only audit trail with provenance. AI outputs carry model identity and source citations. Human actions carry actor attribution. A verifiable chain of custody.

• Covenant Policy — approval gates enforced at the platform layer, not the UI. An AI agent cannot bypass a human-in-the-loop requirement structurally.

• Decision Simulation — probabilistic modeling before action. Operators see confidence intervals and scenario distributions, not point estimates.

• Workflow Engine — durable multi-step orchestration with checkpoint recovery and AI/human handoffs as first-class events.

• Event Fabric — cross-domain signal backbone. A sanctions hit in Vessels surfaces a legal flag in Counsel automatically.

The same six primitives power a SOC alert in Aegis, a voyage P&L call in Vessels, a listing decision in Terra, and a matter triage in Counsel. Different domains, same spine. That's what the one-platform claim actually means at the code level.
