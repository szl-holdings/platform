# Vessels Primer — Maritime Command

## What Is Vessels?
Vessels is SZL's maritime operating intelligence platform. It provides fleet command, voyage economics, compliance monitoring, route risk assessment, and exception management for maritime operators.

## Key Capabilities

### Fleet Map
Interactive map with real-time vessel positions, status indicators, and click-to-detail navigation.

### Vessel Detail
Comprehensive vessel profiles with specifications, voyage history, maintenance schedules, and compliance status.

### Voyage Economics
Revenue, cost, and profitability analysis per voyage. Benchmark comparisons and trend analysis.

### Exception Center
Flagged issues by severity with assignment and resolution workflows. Historical exception analysis.

### Route Risk
Risk assessment for planned routes including weather, sanctions, port security, and compliance factors.

### Sanctions Screening
Vessel and counterparty screening against sanctions lists.

## Who Uses Vessels
- Fleet managers
- Operations directors
- Ship owners
- Chartering teams
- Compliance officers
- Port agents

## Data Sources
- AIS feed (planned)
- Port databases
- Sanctions lists
- Weather services (planned)
