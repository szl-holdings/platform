# Terra Primer — Real Estate Intelligence

## What Is Terra?
Terra is a real estate operating intelligence platform. It helps investors, operators, and deal teams find opportunities, evaluate risk, manage portfolios, and execute transactions with data-driven precision.

## Key Capabilities

### Distress Detection
Terra's AI scans public and proprietary data sources for signals of property distress — financial stress, ownership changes, deferred maintenance, regulatory actions. Alerts are prioritized and contextualized.

### Portfolio Intelligence
See your entire portfolio at a glance. Track occupancy, rent rolls, maintenance schedules, insurance status, and financial performance across all assets.

### Deal Pipeline
Move from opportunity to close with a structured deal pipeline. Track diligence, approvals, inspections, and close conditions in one place.

### Market Intelligence
Comparable sales, market trends, demographic shifts, economic indicators — Terra aggregates market data from Census, HUD, FEMA, and other sources.

### Document Engine
Generate lender packages, diligence reports, market analyses, and investor updates with structured templates.

## Data Sources
- Census.gov — Housing, demographics, economics
- HUD — Fair market rents, housing data
- FEMA — Disaster declarations, risk zones
- County records — Property data (planned)
- MLS — Listings (planned)

## Who Uses Terra
- Real estate investors (institutional and private)
- Property managers
- Deal teams and analysts
- Lenders and underwriters
- Asset managers
