# Business Observability — What It Is and Why It Matters

## The Problem
Most organizations operate with fragmented visibility. Critical signals live in separate tools, departments, and dashboards. Decisions are made with incomplete information. Problems are discovered too late.

## What Is Business Observability?
Business Observability is the ability to understand what's happening across your entire organization — in real time, with context, and with the ability to act.

It's not just monitoring. Monitoring tells you *what* happened. Observability tells you *why* it happened, *who* needs to know, and *what to do about it*.

## How SZL Approaches It

### Signals, Not Alerts
Traditional tools fire alerts. SZL generates signals — contextual, prioritized, evidence-backed. A signal includes:
- What happened
- Why it matters
- Who should act
- What the evidence says
- What the recommended action is

### Evidence, Not Opinions
Every AI recommendation in SZL is backed by retrieved evidence. The system doesn't guess — it retrieves, ranks, and presents evidence for human review.

### Governance, Not Automation
SZL doesn't auto-execute. Every significant action goes through policy gates and human-in-the-loop approval. The system proposes; humans decide.

### Audit, Not Trust
Every decision, action, and approval is permanently logged. The audit trail is immutable. You can prove what happened, when, and why.
