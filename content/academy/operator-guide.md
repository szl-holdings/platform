# Operator Guide — SZL Platform

## Your Daily Workflow

### 1. Start Your Shift
- Sign in and review your dashboard
- Check pending items in your queue
- Review any escalations from previous shift

### 2. Triage
- Process items by priority (Critical → High → Medium → Low)
- Assign owners to unassigned items
- Escalate anything beyond your authority
- Update status as you work

### 3. Execute
- Follow established workflows for each task
- Use the approval queue for actions requiring review
- Document your decisions and rationale
- Log any exceptions or unusual findings

### 4. Monitor
- Watch for new signals and alerts
- Track items you've assigned to others
- Follow up on pending items approaching deadlines

### 5. Handoff
- Review open items
- Update notes for the next operator
- Escalate anything unresolved

## Best Practices
- Always update status — don't leave items in "in progress" overnight
- Document your reasoning — the audit trail is your proof
- Escalate early — it's better to flag something too early than too late
- Use the help center — answers to common questions are already documented
- Report issues — if something is broken or confusing, report it

## Common Tools
- **Signal Queue**: Prioritized work items
- **Approval Center**: Items waiting for review
- **Audit Log**: Full history of all actions
- **Search**: Find records, people, and documents
- **Export**: Generate reports and data exports
