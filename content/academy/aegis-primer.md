# Aegis Primer — Defense & Intelligence

## What Is Aegis?
Aegis is SZL's unified defense and intelligence command center. It provides security operations center (SOC) capabilities, threat intelligence, incident response, vulnerability management, and enterprise governance.

## Key Capabilities

### Command Home
Unified security posture overview. Active incidents, alert severity distribution, threat level, and compliance status.

### Investigations
Structured incident investigation with timeline, evidence, and assignment workflows.

### Decision Console
AI-assisted security decisions with evidence retrieval, policy checks, and human approval gates.

### XDR (Extended Detection & Response)
Kill chain analysis, threat hunting workspace, correlated alerts across domains.

### MITRE ATT&CK Mapping
Threat framework alignment. Map incidents and threats to MITRE ATT&CK techniques.

### Governance Suite
Enterprise governance, trust analytics, compliance monitoring, executive reporting.

## Who Uses Aegis
- Security operations teams
- Incident response teams
- CISO and security leadership
- Compliance officers
- Risk managers

## What Makes Aegis Different
- AI proposes, humans decide (no autonomous security actions)
- Evidence-backed recommendations
- Immutable audit trail for compliance
- Integrated governance (not just detection)
- Simulation capabilities for training and planning
