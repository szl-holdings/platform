# Carlota Jo — Help Guide

## What is Carlota Jo?
Carlota Jo is the premium advisory services brand for high-net-worth clients. It provides discreet, personalized consulting with a focus on trust and confidentiality.

## Key Features
- **Service Overview**: Detailed service catalog
- **Booking Flow**: Secure consultation scheduling
- **Client Portal**: Private space for documents, messages, and updates
- **Inquiry System**: Structured intake for new client requests

## Common Workflows

### Booking a Consultation
1. Visit /book
2. Select service type
3. Choose available time slot
4. Provide contact details
5. Confirm booking
6. Receive confirmation at /booking/success

### Using the Client Portal
1. Sign in to access /client-portal
2. View your documents at /client-portal/documents
3. Check updates at /client-portal/updates
4. Send messages at /client-portal/messages
5. Manage preferences at /client-portal/settings

### Submitting an Inquiry
1. Visit /contact or /inquiries
2. Fill in the inquiry form
3. Select service area and urgency
4. Submit — you'll receive a response within 24 hours

## Troubleshooting
- **Booking not available**: Check available time slots, try a different date
- **Portal access denied**: Verify your account is active, contact support
- **Messages not sending**: Check network connection, try again
