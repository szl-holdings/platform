# General Feedback — SZL Holdings

## We Want to Hear From You

Your feedback helps us build a better platform. Whether it's a compliment, critique, suggestion, or observation, we value your input.

## How to Share Feedback

### Quick Feedback
Email: feedback@szlholdings.com

### Structured Feedback
Visit /contact and select "Feedback" as the topic.

### What We're Interested In
- **Usability**: Is the platform easy to use? What's confusing?
- **Performance**: Is anything slow or unresponsive?
- **Design**: Do the visuals and layout work for you?
- **Content**: Is our documentation helpful? What's missing?
- **Workflows**: Do the workflows match how you actually work?
- **Value**: Is the platform solving your real problems?

## How We Use Feedback
1. All feedback is logged and tagged by product area
2. Feedback is reviewed in weekly product reviews
3. Patterns and themes inform roadmap priorities
4. Significant feedback is acknowledged directly
5. Changes driven by feedback are noted in release notes

## Feedback Privacy
- Your feedback is confidential
- We may quote feedback anonymously in internal reviews
- We will never share your identity or details externally without permission
