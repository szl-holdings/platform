# Contact Support — SZL Holdings

## Support Channels

| Channel | Use For | Response Time |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| support@szlholdings.com | General support, account issues | Within 24 hours |
| security@szlholdings.com | Security concerns, vulnerability reports | Within 4 hours |
| feedback@szlholdings.com | Feature requests, product feedback | Within 48 hours |
| /contact page | General inquiries, demo requests | Within 24 hours |

## What to Include in Your Request
1. **Subject**: Clear description of the issue
2. **Product**: Which product (Lyte, Terra, Vessels, Aegis, Carlota Jo)
3. **Steps to reproduce**: What you did before the issue occurred
4. **Expected behavior**: What should have happened
5. **Actual behavior**: What actually happened
6. **Browser/device**: Chrome, Safari, mobile, etc.
7. **Screenshots**: If applicable

| Severity | Description | Expected Response |

| High | Major feature broken, no workaround | Within 4 hours |
| Medium | Feature degraded, workaround exists | Within 24 hours |
| Low | Minor issue, cosmetic, enhancement | Within 48 hours |
