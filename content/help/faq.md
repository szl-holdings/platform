# FAQ — Help Center

**How do I reset my password?**
Password management is handled by the authentication provider. Use the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in page.

**How do I change my role or permissions?**
Contact your organization administrator or email support@szlholdings.com.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. Most dashboards have export options for CSV or PDF. Use the Document Engine for formatted reports.

**Is there a mobile app?**
Yes. Every product has a companion mobile app built with Expo (React Native).

**How do I request a new feature?**
Visit [Request a Feature](./request-a-feature.md) or email feedback@szlholdings.com.

**How do I report a bug?**
Visit [Report a Bug](./report-a-bug.md) or email support@szlholdings.com.

**What browsers are supported?**
Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). We recommend Chrome for the best experience.

**Is my data backed up?**
Yes. The database is backed up automatically. See our [Business Continuity](/trust) documentation.

**How do I contact support?**
Email support@szlholdings.com or visit [Contact](./contact.md).
