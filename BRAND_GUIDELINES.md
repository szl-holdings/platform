# Brand Guidelines — SZL Holdings Platform

> Visual identity, voice, and brand standards for the SZL Holdings ecosystem.

---

## Brand Identity

### Company Name

- **Correct:** SZL Holdings
- **Incorrect:** SZL holdings, szl holdings, S.Z.L. Holdings, SZL-Holdings

Product names:
- **Lyte** (not LYTE, Lyte Command Center in full)
- **Alloy** (not ALLOY, Alloy Fabric in full)
- **Aegis** (not AEGIS)
- **Vessels** (not VESSELS)
- **Terra** (not TERRA)
- **PRISM Counsel** (PRISM is an acronym — always all-caps)
- **Carlota Jo** (two words, capital J)

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline:** "Governed Operational Intelligence"

**Brand promise:** Enterprise operators get clarity on what is happening, what to do next, who is responsible, and whether the action is safe — all in one accountable surface.

**Voice:**
- Direct. No filler language.
- Confident without being arrogant.
- Technical when speaking to technical audiences; operational when speaking to operators.
- Never sensationalist about AI.

**Tone:**
- Professional and precise
- Outcome-oriented (what it does, not what it is)
- Minimal jargon — when jargon is used, it is explained

## Logo Usage

The SZL Holdings wordmark and symbol are proprietary. When using logos:

- Maintain clear space equal to the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides
- Do not stretch, rotate, or skew the logo
- Do not apply drop shadows or other effects
- Preferred: full-color on dark background (primary brand environment)
- Acceptable: white/light on dark; dark on light (for specific contexts)
- Unacceptable: grayscale on grayscale (insufficient contrast)

For logo files, contact [inquiries@szlholdings.com](mailto:inquiries@szlholdings.com).

## Writing Style

### Headlines
- Sentence case for headers (not Title Case for every word)
- Outcome-first: "Understand your risk" not "Risk Understanding Feature"
- Action-oriented where possible

### Body Copy
- Active voice preferred
- Short paragraphs (3–4 sentences max per paragraph for web)
- Lead with the benefit, follow with the mechanism
- Avoid: "leverage," "synergize," "revolutionize," "disrupt"

### CTAs
- Clear and direct: "Request a Demo," "Explore Lyte," "Get Access"
- Avoid vague CTAs: "Learn More," "Click Here"
- Each CTA should specify the product/action it leads to

## Product Description Templates

### One-liner format
`[Product] is the [category] for [audience] that [core benefit].`

Example: *Lyte is the business observability command surface for enterprise operators that connects signals to accountable action.*

### Feature description format
`[Feature name] — [What it does]. [Why it matters].`

Example: *Signal Timeline — A chronological feed of all correlated business signals with attribution. See what changed, when, and what triggered it.*

---

## Don't

- Don't compare SZL Holdings negatively to specific named competitors
- Don't promise features that don't exist (use "planned" or "roadmap" language)
- Don't use stock imagery of people in marketing materials (illustration and UI screenshots preferred)
- Don't describe the platform as "AI-powered" without noting the governance model — this creates trust risk
- Don't claim enterprise certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001) that haven't been achieved
