## Purpose

A single one-page scorecard reviewed by the founder weekly and presented to the board monthly. It is the canonical view of company health across product, commercial, financial, and people axes.

The North Star is the headline. This scorecard is everything else needed to interpret the North Star.

## Color Coding Discipline

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 🟢 Green | On or ahead of target |
| 🟡 Yellow | Behind target but recoverable; corrective plan in place |
| 🔴 Red | Significantly off; founder intervention required |

A 🔴 anywhere on the scorecard surfaces in the founder's Monday review and triggers a written response within 7 days.

## Review Cadence

| Cadence | Activity | Owner |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Monday morning | Founder reviews scorecard; identifies any reds | Founder |
| Monday + Tuesday | Founder addresses any reds | Founder + relevant lead |
| Friday | Scorecard updated for the week | CSM lead + Finance |
| Last Friday of month | Monthly board update prepared from scorecard | Founder + Finance |
| Last Friday of quarter | Quarterly investor update prepared from scorecard + commentary | Founder |

---

## Scorecard Discipline

| Practice | Why |
|----------|-----|
| One page only | Forces prioritization |
| Same metrics every week | Comparable; not editorial |
| Numbers from systems of record (CRM, billing, product analytics) | Not founder estimates |
| 🟢/🟡/🔴 on every cell | No ambiguity |
| Targets locked at quarter start | Prevents goalpost-moving |
| Trend column on every metric | Direction matters as much as value |
| Comments only when status changes | Reduces editorial bloat |

---

## Annual Review

At year-end, the scorecard is reviewed against:

- Did the metrics chosen actually predict business health?
- Were any metrics gamed or misleading?
- Should any be replaced or added for next year?

The board signs off on next year's scorecard at the Q4 board meeting.

---

## Common Scorecard Failures

| Failure | Why we avoid |
|---------|--------------|
| Adding a metric mid-quarter without board sign-off | Drift; loses comparability |
| Removing a yellow metric instead of fixing it | Hides reality |
| Founder commentary that contradicts the colors | Erodes trust in the scorecard |
| Using the scorecard for compensation | Gaming risk |
| Trying to make every quadrant green by adjusting thresholds | Defeats the purpose |
