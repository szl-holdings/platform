## Core Competency

SZL Holdings builds **governed operational-intelligence platforms** — software that bridges data ingestion, risk analysis, AI-supported decision-making, execution, and auditability into a single continuous workflow. Every action is logged, every decision is provenance-bound, every gated approval is named.

## Differentiators

- **Open-source runtime in production.** `@szl-holdings/ouroboros` v6.2.0, full test suite, dependabot, CodeQL.
- **DOI-pinned theoretical foundation, audited by its successor paper.** Three peer-style papers: v3 (`10.5281/zenodo.19944926`), v9 (Lutar Invariant Family, 17 pp, May 5 2026), v10 (Audit Closure Operator Λ₁₀, 11 pp + Appendix A, May 5 2026). v10 certifies that every formula in v9 executes against the live shipping repo.
- **Proof-chain by construction.** Every output carries a hash chain back to its inputs and the approver who released it.
- **CPS — Covenant Proof Standard.** A first-class API for executing, approving, and rolling back governed cross-vertical workflows with auditable receipts.
- **Live agent gateway with OPA enforcement.** Every agent action passes through a runtime policy boundary.
- **Self-correcting publishing record.** The May 2 thesis re-release publicly retracted and replaced an earlier version after a self-audit. The v9 → v10 sequence continues that discipline.
