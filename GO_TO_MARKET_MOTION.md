## Strategic Position

We are creating the category of **governed decision infrastructure**. Buyers do not have a budget line for it. They have budget for "security tooling," "deal pipeline," "fleet ops," "matter management." Our motion converts those existing budget lines into platform spend by being structurally different from the alternatives.

The motion is founder-led, design-partner-anchored, and operator-validated. It is not built on top-of-funnel volume.

## The Three-Year Motion Arc

| Year | Phase | Goal | Motion |
|------|-------|------|--------|
| 2026 | Design partner | 6 named partners across 3 domains | Founder-led, direct outreach, no SDRs |
| 2027 | Founder + AE | 30 paying customers | Founder + 2 AEs; design partners as references |
| 2028 | Repeatable sales | 80–120 paying customers | Sales team of 6–8; segmented by domain pack |

We do not skip the design partner year. The wedge is conviction, not coverage.

## Motion Stack

### Tier 1: Founder-Led (2026, primary motion)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Audience | Operator champions in 50–500 person organizations |
| Channels | Founder's network, warm intro through advisors, targeted thought leadership |
| First touch | Direct LinkedIn or email from founder |
| Demo | Personally given by founder (executive demo) |
| Close | MSA + design partner agreement signed by founder |
| Cycle time | 30–60 days from first touch to signature |

### Tier 2: Founder + AE (2027, primary motion)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Audience | Operator + technical buyer in mid-market regulated organizations |
| Channels | AE prospecting + founder warm intros + design partner referrals |
| First touch | AE outreach (warm or cold) |
| Demo | Operator demo by AE; technical demo by founder if needed |
| Close | AE-led with founder backstop on first 5 deals |
| Cycle time | 60–120 days |

### Tier 3: Repeatable Sales (2028, primary motion)

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Audience | Mid-market and select enterprise across all 5 packs |
| Channels | Outbound, inbound, partner referrals, content-driven |
| First touch | SDR or marketing-qualified inbound |
| Demo | AE-led; founder for strategic deals only |
| Close | AE-led; deal desk for Enterprise |
| Cycle time | 90–180 days |

## Buyer Journey

| Stage | Customer state | Our move | Surface |
|-------|---------------|----------|---------|
| Aware | Sees a piece of content or hears of us | Capture lead via contact form | `/contact`, `/pricing` |
| Engaged | Books an executive demo | Founder runs executive demo | Live call |
| Evaluating | Wants operator demo + technical demo | CSM and founder co-deliver | Live call |
| Diligence | Reads diligence packet, opens Trust Center | We answer follow-ups within 24 hours | Email + Slack |
| Decision | Picks a path: design partner / PoV / commercial | We scope the path on a one-pager | Live call + doc |
| Active | Pilot or full commercial deployment | CSM owns the relationship per [PILOT_PLAYBOOK.md](PILOT_PLAYBOOK.md) | Live + product |
| Expansion | Adds packs, seats, tenants | Trigger from Lyte usage data + CSM cadence | Live + product |
| Reference | Speaks with our prospects | Founder + marketing coordinate | Live |

## Channels and Partnerships

### Direct (primary)

Founder + AE motion as described above. ~80% of pipeline through 2027.

### Partner referrals (secondary)

Design partners refer new customers; we credit them with 1 month of platform fee per converted referral.

### Industry partners (selective)

| Partner type | Relationship |
|--------------|--------------|
| Advisory firms (security, maritime, real estate) | Implementation referrals |
| Auditors / compliance firms | Diligence + audit packet co-marketing |
| Identity providers (Okta, Azure AD) | Integration partnership |
| Cloud providers (Azure) | Marketplace presence at GA |

### Channel partners (FY27+)

We do not engage VARs / resellers in 2026. Evaluate post-revenue.

## Category Positioning Discipline

We do not say:

- "We compete with [Datadog / Splunk / Palantir]" — we do not
- "We are an AI tool" — we are governance for AI
- "We're like [open source project] but enterprise" — we are net-new infrastructure
- "We do everything" — we do governed decisions across our 5 domain packs

We say:

- "We build governed decision infrastructure"
- "Six primitives, one canonical loop, every domain pack"
- "We replace the gap between dashboards and AI copilots"
- "Operator-tier governance, not nation-state-tier governance"

See [CATEGORY_POSITIONING.md](CATEGORY_POSITIONING.md) and [SALES_NARRATIVE.md](SALES_NARRATIVE.md).

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Founder bandwidth bottleneck | Document the demo and the close motion early; train the first AE in shadow before they own deals |
| Long enterprise cycles delay 2027 ARR | Focus 2026 design partners on mid-market with operator-led deals to compress cycles |
| Category confusion | Repeat the same words: "governed decision infrastructure"; refuse to be classified as a SOC tool |
| Competitive entry by hyperscaler / Palantir | Lean into operator-tier positioning; price below their floor; ship faster |
| Discount drift | Publish discount authority; report discount average monthly |
