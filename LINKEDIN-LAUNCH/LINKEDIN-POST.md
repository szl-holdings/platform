For the past 18 months I've been building something I couldn't find anywhere else:

A Governed Decision Operating System.

Not a dashboard. Not a copilot. Not a wrapper around someone else's model.

The structural layer that sits between signal and action — with governance, attribution, and outcome tracking on every decision that matters.

Nine surfaces. One operating system. Six domain packs:

→ Vessels — maritime intelligence and counterparty risk
→ Terra — real estate and asset intelligence

→ The Decision Fabric — Constellation graph + Trace Graph + Guardian + Eval OS + Memory Fabric + Tool Mesh underneath all of it

Why this, why now:

Every serious enterprise is racing to deploy AI inside high-consequence workflows — finance, legal, defense, real estate, maritime, ops. And every serious enterprise is hitting the same wall:

❌ The AI gives an answer. Nobody can explain why.
❌ The AI takes an action. Nobody can prove it was authorized.
❌ The AI changes its mind next week. Nobody can replay the original run.
❌ The auditor asks for evidence. There isn't any.

We solved that.

Every recommendation in SZL carries provenance, freshness, confidence, citations, a trace ID, and a policy decision.
