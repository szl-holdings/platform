## Posting steps (desktop browser, ~10 minutes)

### Step 1 — Start the post

1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com/feed/
2. At the top of the feed, click **"Start a post"**.
3. In the post composer, click the **photo / image icon** (bottom-left of the composer toolbar).

### Step 2 — Upload the 10 carousel images IN ORDER

1. In the file picker, navigate to the `images/` folder from this zip.
2. Select **all 10 images at once** (⌘-A on Mac, Ctrl-A on Windows). They are pre-numbered `01` through `10` so LinkedIn will respect the order.
3. Click **Open**. LinkedIn will show all 10 thumbnails in the order they're numbered.
4. **Verify the order** in the preview panel. If any slide is out of place, drag it back to the right spot. Slide 1 (`01-cover-szl-holdings.jpg`) must be first — it's the thumbnail people see in the feed.
5. Optional but recommended: click each image once and add **alt text** (the captions are listed in the table inside `LINKEDIN-POST.md`). Alt text helps accessibility and search.
6. Click **Done**.

### Step 3 — Paste the post body

1. Open `LINKEDIN-POST.md` in any text editor.
2. Copy **everything between the two ``` fences** under "POST BODY" — including the line breaks.
3. Paste it into the LinkedIn post composer (above where the carousel preview is).
4. **Check the hashtags at the bottom** — LinkedIn auto-formats them blue. If any look wrong, retype them so they go blue.

### Step 4 — Set audience and post

1. At the top of the composer, make sure the audience dropdown says **"Anyone"** (public, not Connections-only — you want reach).
2. Click **Post** (bottom-right, blue button).
3. Wait ~10 seconds for LinkedIn to confirm the post has gone live.

### Step 5 — Drop the reply-to-self (THIS IS IMPORTANT)

LinkedIn's algorithm boosts posts that get an early comment from the author. Do this within **5 minutes** of posting.

1. Find your post in the feed (or click your profile → Posts).
2. Click the **Comment** icon under the post.
3. Open `LINKEDIN-POST.md` again, scroll to the bottom under "RECOMMENDED REPLY-TO-SELF", and copy everything between the ``` fences.
4. Paste it as a comment on your own post.
5. Click **Post**.

## First-hour checklist (after publishing)

- [ ] **Reply to every comment within 60 minutes.** Even just "Thanks — DM me if you want a closer look." LinkedIn weights early engagement heavily.

- [ ] **Send the post link to 5–10 people in DMs** with a one-line ask: "Built the v1 of this — would value your eyes on it." Personal asks drive 80% of early reach.
- [ ] If anyone asks for the technical detail, reply "Comment 'doctrine' and I'll send it" — this triggers your reply-to-self loop.

## Troubleshooting

**LinkedIn rejects the upload / says "image too large"**
The carousel images are all under 100 KB so this shouldn't happen. If it does, try uploading in two batches (1–5, then drag 6–10 in).

**The carousel order looks wrong after upload**
Drag the thumbnails in the preview panel until the order matches the file numbering (01 → 10). The cover image (slide 1) is what shows in the feed thumbnail.

**The post body looks like one wall of text**
LinkedIn collapses double line breaks into single ones in some browsers. After pasting, manually press **Enter twice** between each paragraph to restore the spacing.

**Hashtags aren't going blue**
Delete the `#` and retype it. LinkedIn only auto-detects hashtags that you type fresh into the composer.

**You want to add a video**
A 60-second product montage was originally produced for this launch but isn't included in this zip. If you want to add a video, you'd post it as a **second standalone post** the next day rather than attaching it to this carousel — LinkedIn doesn't allow videos and image carousels in the same post.
