`@terra_re` subs) only works if the affiliate badge stays visible. Rule of
thumb: **one cross-post per week in each direction.**

## Bookkeeping

- Log the cross-post in both content calendars.
- Re-balance cadence after 4 weeks based on which direction performed better.

## What *not* to do

- **Don't** mirror identical text across accounts on the same day.
- **Don't** drop "× SZL HOLDINGS" / "By @szlholdings" from the subaccount
  bio — that's what makes the cross-promote legible cold.
