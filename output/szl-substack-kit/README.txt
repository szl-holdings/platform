SZL HOLDINGS — SUBSTACK KIT
================================

This kit contains everything needed to set up the Substack profile and start
publishing immediately. Two source documents:

  1) SZL-Substack-Profile.docx       — profile setup (identity, bio, settings, cover, welcome post)
  2) SZL-Substack-Post-Templates.docx — 5 ready-to-publish post templates with a publishing rhythm

PROFILE SETUP — use SZL-Substack-Profile.docx
---------------------------------------------
 1. Open SZL-Substack-Profile.docx in Word or Google Docs.
 2. Go to substack.com → your publication → Settings → Edit Profile.
 3. Copy Name, Handle, Tagline, and Website from Section 1 into Substack.
 4. Paste your chosen Bio from Section 2 into the About field.
 5. Enter each Social Link from Section 3; leave flagged fields blank.
 6. Apply the recommended Settings and privacy toggles from Section 4.
 7. Upload cover-image-1600x533.png as your cover image (Section 5 guidance).
 8. Set your accent colour to #D4A054 in Substack's theme customiser.
 9. Create your first post: paste the Welcome Post from Section 6, pin it.
10. Reference profile-mockup-desktop.png and profile-mockup-mobile.png to preview your finished profile before going live.

PUBLISHING — use SZL-Substack-Post-Templates.docx
-------------------------------------------------
After the welcome post, switch to the templates document for every subsequent
post. It contains five reusable formats:

  Template 1 — The Signal (weekly intelligence digest, every Monday)
  Template 2 — Founder Note (essay format, bi-weekly target)
  Template 3 — Build Log (product update, on every meaningful release)
  Template 4 — Field Report (monthly decision teardown / post-mortem)
  Template 5 — On the Desk (monthly curated reading & watchlist)

Each template includes the headline formula, subtitle line, full section
structure (hook → body → close → CTA), bracketed placeholder guidance for
every section, and the SZL voice and brand rails. The "Publishing Rhythm —
A Default Month" section at the end of the document composes the templates
into a sustainable 8–10 posts per month without writer's block.
