# Vessels ↔ SZL Holdings — Cross-promote plan

The subaccount split (`@szlholdings` parent + `@aegis_soc`, `@vessels_maritime`,
`@terra_re` subs) only works if the affiliate badge stays visible. Rule of
thumb: **one cross-post per week in each direction.**

## Weekly cadence

| Week day | From → To | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Tue | `@szlholdings` → quote-reposts the Vessels announcement | Quote-repost (within 60 min of Vessels post #1) |
| Wed | `@vessels_maritime` → quote-reposts an `@szlholdings` doctrine post | Quote-repost with one operator-voice line |
| Fri | `@szlholdings` → spotlight reply on the Vessels voyage-P&L post | Reply, not repost |
| Mon | `@vessels_maritime` → quotes the SZL "decision can't be replayed" principle | Quote-repost framed for fleet operators |

## Bookkeeping

- Log the cross-post in both content calendars.
- Re-balance cadence after 4 weeks based on which direction performed better.

## What *not* to do

- **Don't** mirror identical text across accounts on the same day.
- **Don't** drop "× SZL HOLDINGS" / "By @szlholdings" from the subaccount
  bio — that's what makes the cross-promote legible cold.
