## Pricing Philosophy

1. **Annual contracts are the default.** Monthly is offered only at Starter, with a 25% premium.
2. **Per-tenant base + per-seat scaling.** Platform fee is per tenant; seats scale within the tenant.
3. **Domain packs are additive.** Each pack carries its own annual fee on top of the platform fee.
4. **Governance is included, never up-sold.** Proof Chain, Covenant Policy, and the Outcome Graph are part of every edition.
5. **List pricing is published at GA.** Discounting policy is documented and approval-gated; see "Discount Authority" below.
6. **Design partners get founder pricing.** Up to 50% off list for the first 12 months in exchange for case study, reference, and structured feedback.

## Discount Authority

| Discount | Authority | Documentation |
|---------:|-----------|---------------|
| 0–10% | AE | Recorded in CRM |
| 11–25% | Sales lead | CRM + deal desk note |
| 26–40% | Founder | Written approval on deal record |
| 41–50% | Founder + finance | Required for design partners |
| > 50% | Founder + board (when applicable) | Exception; documented rationale |

Standard renewal escalator: 7% per year unless otherwise negotiated. Multi-year contracts may lock pricing for the term.

## Renewal & Expansion Triggers

Customer events that trigger an expansion conversation:

| Trigger | Expansion play |
|---------|---------------|
| Approaching seat cap | Seat increment add-on |
| Approaching tenant cap (Pro hitting 1-tenant ceiling) | Edition upgrade Pro → Enterprise |
| New domain pack request from operator | Pack add-on |
| Compliance requirement (extended retention) | Retention add-on |
| Mobile usage > 70% of seats | Already included; no upsell |
| Custom AI model request | Enterprise add-on |

See [LAND_AND_EXPAND.md](LAND_AND_EXPAND.md) for the full expansion motion.

## Pricing Governance

Changes to indicative or list pricing require:

1. Founder approval
2. Update to this document and `PRODUCT_PACKAGING.md`
3. Notification window for design partners and existing customers (60 days minimum)
4. Update to the `/pricing` page

Customer-specific exceptions (one-off discount terms, custom payment terms) do not change list pricing — they are recorded on the deal record and never communicated as "the price."
