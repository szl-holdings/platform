Investor context — read this first

What Khipu is, and what it is not

Plain language for diligence. We mark every claim by its maturity. No green checkmarks where a proof does not exist.

It IS a BFT design

Khipu is a Byzantine-fault-tolerant agreement layer: a fact becomes a knot only when an independent quorum of witnesses attests and signs. The intent is safety under up to f faulty witnesses in a 3f+1 set.

It is NOT proven

Khipu's BFT safety and liveness are Conjecture 2 — a proposed property under active research. It is not a formally verified theorem and is not in the locked, proven set. We will never label it "proven."

Receipts you can re-check

Each agreement emits a signed receipt (DSSE / in-toto). Where a signing key is present, receipts are cryptographically verifiable; where not, they are honestly marked unsigned — never falsely "verified."

Honest by construction

The constellation reads a live probe. When the live source is unreachable it degrades to a clearly-labeled snapshot — never to fabricated data and never to a false "all green."

The knot lifecycle

How a fact becomes a knot

The khipu metaphor is literal: a record is only tied once the witnesses agree.

1

Propose

A candidate fact (a build result, a verification, a decision) is broadcast to the witness set.

2

Attest

Independent witnesses re-derive and attest. Sovereign (own-metal) witnesses carry the highest trust weight.

3

Quorum

When a 3-of-4 style quorum agrees, the fact crosses the BFT threshold. Toggle the "3-of-4 quorum" view above to see this subset.

4

Knot & sign

The agreed fact is tied as a knot and a signed receipt is emitted — independently re-verifiable.
