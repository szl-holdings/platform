### 1.1 Esther Wojcicki — Biography & TRICK Framework

**VERIFIED**

Esther Denise "Woj" Hochman Wojcicki (born 1940/1941, New York City) is an American journalist, educator, and Vice Chair of the Creative Commons advisory council. She is the daughter of Russian Jewish immigrants, was valedictorian of her high school class, and was the first in her family to attend college. ([Wikipedia — Esther Wojcicki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Wojcicki))

**Education:** BA (English and Political Science) + MJ (Journalism) from UC Berkeley; MA (French and French History) from Paris-Sorbonne University; MA (Educational Technology) + secondary admin credential from San Jose State University.

**Career highlights:**
- Taught journalism and English at **Palo Alto High School (Paly) 1984–2020**, building the Media Arts Program from 20 students to 600+ students and nine award-winning publications — one of the largest high school journalism programs in the United States. ([Moonshots in Education](http://www.moonshotsedu.com))
- 1990: Northern California Journalism Teacher of the Year
- 2002: California Teacher of the Year (CA Commission on Teacher Credentialing)
- 2009: Columbia Scholastic Press Association Gold Key
- **Honorary doctorates:** Palo Alto University (2013), Rhode Island School of Design (2016)
- Founded **Journalistic Learning Initiative**, University of Oregon (2016)
- Founded **Global Moonshots in Education (MiE) movement** (2017)
- Co-founded **Tract** (Tract.app) — a peer-to-peer online learning platform for ages 8+ that operationalizes her TRICK teaching philosophy ([Adam Mendler interview](https://www.adammendler.com/blog/esther-wojcicki-co-founder-tract/))
- Co-founded **WOJ IT** at UC Berkeley School of Engineering — tech skills for college students
- Chief Learning Officer, **Explore Planet3** (middle school exploration science platform)
- Created **ParentingTRICK** app (iOS and Android)
- Board member: Newseum (DC), Freedom Forum, Alliance for Excellent Education, Embarcadero Media Foundation, Developmental Studies Center
- Vice Chair, **Creative Commons**

**Notable mentee:** Steve Jobs' daughter **Lisa Brennan-Jobs** — Esther mentored her at Paly ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esther_Wojcicki))

**The TRICK Framework:**  
Esther's signature pedagogical and parenting framework. TRICK = **T**rust, **R**espect, **I**ndependence, **C**ollaboration, **K**indness. ([Goodreads — How to Raise Successful People](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/40796147-how-to-raise-successful-people)) The framework encodes a deny-by-default posture toward fear and control: *Trust* is earned and extended deliberately, not granted unconditionally; *Independence* is scaffolded, not abandoned; *Collaboration* is the method, not the exception.

### 1.8 SZL Mapping: TRICK → SZL Trust Doctrine

**[ANALYTICAL — VERIFIED FOUNDATION, SZL MAPPING IS INTERPRETIVE]**

The TRICK framework maps with high fidelity onto SZL's trust architecture:

| TRICK Element | Esther's Meaning | SZL Doctrine Analog |
|---|---|---|
| **T — Trust** | "Trust is not assumed; it is earned through demonstrated behavior over time. You can give someone trust while still monitoring outcomes." | SZL's deny-by-default trust gate: no agent, node, or receipt is trusted at 100%. Trust decays unless re-confirmed. The Khipu DAG maintains trust lineage — you can always ask "who vouched for this?" |
| **R — Respect** | "Respect the autonomy and intelligence of the learner. Don't micromanage." | SZL's principle of agent sovereignty: sub-agents run autonomously but emit receipts (Khipu events). The system respects their operation while maintaining audit visibility. |
| **I — Independence** | "Give people the tools and then step back. Over-supervision kills initiative." | SZL's async/event-driven arch: agents fire independently via the FIFO receipt bus (F7). The system doesn't poll or gate; it observes via emit-monotonicity (F22). |
| **C — Collaboration** | "Learning happens between people, not from authority figures. Peer-to-peer is the most powerful learning mode." | SZL's multi-agent mesh: a11oy orchestrates peer agents, not top-down. The consensus_basin viz shows collaborative convergence, not hierarchy. |
| **K — Kindness** | "Assume positive intent. Error is learning, not failure." | SZL's non-punitive observability: receipts capture divergence non-destructively. A failed synthesis is still a valid Khipu node (F4 acyclicity — no retroactive erasure). |

**The throughline for SZL:** Esther's TRICK framework operationalized at scale is exactly what the Khipu DAG receipt bus does for machine cognition: it creates a lineage-aware, trust-auditable, consent-first architecture where independence (async agents) and collaboration (shared DAG) coexist because receipts (the "kindness" layer) preserve context without blame. **Trust never 100% / lineage / receipts = TRICK applied to machine governance.**

## PART 2 — DECENTRALIZED SCIENCE (DeSci)

### 2.1 Movement Overview & Key Papers

**VERIFIED**

Decentralized Science (DeSci) is a movement applying Web3 infrastructure (blockchain, DAOs, NFTs, tokenized incentives, IPFS/Arweave storage) to the research lifecycle: funding, IP ownership, peer review, publication, data provenance, and reproducibility.

**Definition (peer-reviewed, VERIFIED):**
> "Decentralized Science (DeSci) represents a collaborative and decentralized approach to science, leveraging technological and infrastructural advancements such as Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), Web3, cryptocurrencies, and Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) to enable permissionless, open, and inclusive participation, facilitating collective governance, equitable incentivization, unrestricted access, shared ownership, and transparent funding of the scientific process."

Source: Weidener & Spreckelsen, *Frontiers in Blockchain*, 2024 — [DOI: 10.3389/fbloc.2024.1375763](https://doi.org/10.3389/fbloc.2024.1375763)
