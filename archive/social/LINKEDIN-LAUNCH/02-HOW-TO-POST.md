# How to publish — 20 minutes, end-to-end

Series A launches don't fail because the post is bad. They fail because the operator publishes at the wrong hour, in the wrong order, without the first-comment trick. Don't be that founder.

---

## ⏰ When to post

| Day | Time (your local) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Tuesday OR Wednesday** | **08:00–09:00** | Highest LinkedIn engagement window for B2B in 2025. Decision-makers check feeds at the start of the workday. |
| Avoid | Mon AM, Fri PM, weekends | Lowest reach |

If you can't hit Tue/Wed AM, **Thursday 11:00** is the secondary slot.

## 📋 The exact 20-minute publish flow

### T-15 min — pre-flight
1. Open LinkedIn on **desktop** (not mobile — image order is unreliable on mobile).
2. Open `LINKEDIN-LAUNCH/01-LINKEDIN-POST.md` in a separate window.
3. Open `LINKEDIN-LAUNCH/images/` in your file manager.
4. Disable your VPN if it's routing you through a different country (LinkedIn distribution is region-weighted to your profile location).

### T-0 — publish Post A (the carousel)
1. Click **Start a post** at the top of your LinkedIn home feed.
2. Click the **photo icon** (not document, not video).
3. **Select all 11 images at once** in your file manager (`01-cover.jpg` through `11-cta.jpg`), then drag-drop them into the LinkedIn upload box in **one motion**. LinkedIn preserves the alphabetical order — that's why every file is pre-numbered.
4. **Verify the order** in the preview. Slide 1 must be the cover. If any image is out of order, remove all and re-drag.
5. Copy the **POST A** body from `01-LINKEDIN-POST.md` and paste it into the text area.
6. **Do NOT add a clickable URL in the post body.** LinkedIn down-ranks posts with outbound links. The handles in the body are intentionally formatted as plain text — viewers will copy them, and the algorithm will reward you.
7. Click **Post**.

### T+5 min — the first-comment trick (this doubles reach)
1. Open your own post.
2. Paste the **first-comment reply** from `01-LINKEDIN-POST.md` into the comments box.
3. **This is where the live links go.** LinkedIn does NOT down-rank links inside comments — only links inside the post body. This is the highest-leverage move in the entire launch.

### T+15 min — first-hour engagement
1. Reply to **every comment** that arrives in the first 60 minutes. Each reply re-surfaces the post in feeds.
2. DM the 10 people you most want to see this — short, personal, "wanted you to see this directly" — and ask them to **comment** (not just like). Comments weigh ~10x more than likes.
3. **Do not edit the post.** Edits within the first hour reset distribution.

## 🚨 Common mistakes (don't make these)

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Uploading images one at a time | Always drag all 11 in one motion |
| Adding a link in the post body | Links live in the **first comment** only |
| Posting on mobile | Image order breaks. Always desktop. |
| Editing the post in the first hour | Resets the algorithmic distribution |
| Hashtag spam (>5 hashtags) | LinkedIn caps weight at ~5. Use the 8 in the template — it's intentional |
| Posting video + carousel together | LinkedIn doesn't allow it. Two separate posts, 24h apart. |
