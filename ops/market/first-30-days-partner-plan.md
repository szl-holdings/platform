# First 30 Days — Design Partner Plan

**Last updated:** April 2026  
**Purpose:** Structured plan for the first 30 days of every design partner pilot. Ensures fast time-to-value, baseline capture, and early signal generation.

---

## Why the First 30 Days Matter

The first 30 days determine whether a pilot succeeds. Partners who do not engage meaningfully in week 1–2 almost never recover. The goal is to make early engagement feel inevitable — not burdensome.

The first 30 days are not about demonstrating everything the platform can do. They are about establishing one working proof cycle: a signal surfaced → reviewed → decision made → outcome started.

---

## Day 0: Kickoff (2–4 hours)

**Participants:** Founder, executive sponsor, domain practitioner, technical POC

**Agenda:**

1. Platform orientation (45–60 min)
   - Walk through the governed decision loop using real examples from their domain
   - Show the Proof Chain in action — let them see what attribution looks like
   - Domain pack tour: the specific views and agents relevant to their use case
   - CORTEX mobile setup for key users who want mobile access

2. Role and access setup (30 min)
   - Assign roles: who is super_admin? Who is manager? Who is analyst? Who is viewer?
   - Walk through the permission model — what each role can and cannot do
   - Test login for all primary users

3. Success metric alignment (30 min)
   - Review agreed success metrics
   - Confirm data sources for baseline metrics are identifiable
   - Confirm first signal scenario: what is the first real decision they expect to route through the platform?

4. First-30-days plan walkthrough (15 min)
   - Review this document together
   - Confirm check-in cadence
   - Confirm escalation path for blockers

5. Q&A and open (15–30 min)

**Deliverable:** Kickoff notes sent within 24 hours, including role assignments, agreed metrics, and first signal scenario.

---

## Days 1–7: First Signal Cycle

**Objective:** Get at least one real signal through the platform — observed, reviewed, decision noted.

**Partner actions:**
- Log in daily (the platform does not become habit without habit-building in week 1)
- Review the domain pack dashboard — what signals does the platform surface from day one?
- Flag any signals that match real operational decisions they are currently facing

**Founder actions:**
- Check in on days 2 and 5 (brief async message)
- Ask: "Have you seen any signals that match real decisions?"
- If not: troubleshoot — is the platform correctly configured? Is their data seeded? Is there a domain setup issue?

**Technical setup check (by end of day 3):**
- [ ] All users can log in
- [ ] Domain pack data is loaded or seeded appropriately
- [ ] Signal sources are active (or clear plan for when live data connects)
- [ ] CORTEX mobile is accessible if desired

**End-of-week-1 check-in (async, 5 minutes):**
```
Week 1 check-in:
1. Has your team logged in? [yes/no]
2. Any signals surfaced that matched real decisions? [yes/no + brief]
3. Blockers? [list or "none"]
```

---

## Days 8–14: First Decision Cycle

**Objective:** Route one real decision through the platform end to end.

The first decision cycle does not need to be high-stakes. It needs to be real — an actual operational decision where:
1. A signal is observed on the platform
2. An AI recommendation is reviewed
3. A practitioner makes a decision (approve or reject)
4. The decision is logged with attribution

**Founder actions:**
- Review signal log from week 1
- Identify the strongest candidate for the first full decision cycle
- Coach partner through running it — stay on a call if needed
- Confirm the Proof Chain captured the attribution correctly

**Checkpoint (end of day 10, 15-minute call):**
- Has a full decision cycle run through the platform?
- If not: what is blocking it? Is this a product issue, a data issue, or an engagement issue?
- Adjust the plan based on the answer

---

## Days 15–21: Baseline Completion and Rhythm Establishment

**Objective:** Complete the baseline document. Establish the weekly operating rhythm.

**Baseline completion:**
- Revisit the baseline document started in pre-pilot setup
- Capture any metrics that were not captured before the pilot started
- Get partner sign-off on the baseline — they need to agree this is an accurate representation of their current state before we track deltas

**Operating rhythm by end of week 3:**
- [ ] Weekly async check-in format agreed and first one sent
- [ ] Bi-weekly sync call cadence on calendar
- [ ] Signal log is being updated (by partner, with founder nudging)
- [ ] Evaluation log is being updated (each significant AI recommendation reviewed and noted)

**What normal looks like at the 3-week mark:**
- 1–3 decisions have run through the platform
- Partner team is logging in without prompting
- Signal log has at least 5–7 entries
- At least one calibration feedback has been provided (a signal that was noise, a recommendation that was wrong)

---

## Days 22–30: 30-Day Checkpoint Preparation

**Objective:** Prepare and deliver the 30-day checkpoint. Confirm pilot trajectory.

**Founder preparation (days 22–26):**
- [ ] Compile signal log review: what surfaced, what was actioned, what was dismissed
- [ ] Compile evaluation log: which recommendations were used, which were rejected, why
- [ ] Early delta indicators: can we start to see movement in any baseline metrics?
- [ ] List of open blockers and product gaps surfaced in the first 30 days

**30-Day Checkpoint Meeting (30–45 minutes):**

1. Signal log review (10 min)
   - What did the platform surface?
   - What matched real operations vs. what was noise?
   - Any false positives to calibrate?

2. Decision cycle review (10 min)
   - How many decisions ran through the platform?
   - What did the Proof Chain attribution look like?
   - Did practitioners feel the approval workflow was right?

3. Early delta indicators (5 min)
   - Any visible movement in baseline metrics?
   - Too early for conclusions, but worth noting early signals

4. Blockers and product gaps (10 min)
   - What is not working?
   - What is missing that would make the next 60 days more effective?
   - Capture everything — this is the most valuable product feedback

5. Next 60 days focus (5 min)
   - What are the 2–3 things to focus on between now and the 60-day checkpoint?
   - Any staffing or access changes needed?

**30-Day Checkpoint deliverable (within 24 hours):**
Send partner a written summary of the checkpoint discussion and agreed next steps. Keep it brief — 1 page maximum.

---

## 30-Day Success Criteria

A pilot is on track at day 30 if:

- [ ] All users are logging in at least 2–3 times per week
- [ ] At least 3 full decision cycles completed (signal → review → decision → log)
- [ ] Baseline document is complete and signed off
- [ ] Weekly rhythm is established and partner is maintaining logs
- [ ] At least one piece of meaningful product feedback has been surfaced
- [ ] No unresolved technical blockers

A pilot is at risk at day 30 if:

- Users are not logging in consistently
- No decision cycles have completed
- Baseline document is incomplete
- Partner is not maintaining logs

If at risk: hold an emergency 30-minute call to diagnose and either restructure the pilot or have a direct conversation about whether the pilot can succeed.
