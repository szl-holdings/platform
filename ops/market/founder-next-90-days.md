# Founder Next 90 Days

**Last updated:** April 2026  
**Purpose:** 90-day founder operating rhythm — what to do, in what order, to convert platform readiness into first commercial traction.

---

## The 90-Day Objective

By the end of 90 days from April 2026:

1. At least one design partner pilot is active (30 days in or deeper)
2. At least one additional pilot has been qualified and is in setup or active
3. Baseline documents exist for all active pilots
4. Mobile beta (TestFlight Internal) is live and used by at least one partner
5. Trust Center is publicly accessible with accurate, honest content
6. Pricing and commercial path is clear and stated consistently
7. At least one anonymous case study or signal story is ready to share

This is not a product roadmap. This is a commercial activation plan.

---

## Month 1 (Days 1–30): First Partner Activation

**Primary objective:** Get first design partner pilot live and past the 30-day checkpoint successfully.

### Week 1: Foundation

- [ ] Read all ops/market documents — understand the full commercial operating model
- [ ] Confirm design partner pipeline: who is closest to qualified? Who needs a qualification call?
- [ ] Run qualification calls on top 3 pipeline prospects
- [ ] Schedule kickoff for first qualified partner
- [ ] Verify platform is stable: run full smoke tests, confirm all web apps load, confirm API health

### Week 2: First Kickoff

- [ ] Run first design partner kickoff (use `first-30-days-partner-plan.md`)
- [ ] Complete baseline document with partner
- [ ] Set up partner Slack channel
- [ ] Confirm all users can log in and navigate the domain pack
- [ ] Day 2 and day 5 check-ins

### Week 3: First Signal Cycle

- [ ] Guide partner through first signal cycle (signal surfaced → reviewed → decision logged)
- [ ] Confirm Proof Chain attribution is capturing correctly
- [ ] Address any onboarding gaps or technical issues
- [ ] Qualify second pipeline prospect if not already qualified

### Week 4: 30-Day Checkpoint Preparation

- [ ] Compile signal log review for partner 1
- [ ] Prepare 30-day checkpoint summary
- [ ] Run 30-day checkpoint with partner
- [ ] Send written summary within 24 hours
- [ ] Assess pilot health: on track or at risk?

**Month 1 deliverables:**
- First pilot past 30-day checkpoint
- Baseline document complete and signed
- Second partner qualified (or close)

---

## Month 2 (Days 31–60): Second Partner and Trust Center

**Primary objective:** Second partner onboarded. Trust Center live. Mobile beta started.

### Week 5: Second Partner Setup

- [ ] Run second design partner kickoff (if qualified)
- [ ] Complete baseline document for second partner
- [ ] Continue weekly rhythm with partner 1 (async check-ins + bi-weekly sync)

### Week 6: Trust Center Launch

- [ ] Review Trust Center content against `trust-center-launch-pass.md` checklist
- [ ] Ensure all content is accurate (no overstated claims)
- [ ] Proactive disclosure of known gaps with roadmap dates
- [ ] Trust Center pages live at /trust (all sections)

### Week 7: Mobile Beta

- [ ] Complete Apple Developer Portal and App Store Connect setup (see `manual-console-actions-master-final.md`)
- [ ] Build and submit first TestFlight internal build
- [ ] Invite first partner's team to TestFlight
- [ ] Verify core mobile flows on physical device

### Week 8: 60-Day Checkpoint (Partner 1)

- [ ] Prepare preliminary delta report for partner 1
- [ ] Run 60-day checkpoint
- [ ] Begin referenceability conversation
- [ ] Identify case study storyline if partner is willing

**Month 2 deliverables:**
- Second partner 30 days in (first 30-day checkpoint done)
- Trust Center publicly accessible
- TestFlight Internal build live
- Partner 1 at 60-day mark with delta data starting to accumulate

---

## Month 3 (Days 61–90): Proof and Commercial Path

**Primary objective:** First proof output ready. Commercial conversion conversation active. Third partner qualified.

### Week 9: Proof Preparation

- [ ] Compile full signal and evaluation logs for partner 1
- [ ] Prepare 90-day delta report
- [ ] Draft case study structure (even if partner has not agreed to be referenceable)

### Week 10: Third Partner Qualification

- [ ] Qualify third pipeline prospect
- [ ] Begin diligence engagement if prospect is ready
- [ ] Continue weekly rhythm with both active partners

### Week 11: Commercial Preparation

- [ ] Draft production agreement template (MSA + Order Form)
- [ ] Configure Stripe billing for first expected pricing tier
- [ ] Prepare commercial conversation agenda for 90-day review

### Week 12: 90-Day Review (Partner 1)

- [ ] Run full 90-day review meeting
- [ ] Present delta report
- [ ] Commercial conversion conversation
- [ ] Referenceability conversation
- [ ] Follow-up: commercial agreement draft OR structured exit
- [ ] Case study draft (if referenceable)

**Month 3 deliverables:**
- Partner 1 90-day review complete
- Commercial conversion in process or structured exit documented
- At least one proof output ready (delta report + case study draft or anonymous story)
- Third partner in qualification or setup
- Billing infrastructure activated

## 90-Day Anti-Patterns

These patterns kill commercial traction. Avoid them:

1. **Letting a pilot drift without check-ins.** Partners disengage without the weekly rhythm. No check-in = dying pilot.
2. **Adding product features instead of running pilots.** The platform is ready. Pilots are the next thing to build. Not more features.
3. **Avoiding the commercial conversation.** The 60-day checkpoint is when to first raise commercial terms. Do not wait for the 90-day review.
4. **Treating all pipeline as equal.** Focus on the 2–3 prospects closest to design partner qualification. Not the 15 who are mildly interested.
5. **Running more than 4 pilots simultaneously.** Quality engagement degrades above 4 concurrent pilots. Better to run 3 great pilots than 6 neglected ones.
