# Executive Demo Brief

**For**: Sales, BD, and executive team members running 5-minute executive demos  
**Audience**: C-suite, investors, board members, strategic partners  
**Duration**: 5 minutes  
**Goal**: Conviction close — leave the executive understanding the core value proposition and ready to discuss next steps

---

## Opening Frame (30 seconds)

> "The hardest operational problem for any complex enterprise is making the right decision fast — with confidence, with evidence, and with a record. Most companies have data. Very few have a governed way to act on it. That's what I want to show you."

Set the screen to: `/command/strategy` — Governed Decision Loop

---

## Act 1 — The Signal (1:00)

Point to the signal card at the top of the loop:

> "This is a real maritime scenario. Three vessels are outside SLA compliance. The system detected the deviation automatically from AIS telemetry — no human had to look for it."

Click through to the Context step:

> "Before surfacing this signal, the AI pulled in cross-domain context: a piracy advisory from Aegis, port congestion data from Terra, and the specific SLA penalty clause from the contract system. It didn't just detect a problem — it understood it."

**Talking points**:
- Signal detection is automatic — no manual monitoring
- Context enrichment pulls from multiple data sources, not just one
- Every source is attributed — no black boxes

---

## Act 2 — The Recommendation (45 seconds)

Click to the Recommendation step, then the Simulation step:

> "The AI generated a recommendation: reroute the vessel via the Strait of Malacca. 82% confidence. Here's what makes this different from a chatbot: there's a Monte Carlo simulation behind the recommendation. 10,000 scenarios. The reroute path has a median net saving of $1.82M. Maintaining course has a median expected loss of $850K. That's a data-driven decision, not a guess."

**Talking points**:
- Recommendations come with confidence scores, not just conclusions
- Monte Carlo simulation makes the risk/reward tradeoff visible
- Executives can see the reasoning, not just the answer

---

## Act 3 — Governed Approval (1:00)

Click to the Policy Gate step, then Approval:

> "Before any action can be taken, the system evaluates it against Covenant Policies — our governance engine. Four policies checked automatically: financial threshold, human approval gate, audit log requirement, and domain lead sign-off. All passed in milliseconds."

Point to the approval chain:

> "Then it went to a 3-step human approval chain: Fleet Operations Lead, Finance Controller, and the CEO. Each approver saw the same evidence — the same signals, the same simulation, the same recommendation. The CEO approved it in 33 minutes. The action was executed immediately after."

**Talking points**:
- Policy evaluation is automatic — governance at machine speed
- Human approval is always in the loop for high-stakes decisions
- Every approver sees the same evidence — no information asymmetry

---

## Act 4 — Proof (45 seconds)

Click to the Proof Chain step:

> "This is the part that matters to your legal team, your compliance team, and your board. Every AI-generated output has a permanent receipt: what the model was, what confidence it had, who reviewed it, when they approved it, and what changed as a result. This is immutable. It cannot be altered."

Click to the Outcome step:

> "And we close the loop. The SLA breach was avoided. $2.1M was protected. The model's prediction accuracy is logged. The next time a similar signal appears, the system is smarter."

**Talking points**:
- Proof chain is designed for board, legal, and compliance audiences
- Outcomes are measured — predictions are validated, not just made
- Every cycle improves the system

---

## Closing Frame (30 seconds)

> "What you just saw is the full governed execution loop: Signal → Context → Recommendation → Simulation → Policy → Execution → Proof → Outcome → Learning. Every step is transparent, every decision is traceable, and every action is reversible. That's the platform."

**Transition to**: questions, pricing discussion, or next steps

---

## Common Executive Questions

**"Is this real data or a demo?"**
> "The data in this demo is illustrative — built to mirror real operational scenarios. In production, it connects to your live data sources. Every data connection is configured, attributed, and visible."

**"What does implementation look like?"**
> "We work with your ops team to configure the domain packs relevant to your business — maritime, real estate, security, or others. Standard implementation is 60–90 days to first live signal."

**"How does the AI know what to recommend?"**
> "It's trained on your operational history and tuned to your policies. The Covenant Policy engine is where your rules live — financial thresholds, approval chains, risk tolerances. You own the rules. The AI works within them."

**"What if the AI is wrong?"**
> "That's why the approval chain exists. The AI proposes. Humans decide. And if the AI was wrong, the proof chain shows exactly what it recommended and why — so you can improve the model. No system is perfect. This one is transparent about where it's uncertain."
