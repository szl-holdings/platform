# Executive Summary — Market Readiness Documentation Suite

**Date:** April 2026  
**Scope:** `/ops/market/` documentation suite — all phases

---

## What This Document Suite Is

This is the complete commercial "paper infrastructure" that converts the SZL Holdings platform from a strong product into something buyers, evaluators, and design partners can actually engage with.

The platform is built. The architecture is real. The documentation was the gap. This suite closes it.

---

## The Strategic Positioning

**SZL Holdings is the governed execution layer for enterprise intelligence.**

The operating loop: **observe → evaluate → decide → approve → act → prove.**

This positioning runs through every document in this suite. It is not marketing language — it is an accurate description of what the platform does architecturally. Every document in this suite reinforces it and avoids the commodity AI language that obscures it.

The platform is real. The architecture is real. The docs are now real.

- Paying customers
- SOC 2 certification

These are not blockers to starting the design partner program. They are milestones that the design partner program helps earn the right to reach.

The documentation suite is the commercial foundation. The design partner program is the commercial activation. The platform is the product.

All three exist now. The work is to connect them.
