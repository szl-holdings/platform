## The Governed Decision Loop

A live maritime scenario: three vessels outside SLA. The platform takes the executive end-to-end through nine governed steps in one screen.

- **Signal** — Detected automatically from AIS telemetry. No manual monitoring.
- **Context** — Cross-domain enrichment from Aegis (security), Terra (port congestion), and PRISM (contract clauses). Every source attributed.
- **Recommendation** — Reroute via the Strait of Malacca. 82% confidence, with reasoning visible.
- **Simulation** — 10,000-iteration Monte Carlo. Median net saving of $1.82M for the recommended path; expected loss of $850K for inaction.
- **Policy Gate** — Four Covenant Policies auto-evaluated in milliseconds: financial threshold, human approval, audit log, domain sign-off.
- **Approval** — Three-step human chain (Fleet Lead → Finance Controller → CEO). Every approver sees the same evidence.
- **Execution** — Reroute order dispatched on CEO approval. Workflow engine, not a person, makes the API call.
- **Proof Chain** — Immutable receipt: model, confidence, reviewer, timestamp, outcome. The artifact your legal, compliance, and board teams ask for.
- **Outcome** — SLA breach avoided. $2.1M protected. Prediction accuracy logged for the next cycle.

## Why This Matters

- **Governance at machine speed.** Policy enforcement happens before anything reaches a human.
- **Evidence parity.** Every approver sees the same simulation, recommendation, and source data — no information asymmetry.
- **Permanent receipts.** Every AI-generated decision is traceable, reversible, and auditable.
