## The Category: Governed Decision Infrastructure

SZL Holdings does not compete in an existing category. It creates one.

The nearest adjacencies — and why SZL is structurally different:

| Adjacent Category | Representative | What They Do | What SZL Does Differently |
|-------------------|----------------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Decision Intelligence | Palantir Foundry/AIP | Ontology-driven data fusion + AI agents on enterprise data | SZL enforces governance *on* the decision, not just on the data. Every recommendation has approval gates, proof chains, and outcome tracking. Palantir's ontology is a data model; SZL's loop is a governance model. |
| Autonomous Systems | Anduril Lattice | Real-time sensor fusion + autonomous effector control across domains | Lattice optimizes for speed-to-action in military OODA loops. SZL optimizes for *accountable* action in high-consequence commercial domains. Lattice connects sensors to effectors; SZL connects signals to governed decisions. |
| Data Governance | Databricks Unity Catalog | Unified governance for data assets, ML models, and AI artifacts | Unity Catalog governs *data access*. SZL governs *decision execution*. Unity answers "who can see this data?" SZL answers "who approved this action, based on what evidence, with what expected outcome?" |
| Compliance Automation | Vanta, Drata | Continuous compliance monitoring + trust center portals | Vanta/Drata prove you *follow rules*. SZL proves you *make good decisions*. Compliance is a subset of governance; SZL's proof chain covers AI attribution, not just SOC 2 controls. |
| Workflow Automation | ServiceNow, Monday.com | Process automation with approval routing | Workflow tools automate *sequences*. SZL adds simulation, policy enforcement, and immutable attribution to every workflow step. The governed loop is not a workflow — it's a decision structure. |

```
Signal → Context → Recommendation → Simulation → Policy → Execution → Proof → Outcome → Learning
```

Every competitor implements *parts* of this loop:

| Step | Palantir | Anduril | Vanta | Stripe | SZL |

| Signal ingestion | ✅ Ontology | ✅ Sensor mesh | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ Event Fabric |
