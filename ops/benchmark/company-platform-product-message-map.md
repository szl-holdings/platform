## Competitive Message Differentiation

### vs. Palantir
- Palantir: "The operating system for the modern enterprise"
- SZL: "The governed decision layer for high-consequence operations"
- Delta: SZL focuses on governance and accountability, not data analytics. SZL's primitives enforce approval gates and outcome tracking that Palantir's ontology does not.

### vs. Anduril
- Anduril: "Transforming defense capabilities with autonomous systems"
- SZL: "Governed decision infrastructure across defense AND commercial domains"
- Delta: SZL covers maritime, real estate, consulting — not just defense. SZL prioritizes human-in-the-loop governance over autonomous action.

### vs. Vanta/Drata
- Vanta: "Automate security and compliance"
- SZL: "Govern every consequential decision, not just compliance checkboxes"
- Delta: Vanta proves you follow rules. SZL proves you make good decisions. Proof Chain covers AI attribution, human decisions, and real-world outcomes — not just SOC 2 controls.

### vs. ServiceNow
- ServiceNow: "The platform of platforms for digital business"
- SZL: "Governed decision infrastructure — not workflow automation"
- Delta: ServiceNow automates sequences. SZL adds simulation, policy enforcement, and immutable attribution to every decision step.
