## SZL's Unique Capabilities (No Competitor Has)

1. **Risk simulation inline with decisions:** Monte Carlo simulation integrated into the decision pipeline
2. **Outcome tracking with variance:** Predicted vs. actual with quantitative variance metrics
3. **Closed-loop confidence calibration:** AI confidence adjusted based on historical outcomes
4. **Cross-domain governance:** Same governance loop across defense, maritime, real estate, legal, consulting
5. **Decision receipt export:** Structured, exportable records for external compliance
