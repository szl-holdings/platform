# Design Partner Offer — Final
Generated: 2026-04-16

## What This Document Is
The canonical, founder-approved definition of the SZL design-partner offer. Use this to qualify inbound interest, frame conversations with prospective partners, and align internal expectations before any commitment is made.

---

## Who Qualifies

Design partners are not beta customers. They are operators who have a live problem in a high-consequence domain and are willing to co-develop the solution with us.

### Qualifying Criteria

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Org size | 50–2,000 employees (enterprise buyers or substantial mid-market) |
| Domain | Legal ops, maritime, defense/security, real estate, or regulated enterprise operations |
| Decision-maker involvement | Sponsor is VP-level or above and is directly engaged |
| Problem specificity | Can articulate a concrete, measurable operational problem today |
| Internal capacity | Has 1+ internal champion who can commit 2–3 hours/week during the pilot |
| Data access | Can share representative (even anonymized) workflow data within 30 days |
| Pilot timeline | Can start within 45 days of signing |

### Disqualifying Signals

- Looking for a vendor relationship, not a co-development relationship
- Sponsor cannot commit personal time to weekly reviews
- Primary motivation is cost reduction, not operational clarity
- Organization is in active regulatory investigation or M&A process that blocks access
- Requires production SLA guarantees before any data ingestion

---

## What Partners Receive

### Platform Access
- Full access to the relevant domain pack (Lyte + Alloy + domain layer: Counsel, Vessels, Aegis, or Terra) for the duration of the pilot
- Direct configuration support from the founding team — not a support queue
- Weekly 45-minute working session with a founder or senior team member
- Access to the product roadmap and the ability to influence prioritization

### Co-Development Rights
- Ability to submit feature and integration requests that go directly into the sprint board
- Early access to new modules before general release
- Named acknowledgment in case study materials (optional — can be anonymous)

### Commercial Terms
- Pilot runs at reduced or no cost for 90 days (exact terms set per partner based on scope)
- First-mover pricing locked at close of pilot if partner converts to paid
- Right of first refusal on enterprise expansion before general availability

| Commitment | Detail |

| Success review | Participate in a 90-day success review with defined outcome criteria |
| NDA | Mutual NDA required before any technical access is granted |

## Success Metrics

Success is defined jointly during onboarding and documented in the partner brief. The following are the default metric categories:

### Operational Metrics (Partner-Defined)
- Reduction in approval latency (target: measurable within 45 days)
- Reduction in workflow errors or ownership gaps
- Improvement in audit trail completeness
- Time-to-resolution for flagged incidents

### Platform Metrics (SZL-Tracked)
- Weekly active usage by the internal champion and at least 2 additional users
- Signal ingestion coverage (% of target workflow data piped to Lyte)
- Action routing events through Alloy
- Proof chain entries generated

### Relationship Health
- NPS score from internal champion at weeks 8 and 12
- Partner sentiment during weekly review (tracked in review log)
- Escalations raised and resolved

## Exit Conditions

Not every pilot succeeds. We plan for clean exits.

| Scenario | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| Partner converts | Move to commercial terms; document and publish case study |
| Pilot succeeds, partner defers | 6-month reactivation window at pilot pricing |
| Pilot fails due to fit | Debrief call, internal post-mortem, no hard feelings |
| Partner goes dark | 2 missed reviews triggers a formal check-in; 3 missed reviews closes the pilot |
