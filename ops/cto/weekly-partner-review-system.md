# Weekly Partner Review System
Generated: 2026-04-16

## Purpose
A structured, repeatable format for the weekly 45-minute working session with each design partner. Consistency builds trust. The review system ensures that every session produces a documented output and a clear next action.

---

## Review Cadence

| Meeting | Timing | Duration | Format |
|---------|--------|----------|--------|
| Weekly working session | Every week, same day/time | 45 min | Video call |
| Week 4 feedback review | End of Week 4 | 60 min | Video call (structured) |
| Week 8 feedback review | End of Week 8 | 60 min | Video call (structured) |
| Week 12 success review | End of Week 12 | 60 min | Video call (see onboarding machine) |

## Standard Weekly Review (45 min)

### Pre-Call Checklist (SZL, 10 min before)
- [ ] Review last week's action items — what was committed, what was delivered
- [ ] Pull platform metrics: sessions, signal events, action routes, proof chain entries
- [ ] Note any issues or anomalies to raise proactively
- [ ] Have the review log open and ready to fill in real-time

### Agenda

| Time | Topic | Owner |
|------|-------|-------|
| 0–5 min | Quick sync: how has the week gone, any blockers | Partner leads |
| 5–15 min | Platform review: what did Lyte surface this week | SZL leads |
| 15–25 min | Action routing review: what was routed, approved, or rejected | Both |
| 25–35 min | Open issues and feature requests | Both |
| 35–40 min | Next week's focus: 1–2 specific things to work on | Both |
| 40–45 min | Confirm action items: who owns what, by when | SZL closes |

### Non-Negotiables
- Every session ends with a written list of action items (even if it's just "no changes this week")
- Every session is logged in the review log (see template below)
- If SZL has nothing new to show from the platform, flag it in advance — do not show up empty

## Week 4 Review (60 min, structured)

### Pre-Call
- Compile signal brief: top 3 operational insights surfaced in Weeks 1–4
- Compile action report: all routed actions and outcomes
- Pull Week 4 Feedback Form responses (sent in advance)

### Agenda

| Time | Topic |
|------|-------|
| 0–10 min | Partner reads their feedback form answers aloud; SZL listens without interrupting |
| 10–25 min | SZL presents the 4-week signal brief |
| 25–35 min | Joint discussion: what's working, what's not |
| 35–45 min | Priority setting: top 3 things to accomplish in Weeks 5–8 |
| 45–55 min | Commercial check-in: any questions about the transition to paid |
| 55–60 min | Action items and confirm Week 8 date |

## Escalation During Reviews

If a partner raises something that falls outside the weekly review scope, apply the following:

| Signal | Response |
|--------|----------|
| Data security concern | Stop, log the concern, schedule a dedicated call within 48 hours |
| Request for contractual change | Note it, do not commit, escalate to founder within 24 hours |
| Threats to withdraw | Don't negotiate on the call — end the meeting cleanly, follow up personally within 24 hours |
| Positive expansion signal | Don't commit to pricing on the call — note it, follow up with a written summary |
| Technical blocker | Triage severity (P0/P1/P2), commit to resolution timeline before end of call |

## Measuring Review Health

Track these across all partners weekly:

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Reviews completed on schedule | ≥ 90% |
| Action items logged | 100% of sessions |
| Partner sentiment: Positive or Very Positive | ≥ 70% |
| Feature requests logged | 100% |
| Reviews with no new platform output to show | ≤ 1 per partner per quarter |
