# First 30 Days Partner Operating Model
Generated: 2026-04-16

## Purpose
A concrete, day-by-day operating model for the first 30 days of an active pilot. The goal of the first 30 days is to get signal ingestion live, surface at least one meaningful insight, and validate that the working relationship is sustainable.

---

## Success Definition for Day 30

By Day 30, a successful pilot will have:
1. At least one live data source connected to Lyte
2. At least 5 approval queue or ownership-gap events surfaced and reviewed by the internal champion
3. At least 3 weekly reviews completed
4. A Week 4 feedback form submitted
5. Internal champion expressing continued interest in the pilot

---

## Week 1 — Environment & Access

**Theme:** Get connected. No insights yet. Just working infrastructure.

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 1 | Credentials confirmed; partner logs in for the first time | Partner |
| 1 | Confirm data source(s) for Week 1 connection | SZL + Partner |
| 2–3 | First data connection attempt (even if partial) | SZL (technical) |
| 3 | Async check-in: what's working, what's blocked | SZL |
| 5 | Week 1 review (see weekly review system) | Both |

**Milestone:** At least one data source connected by end of Week 1, even if only in read mode.

**Escalation trigger:** Data connection blocked by IT or security — escalate immediately, do not wait for Week 2.

---

## Week 2 — First Signal Surface

**Theme:** See something real. Even one insight changes the partner's mental model.

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 6–7 | SZL reviews first signal output; prepares a "signal brief" for review | SZL |
| 8 | Deliver signal brief: 2–3 surfaced insights from actual data | SZL |
| 8–9 | Partner reviews brief with internal champion; records reactions | Partner |
| 10 | Week 2 review: walk through the signal brief together | Both |

**Milestone:** Partner has seen at least one real operational insight derived from their data.

**What a signal brief looks like:**
- Title: "What Lyte found in your [system name] data"
- 2–3 bullet points: what was surfaced, why it matters, what Alloy could do about it
- Screenshot or export from the platform
- 1 question for the partner: "Is this the right thing to be measuring?"

---

## Week 3 — First Action Route

**Theme:** Move from observation to action. Show that Alloy can close the loop.

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 11–13 | Configure Alloy to route one action type from the surfaced signals | SZL |
| 13 | Demo the action route to the internal champion (async video is fine) | SZL |
| 14 | Partner tries the action route themselves | Partner |
| 15 | Week 3 review: was the action route useful? Would they approve it? | Both |

**Milestone:** Partner has approved or rejected at least one Alloy-routed action.

**Key question to answer at Week 3 review:**
- "If this action routing worked as designed, who in your org would need to sign off on it?"
- Answer to this question shapes the governance configuration going forward.

---

## Week 4 — First Feedback & Course Correct

**Theme:** Honest review. Adjust before the pattern solidifies.

| Day | Activity | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| 16–18 | SZL prepares Week 4 summary: what's been built, what's been surfaced, what's been routed | SZL |
| 19 | Partner completes Week 4 Feedback Form (see below) | Partner |
| 20 | Week 4 review: structured feedback session | Both |
| 20 | SZL files internal debrief and updates sprint priorities | SZL |

**Week 4 Feedback Form (send via email or shared doc)**

```
Week 4 Design Partner Feedback — [Org Name]

1. On a scale of 1–10, how useful has the platform been so far? (1 = not at all useful)

2. What has been the single most valuable thing Lyte has surfaced?

3. What has been the most frustrating or confusing part of the experience?

4. If we could only change one thing before Week 8, what should it be?

5. How likely are you to continue the pilot to Week 12? (Definitely / Probably / Unsure / Probably not)

6. Is there anyone else in your organization who should be seeing this?
```

## Day 30 Checkpoint

The Day 30 checkpoint is a brief internal assessment — not a partner-facing meeting.

**Internal checklist:**

| Check | Status |
|-------|--------|
| Live data source connected | Yes / Partial / No |
| Signal brief delivered and reviewed | Yes / No |
| Alloy action route configured | Yes / No |
| 3 weekly reviews completed | Yes / No |
| Week 4 feedback received | Yes / No |
| Partner NPS (implicit from feedback form) | Score |
| Pilot health overall | Green / Amber / Red |

**If Green:** Continue to standard pilot cadence through Week 12.  
**If Amber:** Schedule a founder-to-sponsor call to address blockers before Week 5.  
**If Red:** Evaluate whether to formally extend, restructure, or close the pilot early.

---

## Key Principles for the First 30 Days

1. **Speed builds trust.** The partner needs to see real output within 14 days or they will lose interest, regardless of their commitment level.
2. **Don't wait for perfect data.** A partial connection with partial data is better than a clean connection with no data. Show them something.
3. **Every review ends with one clear next action.** If neither side knows what happens next, the pilot drifts.
4. **Be explicit about what's custom vs. what's standard.** Partners remember the custom work and assume it's free. Define the boundary early.
5. **The internal champion is your lifeline.** If they disengage, the pilot is effectively over. Treat their time as the scarcest resource.
