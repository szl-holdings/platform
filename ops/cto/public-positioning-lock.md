## The Value Loop (locked)

Signal → visibility → explainable forecast → governed action

- **Signal:** Lyte connects to the approval queues, task systems, and workflow tools already in use.
- **Visibility:** Execution risk, ownership gaps, and workflow friction surface before they compound.
- **Forecast:** What is at risk, why, and what happens next — in plain language, with source attribution.
- **Governed action:** Alloy routes action to the right person through the right channel, with approval controls and a full audit trail.

---

## What Alloy Is (locked)

Alloy is the durable operating layer that:
1. Routes action and verifies follow-through.
2. Assigns ownership, triggers steps, escalates when nothing moves.
3. Records the complete proof chain for audit.
4. Enforces policy controls built into the action primitive (not bolted on later).

**Alloy is not a standalone product.** The page `/alloy-fabric` exists as an architecture reference for technical evaluators — it describes the backbone's capabilities, integration surface, and proof chain design. It is not a commercial marketing page and should never carry pricing, trial CTAs, or independent positioning. It is described in context of what Lyte and domain packs run on.

---

## What Domain Packs Are (locked)

Domain packs are governed vertical extensions of the SZL Holdings platform. Each pack:
- Inherits Alloy's proof chain, policy controls, and audit architecture.
- Extends Lyte's observability layer into a specific vertical environment.
- Is not a separate product — it cannot operate without Alloy.
- Is not a one-off integration — it is a governed extension.

**Positioning sentence:** "The same Alloy backbone, policy controls, and proof chain — extended to your domain."
