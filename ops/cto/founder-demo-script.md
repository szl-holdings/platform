# Founder Demo Script — 5-Minute Route
**Phase C — CTO Pass**
*Completed: April 16, 2026*
*Audience: Investors, partners, warm introductions*
*Duration: 4–5 minutes*

---

## Purpose

This is the founder pitch demo — used when you have 5 minutes and need to leave the audience with one conviction: *"This is the operating system for complex businesses, and no one else is building it."*

The script is designed to be run without slides. The product is the demo.

---

## Setup (30 seconds before you begin)

1. Open `/command/` in a clean browser window
2. Activate demo mode via sidebar toggle — confirm amber banner appears
3. Navigate to `/command/operations`
4. Position window at 1440×900 or higher
5. Have `/command/operations/approvals` as the second tab

---

## The Opening Line

*"I'm going to show you something in 5 minutes that most enterprise software vendors would take 45 slides to explain — and still not make you feel it."*

---

## Scene 1: The Operating Loop Rail (45 seconds)

**Screen:** `/command/operations` — Executive Command

**Click path:** None needed. The Operating Loop Rail is already visible at the top.

**What to say:**
> "Most businesses run on six stages — they observe what's happening, they evaluate it, they make a decision, someone approves it, an action gets taken, and then — if they're lucky — they prove it happened. Most organizations can't see this loop. They're running blind. Look at this."

**Point to the Operating Loop Rail:**
> "Observe — 44 signals. Evaluate — 18 under analysis. Decide — 8 decision items. Approve — 4 pending approval. Act — 12 in motion. Prove — 31 evidenced. One rail. Six stages. Every item in the organization accounted for."

**Pause.** Let it land.

> "No other business software shows you this. Not Salesforce. Not ServiceNow. Not Monday. This is the operating loop of a complex business, made visible."

---

## Scene 2: The Approval Chain (90 seconds)

**Screen:** `/command/operations/approvals`

**Click path:** Navigate to Approvals Center → click to expand approval item A-1041

**What to say:**
> "Decisions sit in organizations for days. Weeks. Nobody knows why. Here's an approval that's been pending 31 hours. $1.2 million in revenue attached to it."

**Point to the approval card:**
> "The evidence behind this decision — revenue model, Q1 actuals — is right here. The confidence score is 88%. And look at this chain."

**Point to the approval chain visual:**
> "Sarah Chen submitted it. Michael Torres approved it 28 hours ago. Stephen Lutar — the CXO — is the current bottleneck. It's not lost. It's not stuck in someone's inbox. The business knows where it is."

**Pause.**

> "This is accountability made structural. Not a Slack thread. Not a spreadsheet. A governed approval with a full audit chain."

---

## Scene 3: The Proof Receipt (60 seconds)

**Screen:** `/command/operations/alloy/receipts`

**Click path:** Navigate to Trust Receipts

**What to say:**
> "When an action happens in this system, it produces a receipt. Not a log entry buried in a database — a receipt. Like a transaction. Signal in, evidence retrieved, decision made, approved, action taken, outcome recorded. Every link in the chain."

**Point to a receipt:**
> "Auditors love this. Boards love this. Compliance teams — who have been dealing with unstructured email trails for decades — love this."

**Pause.**

> "This is what 'explainable AI' actually looks like. Not a tooltip. Not a confidence bar. A complete proof chain from input to outcome."

---

## The Close (30 seconds)

> "Most businesses have the data. They have the tools. What they don't have is a system that connects signal to action to proof — in one surface, with a human in the loop at every consequential step."

> "That's what SZL builds. We call it the Business Operating System."

**If time permits — point to the demo mode banner:**
> "Everything you just saw is synthetic demo data — the amber banner tells you that. In a live deployment, this is your real organization. Your real approvals. Your real proof chain."

## Objection Handling (5-minute version)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Is the AI making decisions?" | No. The AI produces structured recommendations. A human approves every consequential action. That's enforced at the workflow layer — not optional. |
| "We have tools for this" | I believe you. Lyte doesn't replace them — it watches them. It ingests from whatever you have and turns the signals into a governed operating loop. |
| "How is this different from a dashboard?" | A dashboard shows you what happened. This tells you what's at risk, who's blocking what, and produces a proof receipt when action is taken. |
| "What does implementation take?" | A pilot starts with one workflow instrumented. We've designed for zero rip-and-replace. |
