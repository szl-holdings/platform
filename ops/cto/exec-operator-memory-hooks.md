**Phase D — CTO Pass**

---

## What This Is

Memory hooks are the phrases, visuals, and moments that stick in the mind of an executive or operator after a demo or first use. They are the building blocks of word-of-mouth.

---

## Memory Hook Set — Unified Command Center

### Hook 1: "The Loop"
> "It shows me where every decision is right now — how many things are being observed, how many are waiting for my approval, how many are already in motion. I've never seen that before."

**What produces it:** The Operating Loop Rail (Observe → Evaluate → Decide → Approve → Act → Prove) with live counts at the top of Executive Command.

**Target audience:** CxO, COO, board observer.

---

### Hook 2: "One Click and I See Everything"
> "I clicked on an approval and it showed me the evidence, who's already signed off, and what the next step is — all without leaving the page."

**What produces it:** Expandable approval items in Approval Overwatch with evidence fields, confidence scores, and full audit chain.

**Target audience:** CEO, CFO, approver roles.

---

### Hook 3: "I Can See Who Owns It"
> "Usually you have to go chase people to find out who owns a problem. This just tells you — here's the owner, here's the stage, here's the due date, here's why it's risky."

**What produces it:** Pressure Board items with Owner, Stage, Due Date, and Risk Category visible inline.

**Target audience:** COO, department heads, delivery leads.

---

### Hook 4: "The Command Bar is Like Having a Co-Pilot"
> "I hit Command-K and it shows me every part of the system, organized. I can go anywhere in two keystrokes. It feels like the product knows what I need."

**What produces it:** Command palette with grouped quick navigation, filtered nav, keyboard navigation, and Enter-to-go.

**Target audience:** Power operators, ops leads, technical executives.

---

### Hook 5: "You Can Tell Immediately It's Not Live"
> "In the demo you see this amber stripe at the top that says 'synthetic data only'. When we switch to production, it disappears. That level of clarity is unusual."

**What produces it:** Demo mode amber banner + header tint; production mode has no indicators.

**Target audience:** Technical buyers, IT directors, compliance-sensitive executives.

---

### Hook 6: "The System Knows Its Own Health"
> "Right there on the main screen — six services, their latency, their uptime. I can see instantly if something's degraded."

**What produces it:** Service Health Strip showing API Gateway, Auth, PRISM Engine, Vessels Feed, Terra Data, Aegis Intel with status/latency/uptime.

**Target audience:** CTO, infrastructure owners, NOC leads.

---

## How to Use These in a Demo

1. **Open with the Loop** — immediately point at the Operating Loop Rail: "Let me show you something you've never seen in a business dashboard before."
2. **Click an approval** — expand it and walk through the evidence + chain: "No email trail, no Slack thread. Everything is right here."
3. **Open the command bar** — hit `⌘K` with no query typed: "Every part of the system is a keystroke away."
4. **Toggle demo mode** — deactivate it and show the amber stripe disappear: "In production, there's nothing there. We take the distinction seriously."
5. **Close with service health** — gesture at the health strip: "This is live in production. You can see latency and uptime for every service at a glance."

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

- Do not click through more than 3 screens without anchoring back to Executive Command.
- Do not demo the sidebar navigation without explaining what it connects to.
- Do not leave demo mode on when showing "what production looks like."
- Do not mention page counts or feature volume — focus on operator outcomes.
