# Demo to Pilot Flow

Phase A · SZL Scale, Close, and Operate Pass
Updated: 2026-04-16

## Purpose

Operational checklist that takes a prospect from discovery call to a
signed, paid pilot in ≤14 calendar days.

## Day-by-Day Track

| Day | Step | Output | Owner |
|-----|------|--------|-------|
| 0 | Discovery call (30 min) | Workload + pain + decision-maker captured in pipeline doc | Founder |
| 1 | Demo prep | Staging workspace seeded with prospect-shaped data; demo script written for their domain | Founder + Engineering |
| 2 | Tailored demo (60 min) | Recorded reaction; explicit "would use" or "would not use" answer | Founder |
| 3 | Follow-up summary email | Restates the workload, the demo result, the proposed pilot scope, the pilot price | Founder |
| 4–5 | Scope alignment | One success metric agreed in writing; data scope agreed | Founder |
| 6 | MSA + DPA sent | Counsel-prepared docs sent via DocuSign | Counsel |
| 7–10 | Procurement | Prospect's procurement, security, and legal review | Prospect |
| 11–12 | Redlines round | Single negotiation round; standard fallbacks defined | Counsel |
| 13 | Signature | All parties signed | Founder + Prospect |
| 14 | Kickoff scheduled | Onboarding day-1 meeting on calendar | Founder |

If signature is not in hand by Day 14, escalate or stop. Do not let the
deal drift — drift is the strongest signal that the workload is not real.

## Deliverables Per Step

### Discovery call output (template, captured in pipeline doc)

```
Prospect:        ____
Domain:          (Aegis | Vessels | Terra | Carlota Jo | Command | CORTEX | flagship)
Real workload:   ____
Today's pain (time saved / $ saved / risk reduced): ____
Decision-maker: ____ (title, sponsor)
Budget cycle:   ____
Data: real-under-DPA | synthetic | n/a
Next step:      Tailored demo on (date)
```

### Tailored demo prep checklist

- [ ] New workspace created in staging
- [ ] Seeded with at least one realistic record per domain object touched
- [ ] Demo script reviewed against current build (no roadmap features)
- [ ] Proof-chain entry from a sample decision available to show
- [ ] One "we don't know" surface identified to demonstrate honesty
- [ ] Recording consent confirmed

### Follow-up email template (≤200 words)

1. One sentence restating their workload
2. One sentence on the metric the demo showed (with the number)
3. Pilot scope (single domain, one team, 60 days, named users count)
4. Pilot price (a real, small number — never zero)
5. Two-line "what happens next" with dates
6. A single CTA — "Reply 'go' to start counsel paperwork."

## Pilot Pricing Defaults

| Engagement | Default | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| 60-day single-domain pilot | $25,000 | Discountable to $15,000 floor with reference-call commitment |
| 90-day multi-domain pilot | $50,000 | Two domains max; founder-led |
| Enterprise diligence pilot | $0 + reimbursed legal | Reserved for named-account land plays per `enterprise-evaluation-flow.md` |

Pricing is intentionally below enterprise list. The pilot is a learning
contract; the post-pilot annual is where margin lives.

## Hand-Off to Onboarding

On signature day, the founder must:

1. Open `partner-first-14-days.md` and copy the day-by-day checklist
   into the partner's shared workspace doc.
2. Provision the production workspace (procedure in
   `manual-console-actions-master.md`).
3. Send the customer launch pack (`customer-launch-pack.md`).
4. Schedule the four onboarding sessions.

## Conversion Targets

| Funnel stage | Target conversion to next |
|--------------|---------------------------|
| Discovery → Demo | 70% |
| Demo → Pilot agreement sent | 50% |
| Agreement sent → Signed | 70% |
| Signed pilot → Annual contract | 50% (Year 1 target) |

These numbers are honest founder-stage targets, not benchmarks. They are
the levers tracked in `founder-pipeline-dashboard-spec.md`.
