# Founder Operating Rhythm

Phase F · SZL Scale, Close, and Operate Pass
Updated: 2026-04-16

## Purpose

The weekly cadence the founder operates by. The point is not the
schedule — it is to be unsurprised by anything that matters and to
know what to drop.

## The Week

| Day | Block | Activity |
|-----|-------|----------|
| Monday AM | 09:00–10:00 | Pipeline review (per `founder-pipeline-dashboard-spec.md`); identify stalled list; commit the week's must-close items |
| Monday AM | 10:00–11:00 | Telemetry weekly review (Tier 3 dashboard from `telemetry-priority-matrix.md`); flag anything trending wrong |
| Monday PM | — | Customer time only — no internal meetings |
| Tuesday AM | 09:00–10:00 | Engineering pairing on the week's release-train scope |
| Tuesday | 10:00–17:00 | Customer time + product time (two design partner sessions max) |
| Wednesday AM | 09:00–11:00 | Strategic block — investor updates, hiring, partnerships |
| Wednesday PM | — | Customer time |
| Thursday | All day | Customer time + demo days |
| Friday AM | 09:00–10:00 | Release review (per `founder-release-approval.md` if a release is queued) |
| Friday AM | 10:00–11:00 | Friday writeup — week's wins, losses, decisions; updates `what-changed.md` |
| Friday PM | — | Strategic reading and admin |

This is a target schedule. Customer demands always win over internal
blocks. The Monday AM blocks are the only fully protected windows.

| Time | Action |

## Standing Meetings

| Cadence | Meeting | Attendees | Length |
|---------|---------|-----------|--------|
| Weekly | Engineering 1:1s | Founder + each direct | 30 min |
| Weekly | Product / engineering planning | Founder + engineering lead | 60 min |
| Weekly | Per design partner working session | Founder + partner | 30–60 min |
| Bi-weekly | Counsel sync | Founder + counsel | 30 min |
| Monthly | Investor update | Founder | Async memo |
| Monthly | Telemetry deep-dive | Founder + engineering | 60 min |
| Quarterly | Roadmap reset | Founder + engineering | Half day |
| Quarterly | Buyer FAQ + diligence map review | Founder + counsel | 60 min |

## Reading List per Week

These are non-negotiable founder reads each week:

1. The pipeline doc / dashboard
2. Tier 1 + Tier 2 alarm history
3. Every `incident-triage-model.md` postmortem produced this week
4. Every release note added to `what-changed.md` this week
5. Every partner Slack Connect channel — read every message even if no
   action

## Things the Founder Will Not Do

This list grows. Today:

- Run sales calls below the VP / Director level without a sponsor
- Ship code directly to Production without the release train
- Approve a feature only one design partner asked for
- Take more than three concurrent design partners
- Add a Tier 1 alarm without retiring one
- Take credit for an engineering decision in writing
- Promise a date in a customer conversation without engineering
  confirmation in the same channel

## Weekly Reset Question

Every Friday writeup answers three questions in writing:

1. What is the most important thing the company learned this week?
2. What is the one thing that would have made next week 10x easier
   if it had been done this week?
3. What did I avoid this week that I should not have?

The answers go in the Friday writeup file in the founder's working
notes. Patterns from the answers feed the quarterly roadmap reset.

## Anti-Patterns

- Treating Mondays as catch-up days — Mondays set the week
- Skipping the Friday writeup because the week was busy — busy weeks
  are exactly the ones that need the writeup
- Letting the standing meetings expand — keep all 1:1s at 30 min
- Letting the customer time blocks become "open office hours" — they
  are scheduled customer working sessions, not drop-in
- Skipping the pager check before sleep on a release day
