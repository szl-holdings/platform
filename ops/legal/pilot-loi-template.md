## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to enter into a structured pilot ("**Pilot**") in which Partner evaluates the SZL Holdings platform ("**Platform**") in Partner's real operating environment, and SZL receives feedback to refine the Platform for Partner's domain.

This Letter of Intent ("**LOI**") records the Parties' good-faith intent to proceed and the framework for the Pilot. Except for Sections 4 (Confidentiality), 5 (Data Handling Summary), 8 (Costs and No Obligation), and 9 (Governing Law), this LOI is **not legally binding** and is not a contract for services. A separate Pilot Agreement or Master Services Agreement will govern any production use.

## 2. Scope of the Pilot

- **Domain pack(s):** [DOMAIN PACK — e.g., Aegis, Vessels, Terra, PRISM Counsel]
- **Users in scope:** Up to [NUMBER] users at Partner
- **Pilot start date:** [START DATE]
- **Pilot duration:** [60 / 90] days from start date
- **Designated executive sponsor (Partner):** [NAME, TITLE]
- **Designated technical POC (Partner):** [NAME, TITLE]
- **Designated lead (SZL):** [NAME, TITLE]

The Parties will agree the Pilot success metrics in writing within [10] business days of the Pilot start date, in line with the framework described in `design-partner-offer.md`.

## 3. Commercials During Pilot

The Pilot is provided **at no fee** to Partner during the Pilot period, in exchange for Partner's engagement, feedback, and good-faith referenceability discussion at the 90-day mark. Out-of-pocket expenses (e.g., third-party subscriptions Partner already pays for) are not reimbursed by either Party.

Design partner pricing for any post-Pilot production agreement is described in Section 7.

## 4. Confidentiality (Binding)

Each Party will treat the other's non-public information disclosed in connection with the Pilot — including Platform capabilities, roadmap, pricing, and Partner's operational data and feedback — as **Confidential Information**, and will:

(a) use it solely for the Pilot;
(b) protect it with at least the same care it uses for its own confidential information of similar sensitivity, and no less than reasonable care; and
(c) not disclose it to any third party except to employees, contractors, and advisors with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 4.

These obligations survive for [3] years from the date of disclosure. They do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information, or is required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to the disclosing Party where legally permitted).

## 5. Data Handling Summary (Binding)

During the Pilot:

- Partner remains the controller of any personal data it provides to the Platform. SZL acts as processor for that personal data and processes it only on Partner's documented instructions and as needed to operate the Platform.
- SZL will not sell Partner data and will not use Partner production data to train models for any third party.
- SZL may use **aggregated, de-identified** operational telemetry to improve the Platform.
- A separate Data Processing Agreement (DPA) will be executed before the Pilot start date if (i) Partner processes personal data of EU/UK/Swiss residents through the Platform, or (ii) Partner's procurement or applicable law requires it.
- At Pilot end or termination, SZL will return or delete Partner data within [30] days on Partner's written request, except where retention is required by law.

## 6. Pilot Success Metrics (To Be Defined)

The Parties will document Pilot success metrics in a separate written attachment within [10] business days of the Pilot start date. Each metric will include:

(a) baseline value at Pilot start;
(b) target value at 90 days; and
(c) measurement method and attribution caveats.

Final assessment against these metrics will occur at the 90-day review meeting.

## 7. Commercial Path Acknowledgment

The Parties acknowledge in good faith that:

- The Pilot is intended to lead to a commercial discussion at the 60-day checkpoint and a decision at the 90-day review.
- If the Parties agree to proceed to production, design partner pricing applies (a minimum [30%] discount versus standard commercial pricing for the relevant domain pack), locked for [12] months from production start.
- A Master Services Agreement and Order Form will govern any production use; this LOI does **not** itself create any obligation to sign a production agreement.
- If the Parties do not proceed, the Pilot will close formally with a written lessons document. Neither Party owes the other a fee or penalty for non-conversion.

## 8. Costs and No Obligation (Binding)

Each Party bears its own costs in connection with the Pilot and any related negotiations. Nothing in this LOI obligates either Party to enter into any further agreement, exclusivity, or commercial commitment, except as expressly set out in the Binding sections of this LOI.

## 9. Governing Law (Binding)

This LOI and the Binding sections are governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY], without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in [VENUE] for any dispute arising under the Binding sections.

## 10. Term

This LOI is effective on the latest signature date below and remains in effect until the earlier of (a) execution of a Pilot Agreement or Master Services Agreement covering the same scope, (b) the end of the Pilot period defined in Section 2, or (c) termination by either Party on [10] days' written notice. The Binding sections survive termination as stated.
