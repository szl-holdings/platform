**DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT**

This Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") forms part of the Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") between **SZL Holdings** ("**Processor**" or "**SZL**") and **[CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME]** ("**Controller**" or "**Customer**"), together the "**Parties**".

**Effective Date:** [DATE]

In any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement regarding the processing of Personal Data, this DPA controls.

---

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement or in applicable Data Protection Laws.

- "**Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations applicable to the Processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("**GDPR**"), the UK GDPR, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, and the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act ("**CCPA/CPRA**"), each as amended.
- "**Personal Data**", "**Process/Processing**", "**Data Subject**", "**Controller**", "**Processor**", "**Subprocessor**", "**Personal Data Breach**", and "**Supervisory Authority**" have the meanings given in GDPR (or the equivalent under other applicable Data Protection Laws).
- "**Customer Personal Data**" means Personal Data SZL Processes on Customer's behalf under the Agreement, as described in Annex A.
- "**Standard Contractual Clauses**" or "**SCCs**" means the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission (Decision 2021/914) for transfers of Personal Data to third countries, and, where applicable, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum issued by the UK ICO.

## 2. Scope and Roles

### 2.1
With respect to Customer Personal Data, Customer is the Controller and SZL is the Processor. Where Customer is itself a Processor for a third-party Controller, SZL acts as Sub-processor and references in this DPA to "Controller" should be read accordingly.

### 2.2
Each Party will comply with its obligations under applicable Data Protection Laws.

### 2.3
SZL will Process Customer Personal Data only:
(a) on documented instructions from Customer, including those set out in the Agreement, in this DPA, and in the configuration of the Services; and
(b) as required by applicable law, in which case SZL will inform Customer of that legal requirement before Processing, unless prohibited by law.

If SZL believes any instruction breaches Data Protection Laws, SZL will notify Customer without undue delay.

## 3. Details of Processing

The subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, types of Personal Data, and categories of Data Subjects are described in **Annex A**.

## 4. Confidentiality

SZL will ensure that personnel authorized to Process Customer Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations and have received appropriate data protection training.

## 5. Security

### 5.1
SZL will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, taking into account the state of the art, costs, and the nature of the Processing. The measures in effect are described in **Annex B**.

### 5.2
SZL will regularly test and evaluate the effectiveness of these measures and may update them, provided the level of security is not materially decreased.

## 6. Subprocessors

### 6.1
Customer authorizes SZL to engage Subprocessors to Process Customer Personal Data, subject to this Section 6. The current list of Subprocessors is set out in **Annex C**.

### 6.2
SZL will impose on each Subprocessor data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

### 6.3
SZL will provide notice of any new or replacement Subprocessor at least [30] days in advance (by email or via a designated subprocessor page). Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds within [15] days of notice. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection, Customer may terminate the affected Services with a pro-rata refund of prepaid, unused fees as Customer's exclusive remedy.

### 6.4
SZL remains liable for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors with respect to Customer Personal Data to the same extent as if performed by SZL.

## 7. Data Subject Requests

### 7.1
Taking into account the nature of the Processing, SZL will provide reasonable assistance to enable Customer to respond to requests from Data Subjects to exercise their rights under Data Protection Laws (including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection).

### 7.2
If SZL receives a Data Subject request directly relating to Customer Personal Data, SZL will, unless legally prohibited, promptly forward it to Customer and not respond except on Customer's instruction.

## 8. Personal Data Breach

### 8.1
SZL will notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within [72] hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

### 8.2
SZL will provide Customer with information reasonably needed for Customer to comply with its notification obligations, including (to the extent known): the nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records affected, likely consequences, and measures taken or proposed.

### 8.3
SZL's notification of, and response to, a Personal Data Breach is not an admission of fault or liability.

## 9. Data Protection Impact Assessments and Prior Consultation

SZL will provide reasonable assistance to Customer with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities, where required by Data Protection Laws and taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to SZL.

## 10. International Data Transfers

### 10.1
Where Processing of Customer Personal Data involves a transfer outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland to a country without an adequacy decision, the Parties agree that the Standard Contractual Clauses are incorporated into this DPA by reference and will apply to such transfers, with:

- Module Two (Controller to Processor) where Customer is a Controller and SZL is a Processor;
- Module Three (Processor to Sub-processor) where Customer is a Processor and SZL is a Sub-processor;
- Annex I, II, and III of the SCCs populated by Annexes A, B, and C of this DPA respectively;
- Clause 7 (docking clause): not applicable;
- Clause 9: Option 2 (general written authorization), with the notice period in Section 6.3;
- Clause 11: optional independent dispute resolution language not selected;
- Clause 17: governed by the law of [EU MEMBER STATE — e.g., Ireland];
- Clause 18: courts of [EU MEMBER STATE — e.g., Ireland] for disputes.

### 10.2
For UK transfers, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum applies and is incorporated by reference, with the SCCs as the approved EU SCCs above.

### 10.3
For Swiss transfers, the SCCs apply with the adjustments published by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

## 11. Audit

### 11.1
SZL will make available to Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, primarily through then-current third-party audit reports (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001) where available, and security documentation.

### 11.2
Where Data Protection Laws require, Customer may, on at least [30] days' prior written notice, conduct an audit (including by an independent auditor mutually agreed and bound by confidentiality), no more than once per [12] months, during business hours, in a manner that does not unreasonably disrupt SZL's operations and respects the confidentiality and security of other customers' data. The Parties will agree the scope in advance. Customer bears its own audit costs and reimburses SZL for reasonable assistance beyond [8] hours.

## 12. Deletion or Return of Customer Personal Data

On termination or expiry of the Services, or earlier on Customer's written request, SZL will, within [30] days, delete or return Customer Personal Data, at Customer's choice, except where retention is required by law. SZL may retain backup copies subject to its standard retention schedule, with continued confidentiality and security obligations.

## 13. CCPA/CPRA Specific Terms

To the extent SZL Processes Personal Information of California residents on Customer's behalf, SZL is a "service provider" or "contractor" (as applicable) under the CCPA/CPRA. SZL will not:

(a) sell or share the Personal Information;
(b) retain, use, or disclose the Personal Information outside the direct business relationship or for any purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Agreement;
(c) combine the Personal Information with personal information from other sources, except as permitted by the CCPA/CPRA.

SZL will notify Customer if it determines it can no longer meet its obligations under the CCPA/CPRA.

## 14. Liability

The liability of each Party under this DPA is subject to the limitations of liability set out in the Agreement. Nothing in this DPA limits liability that cannot be limited under applicable law (including, where applicable, liability to Data Subjects under the SCCs).

## 15. Term and Termination

This DPA takes effect on the Effective Date and remains in effect for as long as SZL Processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement. Sections that by their nature should survive (including Sections 4, 5, 8.3, 12, 13, and 14) survive termination.

## 16. Order of Precedence

In any conflict between this DPA, the SCCs, and the Agreement on Personal Data matters: the SCCs prevail over this DPA; this DPA prevails over the Agreement.

## Annex A — Description of Processing

**Subject matter:** Provision of the Services described in the Agreement.

**Duration:** For the term of the Agreement and any post-termination retention period set out in Section 12.

**Nature and purpose of Processing:** Hosting, storing, transmitting, and processing Customer Personal Data so Customer and its authorized users can use the Services (including decision-support workflows, signal logging, and reporting).

**Types of Personal Data (typical):**
- Business contact data of Customer's authorized users (name, work email, work phone, role, organization)
- Authentication data (account identifiers, hashed credentials, session tokens)
- Usage data (logs of actions taken in the Services, IP address, device and browser metadata)
- Operational content Customer chooses to upload, which may include Personal Data depending on Customer's configuration (e.g., names mentioned in matter notes, voyage logs, or counterparty records)

**Special categories of Personal Data:** Not intended. Customer should not upload special-category data unless the Parties have agreed additional safeguards in writing.

**Categories of Data Subjects:**
- Customer's authorized users and personnel
- Customer's counterparties, contacts, and other individuals referenced in the operational content Customer uploads

**Frequency of transfer:** Continuous, for the duration of the Services.

**Retention:** Per Section 12 and Customer's configuration of the Services.

## Annex B — Technical and Organizational Measures

> **To be completed and reviewed by SZL security/engineering before signature. The list below is illustrative; remove any control that is not actually implemented and add any that are.**

**Access control:**
- Role-based access control with least-privilege defaults
- Multi-factor authentication required for SZL personnel access to production systems
- Documented joiner/mover/leaver process with access reviews at least quarterly

**Encryption:**
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) for all external traffic
- Encryption at rest for production data stores

**Network and infrastructure security:**
- Production environment isolated from development and staging
- Network-level firewalling and security group controls
- Centralized logging and monitoring of production systems

**Application security:**
- Secure development lifecycle, including code review and dependency scanning
- Vulnerability management program with prioritized remediation
- Periodic penetration testing by a qualified third party

**Operational security:**
- Documented incident response process with defined roles and escalation
- Backup and restore procedures with periodic restore testing
- Change management for production deployments

**People and governance:**
- Background checks for personnel with production access, where lawful
- Security and privacy awareness training on hire and at least annually
- Confidentiality obligations for all personnel and contractors
