**MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT**

**Effective Date:** [DATE]
**SZL Holdings** ("**SZL**"), a [STATE/COUNTRY] [ENTITY TYPE], at [ADDRESS]
**[CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME]** ("**Customer**"), a [STATE/COUNTRY] [ENTITY TYPE], at [ADDRESS]

This Master Services Agreement ("**Agreement**") governs Customer's access to and use of the SZL Holdings platform and related services (the "**Services**"). Specific commercial terms are set out in one or more Order Forms that reference this Agreement. In any conflict between this Agreement and an Order Form, the Order Form controls only for that engagement.

---

## 1. Services

### 1.1 Provision of the Services
SZL will make the Services described in each Order Form available to Customer during the applicable subscription term, in accordance with this Agreement and any Service Level Addendum referenced in the Order Form.

### 1.2 Access and accounts
Customer is responsible for (a) provisioning and managing user accounts within the seat counts purchased, (b) safeguarding credentials, and (c) all activity occurring under its accounts.

### 1.3 Support
Standard support is included as described in the then-current support documentation. Enterprise support tiers, where purchased, are governed by the SLA Addendum.

### 1.4 Changes to the Services
SZL may update the Services from time to time. SZL will not materially decrease the core functionality of a domain pack Customer has purchased during the then-current subscription term without Customer's consent.

## 2. Acceptable Use

Customer will not, and will not permit any user to: (a) use the Services in violation of applicable law; (b) reverse engineer, decompile, or attempt to derive source code of the Services, except to the extent expressly permitted by law notwithstanding this restriction; (c) resell, sublicense, or provide the Services to any third party as a standalone offering; (d) upload malicious code or attempt to interfere with the Services; or (e) use the Services to build a competing product. SZL may suspend access on reasonable notice if Customer's use materially threatens the security or integrity of the Services, and will restore access promptly once the issue is resolved.

## 3. Fees, Invoicing, and Taxes

### 3.1 Fees
Customer will pay the fees set out in each Order Form. Unless otherwise stated, fees are in [USD] and are non-refundable except as expressly provided.

### 3.2 Invoicing and payment
SZL will invoice Customer per the Order Form. Invoices are due within [30] days of the invoice date. Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of [1.0%] per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

### 3.3 Taxes
Fees exclude all taxes. Customer is responsible for sales, use, VAT, and similar taxes, excluding taxes on SZL's net income.

### 3.4 Renewal and price changes
Subscriptions renew for successive terms equal to the initial term unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least [90] days before the end of the then-current term. SZL may adjust renewal pricing on at least [60] days' written notice; design partner lock pricing, where applicable, is honored for the period stated in the Order Form.

## 4. Data and Confidentiality

### 4.1 Customer Data
"**Customer Data**" means data, content, and materials Customer or its users submit to or generate through the Services. As between the Parties, Customer owns all right, title, and interest in Customer Data.

### 4.2 License to Customer Data
Customer grants SZL a non-exclusive, worldwide license to host, process, transmit, and display Customer Data solely as necessary to provide the Services and as permitted by this Agreement. SZL may use **aggregated, de-identified** data derived from Customer Data to operate, secure, and improve the Services. SZL will not use Customer Data to train models for any third party and will not sell Customer Data.

### 4.3 Data Processing
Where SZL processes personal data on Customer's behalf, the Data Processing Agreement (DPA) attached to or referenced by an Order Form applies. The DPA controls in any conflict with this Section 4 in respect of personal data.

### 4.4 Security
SZL will maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data, as further described in the DPA's Technical & Organizational Measures Annex (where executed) or in SZL's then-current security overview.

### 4.5 Confidentiality
Each Party will use the other's Confidential Information only to perform under this Agreement, will protect it with at least reasonable care, and will limit disclosure to those with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective. Confidentiality obligations survive for [3] years after termination, except for trade secrets, which remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law. Standard exclusions apply for information that is public through no fault of the receiving Party, independently developed, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice where permitted).

### 4.6 Return or deletion of Customer Data
On termination or written request, SZL will, within [30] days, return or delete Customer Data, except where retention is required by law. SZL may retain backup copies subject to its standard retention schedule, with continued confidentiality obligations.

## 5. Intellectual Property

### 5.1 SZL IP
SZL and its licensors own all right, title, and interest in the Services, the underlying platform, and all related intellectual property, including any improvements derived from aggregated, de-identified data. No rights are granted except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

### 5.2 Feedback
Customer may, but is not required to, provide feedback on the Services. SZL may use feedback without obligation, provided SZL does not identify Customer as the source without consent.

## 6. Warranties and Disclaimer

### 6.1 Mutual warranties
Each Party warrants it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

### 6.2 SZL warranty
SZL warrants the Services will perform materially in accordance with the documentation. Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is, at SZL's option, repair of the Services or termination of the affected Order Form with a pro-rata refund of prepaid, unused fees.

### 6.3 Disclaimer
EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED, THE SERVICES ARE PROVIDED "AS IS" AND SZL DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT. SZL DOES NOT WARRANT THAT THE SERVICES WILL BE UNINTERRUPTED OR ERROR-FREE. **THE SERVICES PROVIDE DECISION SUPPORT; THEY DO NOT REPLACE PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT, AND CUSTOMER REMAINS RESPONSIBLE FOR ANY DECISION OR ACTION TAKEN ON THE BASIS OF THE SERVICES.**

## 7. Indemnification

### 7.1 By SZL
SZL will defend Customer against any third-party claim that the Services, as provided by SZL and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe a valid intellectual property right, and will pay damages finally awarded or amounts in a settlement approved by SZL. SZL may, at its option, modify the Services to be non-infringing, obtain a license, or terminate the affected Order Form with a pro-rata refund. SZL has no obligation for claims arising from (a) Customer Data, (b) Customer's modification or combination of the Services, or (c) use after notice to stop.

### 7.2 By Customer
Customer will defend SZL against third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services in violation of this Agreement, and will pay damages finally awarded or amounts in a settlement approved by Customer.

### 7.3 Procedure
The indemnified Party must (a) promptly notify the indemnifying Party, (b) give the indemnifying Party sole control of defense and settlement (without admission of liability against the indemnified Party), and (c) reasonably cooperate.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR (i) BREACH OF SECTION 4 (DATA AND CONFIDENTIALITY), (ii) INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 7, (iii) CUSTOMER'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS, OR (iv) GROSS NEGLIGENCE, WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, OR FRAUD:

(a) **NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, OR DATA**, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY; AND

(b) **EACH PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY IS CAPPED AT THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THE APPLICABLE ORDER FORM IN THE [12] MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.**

These limitations apply regardless of the form of action, in contract, tort, or otherwise.

## 9. Term and Termination

### 9.1 Term
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Order Forms have expired or been terminated, unless earlier terminated as set out below.

### 9.2 Termination for cause
Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form on [30] days' written notice if the other Party materially breaches and fails to cure within the notice period. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other becomes insolvent, makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or is the subject of bankruptcy proceedings not dismissed within [60] days.

### 9.3 Effect of termination
On termination: (a) Customer's right to use the Services ends; (b) accrued fees remain payable; (c) Section 4 (return or deletion of Customer Data) applies; and (d) provisions that by their nature should survive (including Sections 4.5, 5, 6.3, 7, 8, 10, and 11) survive.

## 10. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of [STATE/COUNTRY], without regard to conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in [VENUE]. Before filing suit (other than for injunctive relief or unpaid fees), the Parties will attempt good-faith resolution between executives for [30] days.

## 11. General

### 11.1 Notices
Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses on the cover page (or as updated by written notice). Email notice is permitted to the legal contact identified in the Order Form for routine notices; notices of breach or termination must also be sent by recognized courier or registered mail.

### 11.2 Assignment
Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets, with prompt notice to the other Party.

### 11.3 Subcontractors and subprocessors
SZL may use subcontractors and subprocessors to provide the Services and remains responsible for their performance. Subprocessor terms are addressed in the DPA where applicable.

### 11.4 Force majeure
Neither Party is liable for delay or failure due to causes beyond its reasonable control, except for payment obligations.

### 11.5 Publicity
Neither Party will use the other's name or marks without prior written consent, except SZL may include Customer's name and logo in a customer list with Customer's reasonable approval.

### 11.6 Independent contractors
The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship.

### 11.7 Entire agreement; order of precedence
This Agreement, together with any Order Form, DPA, and SLA Addendum, is the entire agreement and supersedes all prior discussions on the subject. Order of precedence: (1) DPA (for personal data matters), (2) Order Form, (3) SLA Addendum, (4) this Agreement.

### 11.8 Amendments and waivers
Amendments must be in writing and signed by both Parties. A failure to enforce any right is not a waiver.

### 11.9 Severability
If any provision is unenforceable, it is modified to the minimum extent necessary to be enforceable, and the rest of the Agreement remains in effect.

### 11.10 Counterparts and electronic signature
This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
