# Legal Templates

**Last updated:** April 2026
**Owner:** Founder / SZL Holdings

---

## Status

> **All templates in this directory are FOUNDER DRAFTS. They have NOT been reviewed by counsel. Do not send any of these documents to a prospect, partner, or customer for signature until a qualified attorney licensed in the relevant jurisdiction has reviewed and revised them.**

These drafts exist to unblock commercial conversations: they let the founder share concrete structure with a prospect, give partner counsel something to redline against, and shorten the path from verbal interest to signed pilot. They are not a substitute for legal advice.

---

## How to use

1. Identify which document fits the stage of the conversation (LOI for pilot kickoff, MSA for production, DPA when partner processes personal data or procurement requires it).
2. Copy the template into a working document (Google Docs, Notion, Word). Do not edit the template in place — keep the canonical version in this repo clean.
3. Fill every placeholder marked with `[BRACKETS]`. Do not send a document with unfilled placeholders.
4. Send the working document to counsel for review before sharing externally.
5. After counsel review, mark the working document with the review date and reviewing attorney for the audit trail.

---

## Counsel review checklist

Before any of these templates are sent to a counterparty, counsel should at minimum confirm:

- Governing law and venue match the SZL Holdings entity of record
- Liability cap and indemnification language is appropriate for the deal size
- Data handling language is consistent with the technical reality of the platform
- IP ownership clauses do not conflict with the platform's underlying license model
- Termination and data return mechanics are operationally feasible
- DPA Annexes (Subprocessors, Technical & Organizational Measures) reflect the actual subprocessor list and security controls
