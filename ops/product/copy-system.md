## Tagline

**Primary:** Governed Decision Infrastructure  
**Brand promise:** Enterprise operators get clarity on what is happening, what to do next, who is responsible, and whether the action is safe — all in one governed decision surface.

Do not use "business observability" as the primary company positioning. It is accurate for Lyte specifically but undersells the platform company story.

## Headline system

### Company-level headlines

These communicate SZL Holdings as a platform company.

- "The governed decision layer for enterprise operations."
- "One intelligence backbone. Every high-consequence domain."
- "Connect what's observable to what's executable — under governance."
- "Built for operators who can't afford to be wrong."

### Platform-level headlines (Lyte + Alloy)

- "Signal → Context → Action → Proof."
- "The execution fabric that closes the loop."
- "Alloy routes action. Lyte makes it visible. Proof Chain makes it defensible."

### Domain pack headlines

Use the format: `[Domain Pack] is the [domain] domain pack — [what it does for whom].`

- "Aegis is the security & defense domain pack — SOC command, XDR, and AI triage for high-consequence environments."
- "Vessels is the maritime domain pack — fleet command, AIS telemetry, and voyage economics for fleet operators."
- "Terra is the real estate intelligence domain pack — distress signals, deal pipeline, and ownership graph for NYC operators."
- "PRISM Counsel is the legal intelligence domain pack — matter command, deadline tracking, and governed demand for litigation teams."

## Trust copy

Lead with what is enforced architecturally, not what is aspirational.

**Accurate statements:**
- "Human-in-the-loop enforced at the workflow layer, not the UI layer."
- "All AI recommendations include model identity, source citations, and confidence score."
- "Proof Chain records every action, approval, and inference — append-only, tamper-resistant."
- "AI agents are advisory-only. No consequential action without explicit human approval."
- "TLS 1.3 in transit, AES-256 at rest."
- "HMAC-signed WebSocket tickets with 5-minute TTL."

**Do not claim:**
- SOC 2 Type II (targeted post-funding — not achieved)
- ISO 27001 (not achieved)
- GDPR compliance (not evaluated)
- Any enterprise certification not in-hand

## Tone guardrails

**Always:**
- Active voice
- Outcome-first headlines
- Pair every AI capability with governance commitment
- Use "domain pack" not "vertical" or "product"

**Never:**
- "Leverage," "synergize," "revolutionize," "disrupt"
- Describe the platform as "AI-powered" without noting governance model
- Use stock superlatives: "cutting-edge," "next-generation," "world-class"
- Claim enterprise certifications not yet achieved

## Page-specific copy notes

### Homepage

The homepage is for the enterprise buyer, security reviewer, investor, and design partner simultaneously. It must communicate:
1. **Company** — SZL Holdings is a platform company building governed decision infrastructure
2. **Platform** — Alloy is the intelligence backbone; Lyte is the command surface
3. **Products** — Domain packs extend the same governance layer to specific domains

Do not open with a product benefit. Open with the company/platform positioning.

### Platform page

Lead with the architecture, not the feature list. Show the three layers (Command Surfaces, Execution Fabric, Domain Packs) before explaining individual products.

### Domain pack pages

Lead with the domain context: "What is broken in [domain] that this fixes?" Then explain how the Alloy/Lyte backbone handles it with domain-specific intelligence.

### Trust Center

Lead with what is architecturally enforced, not what is aspirational. Never claim certifications not in-hand.
