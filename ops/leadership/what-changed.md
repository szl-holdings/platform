## What These Documents Establish

### A category position
Before this pass: SZL had a strong architectural moat (`MOAT_MAP.md`, `PLATFORM_PRIMITIVES.md`, `CATEGORY_POSITIONING.md`) and strong commercial documents (`PRODUCT_PACKAGING.md`, `GO_TO_MARKET_MOTION.md`, `SALES_NARRATIVE.md`). Missing was a single canonical category sentence and a coherent narrative architecture that connects every founder-led conversation.

After this pass: The category sentence is canonical. The narrative architecture is six elements. Every conversation type has a talk track. Every commodity AI phrase has an approved replacement.

### A founder voice
Before this pass: The founder voice was implicit, scattered across multiple documents.

After this pass: `founder-message-pack.md` is the compressed founder voice across five conversation contexts. Stephen does not need to invent the language each time; the language is built and tested.

### A discipline
Before this pass: There was no enforcement mechanism against commodity AI drift.

After this pass: `no-commodity-ai-language.md` is the enforceable style guide. Every public-surface change is reviewable against it.

### A verdict
Before this pass: It was unclear whether SZL was in a one-of-one position or a strong-but-replicable position.

After this pass: The verdict in `one-of-one-readiness-verdict.md` is GO with a defined window.
