# Weekly Partner Review — SZL Holdings

**Phase:** G · **Audience:** Founder, design partner ops · **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-16

---

## Purpose

The Weekly Partner Review is the heartbeat of every design partner pilot. It is a 60-minute, structured, recurring meeting between the founder and the buyer's ops lead during the 90-day pilot. It exists to:

1. Keep the pilot moving
2. Catch problems early
3. Capture proof artifacts continuously
4. Build the relationship that becomes a reference

Skipped weekly reviews are the single strongest leading indicator of pilot failure.

---

## Cadence

- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Duration:** 60 minutes (45 min agenda + 15 min buffer)
- **Day:** Same day every week (Tuesday or Wednesday recommended)
- **Time:** Same time every week (early afternoon buyer's TZ recommended)
- **Format:** Video call preferred; in-person occasional; phone as fallback

The cadence is non-negotiable. Both sides commit to this in the design partner agreement.

---

## Attendees

### From buyer (required)
- **Ops lead** (the operational owner of the pilot — typically a director or VP-level)
- **Operator(s)** (the people actually using the platform; rotate if there are multiple)

### From buyer (optional)
- Economic decision-maker (CFO, COO, principal) — invite for milestone reviews (Day 30, 60, 75, 90)

### From SZL (required)
- **Founder** (until pilot 15+; then CS lead with founder for milestone reviews)

### From SZL (optional)
- Product or engineering team member if a specific platform topic is on the agenda

---

## Agenda (45 minutes structured)

```
0:00 - 0:05  Open: state of the loop this week
0:05 - 0:20  Operational review: what happened, what worked, what didn't
0:20 - 0:30  Decision review: walk one specific decision end-to-end
0:30 - 0:40  Roadblocks and asks
0:40 - 0:45  Next week commitments
```

### Open (5 min)
- Founder: 1-2 sentence summary of platform usage this week
- Ops lead: 1-2 sentence summary of how the team felt about the platform this week

### Operational review (15 min)
- Walk through the operational metrics (the success criteria from `pilot-success-criteria.md`)
- Compare to baseline and to last week
- Identify any drift or surprise

### Decision review (10 min)
- Pick one specific decision the platform helped make this week
- Walk it: signal → recommendation → simulation → policy gate → execution → outcome
- Capture: was the recommendation right? did the operator override? what was the outcome?
- This is the source material for case studies and lessons

### Roadblocks and asks (10 min)
- Buyer: what's blocking adoption this week? (Often: integration issue, training gap, operator confusion)
- SZL: what does the founder need from buyer this week? (Often: data access, stakeholder intro, feedback on a feature)

### Next week commitments (5 min)
- 2-3 specific things each side will do in the next 7 days
- Captured in the weekly note (see below)

---

## The Weekly Note

After every weekly review, the founder writes a 4-6 sentence weekly note within 24 hours. The note is:

- **Sent to the buyer** as a written summary
- **Captured in the pilot's lessons file** for proof engine input
- **Referenced in the Day 75 conversion conversation**

### Weekly note template

```
WEEK [N] — [PILOT NAME] — [DATE]

State of the loop:
[1-2 sentences on platform usage and operator engagement this week]

Operational metrics:
[1-2 sentences with key metrics: trajectory vs. baseline]

Notable decision:
[1-2 sentences on the specific decision walked in the review]

Roadblocks:
[1 sentence on what's blocking; null if none]

Commitments next week:
- Buyer: [specific commitment]
- SZL: [specific commitment]

Founder qualitative read: [1 sentence on overall pilot health]
```

---

## Milestone Reviews (Day 30, 60, 75, 90)

Four reviews in the 90-day cycle are upgraded to milestone reviews. They include the economic decision-maker and have an extended agenda.

### Day 30 — First check-in
- Operational metrics review (will we hit Day 90 thresholds?)
- Adoption review (are operators using the platform daily?)
- Roadblock surfacing
- 90-minute meeting; CFO/COO attends if available

### Day 60 — Mid-pilot
- Mid-pilot scope adjustment (if needed)
- Production-readiness signals (which gaps would block production use?)
- Reference-readiness signal (would they be willing to be a reference if pilot succeeds?)
- 90-minute meeting; economic decision-maker attendance recommended

### Day 75 — Conversion conversation
- Pre-conversion review of all metrics
- Conversion conversation per `pilot-vs-production-commercial-model.md`
- 60-minute meeting; economic decision-maker required

### Day 90 — Pilot close
- Final metrics review
- Case study draft walkthrough
- Reference willingness conversation
- Commercial close (if not already closed) OR exit conversation
- 90-minute meeting; economic decision-maker required

## What a Good Weekly Review Looks Like

- Buyer arrives prepared with operator feedback
- Founder arrives prepared with metrics and one specific decision to walk
- Both sides leave with 2-3 specific commitments for the next 7 days
- Founder writes the weekly note within 24 hours
- Buyer references the prior week's note in the next review

---

## What a Failing Weekly Review Looks Like

- Buyer cancels or no-shows (>1 cancellation per month is a problem)
- Operator never attends (their feedback isn't being captured)
- "Nothing new this week" responses (no decisions running through the loop)
- Founder doesn't write the weekly note (no continuity, no proof)
- Roadblocks surfaced but never resolved (engineering or product not responding)

If you see two of these in a row, the pilot is in trouble. Escalate immediately:
- Schedule a founder-to-economic-decision-maker call
- Surface the failure pattern explicitly
- Re-establish the cadence OR exit the pilot

---

## Common Buyer Objections (and Responses)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Can we do every other week?" | "60 days into the pilot, sure. The first 30 days we need weekly to catch issues early." |
| "Can my deputy attend instead?" | "Once a month is fine. We need the ops lead in 3 of 4 meetings to keep the relationship intact." |
| "We don't have anything new to discuss." | "Then let's walk one specific decision from this week. The detail surfaces what's working and what isn't." |
| "Can we make it 30 minutes?" | "We can compress to 45 if we have a tight agenda. Below that and we lose the decision review." |
| "Can we do async via email?" | "Async after the call, yes. The call itself catches things email doesn't." |

---

## Anti-Patterns

- **Skipping the call when the founder is "too busy."** The pilot is the business in the design partner phase. The call is the business.
- **Not writing the weekly note.** No note = no proof artifact = no case study material.
- **Letting the call drift to a status update.** Status update is not a partner review. The decision walk is the heart.
- **Avoiding bad news.** If a metric is off-trajectory, surface it. Avoidance compounds.
- **Treating the call as one-way.** The buyer should be asking SZL for things too. If they aren't, the relationship isn't real yet.

---

*The Weekly Partner Review is the most leveraged hour the founder spends each week during the design partner phase. It is where pilots succeed or fail, where references are built or lost, where proof is captured or evaporates. Run it. Every week. Without exception.*
