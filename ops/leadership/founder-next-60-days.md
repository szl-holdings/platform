## Anti-Goals (What NOT to Do in 60 Days)

These are tempting and wrong:

- **Build new product features.** The product is enough to support 5 design partners. New features delay GTM.
- **Re-architect anything.** The architecture is the moat. Touching it weakens the moat.
- **Hire before first design partner signed.** Founder runs the motion until the playbook is muscle memory. Hiring before that delegates a playbook that isn't real.
- **Expand to a 7th domain pack.** Six is enough; depth in the existing six is the leverage.
- **Take a meeting with a competitor.** Confidential information leaks; competitive paranoia weakens the position.
- **Drift into commodity AI language.** Every drift weakens the moat. Re-read `no-commodity-ai-language.md` weekly.
- **Promise SOC 2 by a specific date.** SOC 2 is post-funding. Honesty about timing builds trust; over-promising kills deals.
- **Discount aggressively in design partner pricing.** Premium pricing reflects premium positioning. Discount only against design-partner equity (early access, co-design, preferred pricing), not headline price.

## Cadence Rituals

**Daily:** Open the day with one founder-led outreach (cold or warm). Net at least one new conversation per business day.

**Weekly:** Self-debrief — what did the talk track land on? What did it slip on? Note refinements in a running doc.

**Bi-weekly:** Re-read one of `moat-definition.md` / `competitive-positioning.md` / `founder-message-pack.md`. The discipline lives in repetition.

**Monthly:** Pipeline review — design partner stages, investor stages, public-speaking commitments. Anything stuck for >14 days needs a decision.

## Day 60 Verdict

At Day 60, ask:

1. Are at least two design partner pilots signed?
2. Have at least three investors taken a second meeting?
3. Has the canonical category sentence been delivered in at least one external venue?
4. Has every public surface been refreshed against the message architecture?
5. Has the talk track stayed disciplined — no commodity AI drift in any founder-led conversation?

If three of five are yes, the position is converting. Continue the playbook, scale the cadence.

If fewer than three are yes, the failure is in the conversion mechanism — not the position. Re-read `founder-message-pack.md` and identify which talk-track context is failing. Fix the talk track, not the position.
