## Why the Window Is Months, Not Years

The lead time for a competitor to close the gap:

| Capability | Estimated competitor lead time |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| Schema-validated decision object model | 6-9 months (engineering + adoption across surfaces) |
| Governance library set usable by any product | 9-12 months (architectural retrofit is harder than greenfield) |
| Six domain packs on shared substrate | 18-24 months minimum (each domain is a serious engineering investment) |
| Unified mobile command surface | 6-9 months (Expo / RN, but data model integration is the long pole) |
| Operator-first command experience | 6-12 months (cultural shift inside the competitor org) |
| Founder-led narrative without commodity drift | Indefinite — depends on founder voice, not engineering |

**Architectural lead time:** 12-18 months for a competitor with intent and capital.

**Narrative lead time:** Indefinite. The narrative is the founder's voice. The competitor needs not just code but a category-articulating founder. That is the harder one to acquire.

The window closes when:
- A hyperscaler decides governance is the wedge and assigns 50 engineers
- A well-funded copilot vendor decides to pivot from prose to schema
- An incumbent (ServiceNow, Splunk, Datadog) decides to acquire a governance startup

None of these have happened in observable signals as of April 2026. They could happen in any quarter.

**Implication:** The next 60-180 days are the window to convert the architectural lead into:
- Signed design partners (commercial validation)
- Public category articulation (narrative claim staked)
- Investor capital (financing the GTM motion)
- First public references (external voices repeating the category)

If those four things happen in the window, the one-of-one position becomes the dominant position. If they do not, the window closes around us.

## What Must Be True for the Verdict to Hold

The verdict is GO **only if** the following remain true:

1. **The category sentence is held disciplined.** No drift into commodity AI language. The `no-commodity-ai-language.md` audit is enforced on every public surface.
2. **The founder uses the talk tracks in every conversation.** Repetition by the founder is what establishes the category. Delegating the message before the playbook is muscle memory weakens the position.
3. **The architecture stays validated.** No re-architecture in the next 60 days. The product is enough for design partners; new features delay GTM.
4. **The honesty discipline holds.** No over-claiming on SOC 2, customer count, revenue, or live data. Pre-revenue is not a weakness when the architecture is real and the path is clear.
5. **The first three design partners get signed.** Pilots are the commercial proof that converts the moat into pipeline.
6. **At least one external venue carries the category sentence.** A podcast, panel, conference, or major editorial piece where the category is named by someone other than the founder — even quoting the founder — is the first external repeat.

If any of these six erode, the verdict downgrades from GO to RECONSIDER. None have eroded as of this writing.
