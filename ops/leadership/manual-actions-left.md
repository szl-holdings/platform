**Action:** In every founder-led conversation — sales call, investor meeting, demo, podcast, panel, dinner — open with the canonical category sentence from `one-sentence-category-statement.md`.

**Why founder-only:** Repetition by the founder establishes the category. Repetition by anyone else delegates the position.

**Cadence:** Every conversation, no exceptions, for the next 90 days.

## What an Agent Cannot Do

For the avoidance of doubt, the following actions in this pass cannot be automated:

- Make a phone call to a design partner candidate
- Take a meeting with a fund partner
- Update a personal LinkedIn profile
- Decide which 5 design partner candidates to outreach
- Approve final copy on the company website
- Speak on a podcast
- Write the founder's email outreach voice
- Decide on commercial terms in a pilot conversation
- Sign a contract

These remain founder actions. The agent's job is to make the founder's voice repeatable; the founder's job is to use that voice in the rooms that close the deals.
