2. For each: confirm exec contact via LinkedIn / public sources, find a warm intro path, validate the hook against current public news.
3. Send the personalized outreach (templates in [OUTREACH_SEQUENCES.md](OUTREACH_SEQUENCES.md)).
4. Log the deal in `/admin/pipeline-command` only when you've made contact.
5. Goal: 6 signed design partners by end of Q2 2026 (Cohort 1).
