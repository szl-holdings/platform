## POST 2 — Lyte (Business Observability)

**Hook:**
By the time a problem surfaces in your metrics, the damage has already compounded.

That's the insight that built Lyte.

---

Revenue doesn't stall overnight. It stalls in slow motion — through approval queues that age out, ownership gaps that nobody owns, pipeline signals that nobody surfaces.

Lyte is the business observability engine that makes those invisible dynamics visible — before they become expensive.

**The core doctrine:**
You can't manage what you can't observe. And you can't fix what you can't explain.

Lyte doesn't just show you that revenue is declining. It shows you *why*, traces the causal chain, and routes the right action to the right owner — with a full audit trail.

This is what we call explainable execution. Signal to decision to action — closed loop.

Lyte is the flagship product in the SZL Holdings portfolio. Every other platform is built on the same observability spine.

If you've ever sat in a QBR wondering how a $2M opportunity quietly fell off the radar, Lyte is the answer to that question.

**Hook:**
214 vessels. One command surface.

Most maritime operators manage their fleets across 6 different tools. We collapsed that into one.

---

Maritime is one of the most data-rich industries in the world — and one of the most operationally fragmented.

Position data lives in one system. Voyage economics in another. Compliance and sanctions screening in a third. Exception management in email.

Vessels changes that.

It's a maritime intelligence platform built for operators who need decisions, not dashboards. Positions, voyage P&L, compliance status, and fleet exceptions — unified under one command surface.

The flagship feature: our voyage exception engine. When a vessel deviates from planned route, arrives late at anchorage, or enters a sanctioned zone, the system surfaces the right signal to the right person — automatically.

We're in conversations with two mid-sized maritime operators currently. The opportunity in this space is real, it's underserved, and the incumbents are slow.

## POST 5 — Terra & PRISM Counsel (Real Estate & Legal)

**Hook:**
Two industries running on spreadsheets and email.

We built intelligence platforms for both.

---

**Terra — Real Estate Intelligence**

"The operating surface for serious real estate."

Terra is built for investors, brokers, and portfolio teams who treat property like an operating business — not a passion project.

It surfaces distressed property opportunities using ownership graph analysis, manages deal pipelines with probability scoring, and gives portfolio teams a unified view of performance, exposure, and exit timing.

The unique insight that drove Terra's design: the best deals don't show up in MLS. They show up in probate filings, distressed liens, and expired listings — if you know where to look and how to move fast.

**PRISM Counsel — Legal Matter Command**

Legal operations have a visibility problem. Matters live in email. Deadlines are tracked in spreadsheets. Exposure accumulates quietly until settlement time.

PRISM Counsel is matter observability and governed legal execution in one platform.

Active matters, total exposure, upcoming deadlines, AI-assisted settlement forecasting, deadline risk queuing, discovery management — all in one surface. For legal teams that can't afford to be reactive.

Both of these markets have legacy incumbents with 20-year-old architecture. The opening for purpose-built intelligence platforms is significant.

**Hook:**
7 days. 11 systems. One architecture.

Here's why this matters for investors.

---

I didn't build a startup.

**1. Structural moat through shared infrastructure**
Every new product launches with authentication, observability, design language, and intelligence already built. The marginal cost of entering domain 11 is a fraction of entering domain 1.
