It must become a flagship command center and launch surface for all ventures, websites, and applications.

- Linear-level clarity, speed, and structured project visibility

- Stripe-level trust, conversion quality, and commercial readiness
- Supabase-level secure auth, permissions, realtime data, and modern backend architecture

DO NOT produce a generic template.

DO produce a premium, elegant, highly intentional product with serious enterprise-grade structure.
