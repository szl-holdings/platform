SECTION 1 — THE COMPANY OPERATING MODEL
==================================================

DEFINE THE COMPANY AS FOUR CONNECTED LAYERS:

LAYER 1 — OBSERVE
Capture system activity, workflow state, entity status, risk indicators, and business events.

LAYER 2 — UNDERSTAND
Correlate signals, map dependencies, surface causality, and explain what matters.

LAYER 3 — DECIDE
Prioritize actions, produce recommendations, score confidence, and frame options.

LAYER 4 — EXECUTE
Route work, escalate issues, trigger workflows, audit actions, and track outcomes.

EVERY APP MUST MAP TO THIS MODEL.
