CORE PRODUCT MODULES
Build the following modules into the app:

1. COMMAND OVERVIEW
Top-level operating summary.
Must include:
- risk at a glance
- money/value at risk
- approval bottlenecks
- ownership gaps
- stalled workflows
- forecast drift
- top actions required
- signal trend overview

2. SIGNALS FEED
A live-style feed of observed business/operational issues.
Each signal card must include:
- title
- severity
- affected function
- summary
- why it matters
- owner
- recommended action
- time detected
- business value at risk
- ability to click into deeper detail

3. INSIGHT NARRATIVE RAIL
This is a signature Lyte element.
A right-side or dedicated panel that translates raw signals into human-readable operating insight.
Examples:
- “Approval latency in enterprise deals has increased 21% over the past 10 business days.”
- “Three high-value workflows are active without a clear owner.”
- “Forecast confidence is degrading due to stage/status mismatches and aged open items.”
- “Handoff failures are clustering in the same segment, indicating a process design issue.”

4. ACTION CENTER
A prioritized action queue.
Must show:
- what needs attention
- who owns it
- urgency
- due timing
- estimated value protected or recovered
- dependency chain if applicable
- status of action

5. ROLE-BASED DASHBOARDS
Create separate filtered modes for:
- Executive
- Operations
- Delivery/Sales

Each mode should change:
- headline KPIs
- insight emphasis
- action recommendations
- language framing
- relevant workflows

6. WORKFLOW LATENCY VIEW
A visual system for:
- approval delay
- stage dwell time
- task aging
- queue congestion
- step-by-step slowdown detection

7. OWNERSHIP MAP
Create a module that exposes:
- where ownership is clear
- where it is ambiguous
- where it is missing
- who currently holds responsibility
- where work is stalling because accountability is weak

8. VALUE-AT-RISK PANEL
This is critical.
Estimate and visualize:
- total value at risk
- risk by workflow
- risk by team
- risk by stage
- risk by signal type
- trend over time
This can be model-driven or demo-data-driven initially.

9. DRILL-DOWN DETAIL DRAWER / PAGE
Every major signal or insight should open into a deeper detail view showing:
- source data summary
- event timeline
- detected anomaly or problem
- related entities
- recommended action
- owner
- linked workflow
- value exposure
- notes / rationale

10. DEMO MODE
Build a beautiful demo dataset so the product always feels alive.
Needs to simulate:
- active signals
- changing KPIs
- latency patterns
- owner gaps
- forecast issues
- business impact
Must feel realistic, not random.
