SECTION 2: MASTER BRAND PRINCIPLES
==================================================

The full system must embody these principles:

1. CLARITY
Everything should feel intelligible, structured, and calm.

2. PRECISION
Design, copy, spacing, states, and motion must feel deliberate.

3. RESTRAINT
Avoid visual overstatement, clutter, hype, and gimmicks.

4. AUTHORITY
The system must feel premium, credible, and senior.

5. COHESION
Every lane should feel related without losing its own identity.

6. TRUST
The product and service surfaces must feel enterprise-ready and client-safe.

7. LONGEVITY
The design should still feel strong and relevant a year from now.

==================================================
SECTION 13: CONTENT HIERARCHY RULES
==================================================

Each homepage should answer these in order:
1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What does it do?
4. Why trust it?
5. What should I do next?

Never bury the value proposition.
Never overwhelm the user with too many equal-priority sections.

==================================================
SECTION 14: FINAL QA CHECKLIST
==================================================

Before approving any brand page, verify:
- Is the brand identity clear in 5 seconds?
- Does this feel premium and original?
- Does the copy sound senior and credible?
- Is the CTA language specific?
- Does the page feel cohesive with the ecosystem?
- Does Carlota Jo clearly present as Carlota Jo, not as a personal-name-first founder brand?
- Does the design avoid cliché startup patterns?
- Would this look strong to an enterprise buyer, luxury client, or investor?
- Does the mobile view still feel expensive?
- Is there any filler that should be removed?

If yes, proceed.
If not, refine until it is category-defining.
