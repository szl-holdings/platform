MISSION
Take Beacon, Lyte, Nimbus, and AlloyScape and evolve them into the flagship center of the SZL ecosystem.

These four products must stop feeling like adjacent apps and start functioning like one unified operating system for:
- execution visibility

PRIMARY THESIS
This is not a dashboard suite.
This is a Business Observability and Execution Intelligence OS.

CORE CATEGORY DEFINITION
Business Observability means:
the ability to trace workflows, approvals, risks, recommendations, actions, and outcomes end-to-end so users can understand:
- what is happening
- why it is happening
- what is blocked
- what is likely next
- what action should be taken now
- what value, time, or risk is at stake

NON-NEGOTIABLE OUTCOME
When someone sees the core flow across Beacon, Lyte, Nimbus, and AlloyScape, their reaction should be:
“This is one coherent command system.”

==================================================
SECTION 1 — PRODUCT ROLES
==================================================

DEFINE EACH PRODUCT AS A CLEAR PART OF ONE ENGINE:

BEACON
Role:
Observability Core

Purpose:
Surface business state, workflow health, drift, bottlenecks, value leakage, and operational signals.

BEACON ANSWERS:
- what changed
- where execution is drifting
- where bottlenecks are emerging
- what impact is building

LYTE
Role:
Command and Orchestration Layer

Purpose:
Route work, clarify ownership, manage approvals, escalate issues, and turn signal into accountable action.

LYTE ANSWERS:
- who owns this
- what needs action now
- what is waiting
- what should be escalated

NIMBUS
Role:
Predictive Intelligence Layer

Purpose:
Forecast likely next states, model scenarios, assign confidence, and explain recommendations.

NIMBUS ANSWERS:
- what is likely to happen next
- how confident we are
- what assumptions drive the prediction
- what action has the best expected outcome

ALLOYSCAPE
Role:
Execution Fabric

Purpose:
Run workflows, manage automations, connect systems, track retries, support intervention, and audit what happened.

ALLOYSCAPE ANSWERS:
- what was executed
- what failed
- what was retried
- where intervention is needed
- what the final outcome was
