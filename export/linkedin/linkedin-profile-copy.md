## About Section

I founded SZL Holdings in 2023 to build the operating layer between business intelligence and accountable execution.

The portfolio today: 16 live applications across five industries — maritime, defense, real estate, legal, and advisory — running on one TypeScript architecture. 446 database tables. 1,618+ REST API endpoints. One shared schema. One execution engine. One developer.

What I build:

→ Lyte — Business observability. Surfaces revenue stalls, approval drift, and ownership gaps before they compound.

→ Vessels — Maritime intelligence. Fleet command, AIS analytics, voyage management, and compliance monitoring for global operators.

→ Aegis — Unified defense. SOC command, threat correlation, analyst tradecraft tools, and incident governance in one surface.

→ Terra — Real estate intelligence. Distress signal detection, deal pipeline, and ownership analysis across NYC's five boroughs.

→ PRISM Counsel — Legal matter command. Deadline governance, evidence management, and proof chains for litigation teams.

→ Carlota Jo — Private advisory. Operational command for UHNW household and estate management.

Every platform runs on Alloy — our execution fabric that handles signal normalization, approval routing, and governed automation. Every AI recommendation includes source attribution and confidence scoring. Every action has an audit trail.

The thesis: domain-specific intelligence compounds when the architecture is shared. One schema, one execution engine, one operating philosophy — applied consistently across every domain we enter.

No fake traction. No vaporware. The products are built. The architecture is real.

Writing about business observability, domain intelligence, and what it takes to build a real enterprise software company at: szlholdings.substack.com
